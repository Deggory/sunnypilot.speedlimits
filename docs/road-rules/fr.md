## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agglomération (built-up area) | 50 km/h | Between the entry and exit signs (EB10 / EB20). May be raised to 70 by order where accesses and pedestrian crossings are few and protected. Paris boulevard périphérique: 70 in the code. | R413-3; R110-2 "agglomération" |
| Other roads outside built-up areas | 80 km/h | **90 on sections with at least two lanes in the same direction, on those lanes only, where signed.** | R413-2 I 3° |
| Roads with two carriageways separated by a central reservation | 110 km/h | | R413-2 I 2° |
| Autoroute | 130 km/h | A national road reclassified as autoroute keeps its earlier limit. | R413-2 I 1° |
| Zone 30 | 30 km/h | | R110-2 "zone 30" |
| Zone de rencontre | 20 km/h | | R110-2 "zone de rencontre" |
| Aire piétonne | walking pace | Only vehicles serving the zone. | R110-2 "aire piétonne" |

Conditions (all without signs):

- **Rain or other precipitation** (R413-2 II): 110 on autoroutes normally at 130; 100 on autoroutes with a lower limit and on dual carriageways; 80 on the "other roads" sections that were at 90. Certainty high.
- **Visibility below 50 m:** 50 km/h on all roads and autoroutes (R413-4). Certainty high.
- **Learners and probationary licence holders** (R413-5): 110 on autoroutes normally at 130; 100 on other autoroutes and dual carriageways; 80 on other roads. Exceptions in R413-6.
- **Studded tyres:** 90 km/h (R413-7).
- **Departmental roads at 90:** the president of the departmental council (or the mayor, or the president of the intercommunal body, where they hold the police power) may set a limit **10 km/h above** the code limit on certain sections outside built-up areas that do not have two lanes in each direction, by a reasoned order after the departmental road-safety commission's opinion (CGCT L3221-4-1, in force since 27-12-2019). In practice this gives signed 90s on 80 roads. Certainty high on the text.
- Local authorities may set lower limits; these prevail (R413-1, R411-8).
- Limits are for optimal conditions; drivers must still adapt (R413-17).
- Heavy vehicles, buses, dangerous goods: R413-8 to R413-10. Not relevant for a car without a trailer.

## Road kinds and their signs

| Kind | Begins | Ends | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agglomération | EB10 "Entrée d'agglomération" (white rectangle, red border) | EB20 "Sortie d'agglomération" (white rectangle, black edge) | Arrêté 24-11-1967 art. 5-8 |
| Autoroute | C207 "Début d'une section d'autoroute" | C208 "Fin d'une section d'autoroute" | Arrêté 24-11-1967 art. 5 |
| Route à accès réglementé (route express) | C107 | C108 | Arrêté 24-11-1967 art. 5 |

Facts:

- **The town sign sets the 50:** the agglomération is the area whose entry and exit are signed by the panels placed for that purpose (R110-2), and in an agglomération the limit is 50 (R413-3). The limits of the agglomération are fixed by the mayor (R411-2). Certainty high.
- EB10 may be completed by B14, B30, B52, B54 and others (Arrêté 24-11-1967 art. 5-8): a speed sign under the town sign applies to the town (certainty medium; the IISR text on its scope was not read).
- **The 110 depends on the road layout, not on a sign:** "routes à deux chaussées séparées par un terre-plein central" (R413-2 I 2°). C107 (route à accès réglementé) does not by itself set a limit; a C107 road gets 110 only if it has separate carriageways, otherwise 80 (or a signed value). Certainty high on R413-2; the camera needs to see the central reservation or the map.
- Motorway slip road ("bretelle de raccordement autoroutière"): a road linking autoroutes to the rest of the network (R110-2). Whether 130 applies on it without a sign: not found.

How a camera can tell motorway from expressway: C207 (autoroute) vs C107 (route à accès réglementé). Colours: not verified from the figures.

## How long a speed sign holds

- B14 "notifie l'interdiction de dépasser la vitesse indiquée" (Arrêté 24-11-1967 art. 4).
- **IISR art. 68 (end of prescriptions):** its second paragraph lists places where a prescription ends. The 2008 amendment added a fifth item: "après un poste de péage, à la fin d'une bretelle d'insertion sur autoroutes ou routes à chaussées séparées. Si une prescription s'étend au-delà, elle doit être portée à connaissance par la pose d'un panneau de prescription correspondant." (Arrêté du 11 février 2008, 4th part, 12°). So **a limit signed on an entry ramp ends at the end of the insertion lane** and **any limit ends after a toll** unless repeated. Certainty high for this item.
- **The other four items of art. 68** (which may include intersections or agglomeration boundaries): **not verified**, the consolidated IISR could not be read.
- **IISR art. 63 (speed limits), as amended in 2008:**
  - on autoroutes and dual carriageways, reductions are signed with several B14 in steps of 20 km/h (paragraph b);
  - on an autoroute, the first limit on an exit slip road is 90 km/h, placed at the earliest at the advance direction signs (paragraph f);
  - a B14 on a deceleration lane placed before the divergence point is completed by a directional panel M3a2 (paragraph g): that B14 applies to the exit, not to the main carriageway.
  - "Il est conseillé de répéter ces panneaux à intervalles réguliers en fonction des points singuliers" (art. 63, as reported from the 2008 text). Certainty medium (wording from a page summary).
- Rules that must be signed are enforceable only once the signs are in place; drivers must respect the signs in all circumstances (R411-25).

## Zones

- Zone 30: B30 (entry) / B51 (exit); 30 km/h in the whole zone (R110-2; R411-4; Arrêté art. 4).
- Zone de rencontre: B52 (entry); 20 km/h (R110-2; R411-3-1).
- Aire piétonne: B54 (entry); walking pace (R110-2; R411-3).
- Exit signs of zones de rencontre and aires piétonnes: exist ("entrées et sorties … annoncées par une signalisation", R110-2); their numbers were not checked.
- Zones hold until their exit sign.

## Variable message signs

- Dynamic signs on "panneaux à messages variables installés sur supports fixes ou mobiles" are one of the legal signalling modes (Arrêté 24-11-1967 art. 2). Users must respect signs established under that arrêté (R411-25). So a limit on a variable sign binds. Certainty high.
- **Dark (blank) panel:** not found. The arrêté says only, for temporary panels that can be covered ("panneaux occultables"), that in the neutral state none of the signs may be visible. Treat a dark sign as "no information"; keep the last limit. Certainty low on the law.

## Supplementary panels (panonceaux)

From the Arrêté 24-11-1967 art. 2-1: M1 distance to the start; M2 length of the section; M3a position of the lane concerned (M3a2 directional, IISR art. 63 g); M4 restricts the sign to vehicle categories; M9 additional information (e.g. times, weather); M11 exceptions. Exact list not checked item by item.

## Special cases for a camera system

- **C25b** "Rappel des limites de vitesse sur autoroute" and **C25a** (general limits at the borders): reminder signs showing several numbers (130, 110 …). They are not a new limit; a camera must not read their numbers as B14.
- **C4a / C4b** "Vitesse conseillée" / end: advisory, not a limit.
- **Rain and fog** change the limit without any sign (R413-2 II, R413-4). The comma could use the wiper state; visibility is not measurable reliably.
- **B14 with M3a2 on a deceleration lane** applies to the exit only (IISR art. 63 g).
- **90 on 80 roads** (R413-2 I 3°, CGCT L3221-4-1) and **70 in towns** (R413-3) are lawful signed values above the default.
- **110 vs 80 outside towns** depends on whether the road has a central reservation (R413-2 I 2°); a sign kind does not decide it.
- **Border:** France borders Belgium, Luxembourg, Germany, Switzerland, Italy, Monaco, Spain and Andorra; the country must come from the position.

Other rules the code should know:

1. Rain: 110 / 100 / 80 (R413-2 II); visibility below 50 m: 50 (R413-4).

3. The first limit on a motorway exit slip road is usually 90 (IISR art. 63 f): a 90 seen on the right just before an exit, with an M3a2 arrow, belongs to the exit.
4. C25a / C25b reminder signs must not be read as limits.
