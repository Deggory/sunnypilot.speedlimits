# Poland (PL): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: ustawa z dnia 20 czerwca 1997 r. – Prawo o ruchu drogowym (road traffic act, consolidated text Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1251, with later amendments); rozporządzenie Ministrów Infrastruktury oraz Spraw Wewnętrznych i Administracji z dnia 31 lipca 2002 r. w sprawie znaków i sygnałów drogowych (signs regulation, consolidated text Dz.U. 2019 poz. 2310, amended by Dz.U. 2021 poz. 433, 2021 poz. 2065, 2022 poz. 2372 and 2026 poz. 133); rozporządzenie Ministra Infrastruktury z dnia 3 lipca 2003 r. w sprawie szczegółowych warunków technicznych dla znaków i sygnałów drogowych (technical regulation, consolidated text Dz.U. 2019 poz. 2311, annex 1); ustawa z dnia 5 stycznia 2011 r. o kierujących pojazdami (drivers act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1226, amended by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1676).

All texts were read from the Sejm ELI API (the official publisher of Dziennik Ustaw): `https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/<year>/<pos>/text.html` or `/text.pdf`, checked 2026-10-29. The amendments to the signs regulation listed above were read too; none of them changes the speed paragraphs quoted here.

Certainty labels: **high** = the wording of the law says it; **medium** = a reading of the law that a court or ministry has not confirmed in a source I found; **low** = practice or an inference.

Search note: the shared web-search budget ran out during this work, so I could not look for case law, ministry answers or road-authority practice. Everything below comes from the primary texts, read directly; points that need case law are marked "not found".

## Road kinds and their signs

The road kinds are defined **by the sign**. Art. 2 pkt 3 PoRD: an *autostrada* is a motorway under the public roads act "oznaczoną odpowiednimi znakami drogowymi"; pkt 4: a *droga ekspresowa* is the same with its own signs; pkt 15: "obszar zabudowany - obszar oznaczony odpowiednimi znakami drogowymi"; pkt 16: *strefa zamieszkania* is an area whose "wjazdy i wyjazdy oznaczone są odpowiednimi znakami drogowymi". (https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/1251/text.html, 2026-10-29, high.) A town is therefore not a built-up area unless D-42 marks it.

**Placement details that matter for a camera** (technical regulation, high):
- D-7 and D-9 with plate **T-1a** (distance) stand **1000 m before** the expressway or motorway begins, on the ordinary road that leads into it (5.2.7, 5.2.9). That is an advance notice, not the start.
- Where the entry comes from a built-up area, **D-43 is placed under D-7 or D-9** (5.2.7, 5.2.9). One post then ends the built-up area and starts the expressway or motorway.
- Where two built-up stretches of a locality are less than 300 m apart, D-43/D-42 are left out and a B-33 is used instead (5.2.48).
- Footnote to 6.3.8: E-17a, E-18a and E-19a are not used on motorways and expressways.
- E-17a stands before the first buildings; where it stands with D-42, D-42 goes under it (6.3.8).

**Telling motorway from expressway.** The blue D-9 and D-7 differ only in their pictogram. Since 2026-02-19, § 67a of the signs regulation (added by Dz.U. 2026 poz. 133) says direction signs "barwy niebieskiej – oznaczają kierunki lub miejscowości na autostradzie lub na dojeździe do autostrady" and "barwy zielonej – ... na drodze niebędącej autostradą" (https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/133/text.pdf, 2026-10-29, high). Blue direction signs therefore point to a motorway, green ones to an expressway or an ordinary road. The interchange board E-20 is blue on motorways and green on expressways (tech. 6.3.11, high). Road numbers: "A…" for motorways, "S…" for expressways (general knowledge, medium).

**Single or dual carriageway.** The law sets 100 on a single-carriageway expressway and 120 on a dual one, under the same D-7. No sign or plate distinguishes them. A camera system needs to see whether there is a central reservation (medium; inference from the law).

## How long a speed sign holds

**Rule: a B-33 speed limit ends at the nearest intersection, unless an end sign or another sign ends it first.** This matches what the owner was told (as in Switzerland). Confirmed in the primary text.

- **§ 27 ust. 1 signs regulation** (verbatim): "Znak B-33 „ograniczenie prędkości” oznacza zakaz przekraczania prędkości określonej na znaku liczbą kilometrów na godzinę. Dopuszczalna prędkość określona na znaku obowiązuje, z zastrzeżeniem ust. 4 i § 32, do miejsca: 1) wprowadzenia innej dopuszczalnej prędkości znakiem B-33 lub B-43; 2) umieszczenia znaku D-40 oznaczającego początek strefy zamieszkania; 3) umieszczenia znaków oznaczających odpowiednio początek lub koniec obszaru zabudowanego, o których mowa w § 58 ust. 3 i w § 114 ust. 1 pkt 1 i 2." (https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2019/2310/text.html, 2026-10-29, high.)
- **§ 27 ust. 4**: "Znak B-34 „koniec ograniczenia prędkości” oznacza odwołanie ograniczenia prędkości wprowadzonego znakiem B-33." (Same URL, high.)
- **§ 32 ust. 1**: "Znak B-42 „koniec zakazów” oznacza odwołanie zakazów wyrażonych znakami: B-23, B-25, B-26, B-29 i B-33." (Same URL, high.)
- **§ 32 ust. 2, the intersection rule** (verbatim): "Jeżeli zakaz wyrażony przez znaki B-25, B-26, B-29, B-33, B-35, B-36, B-37 i B-38 nie jest uprzednio odwołany znakiem oznaczającym koniec zakazu, to obowiązuje on do najbliższego skrzyżowania; nie dotyczy to skrzyżowania na drodze dwujezdniowej, na którym wlot drogi poprzecznej znajduje się tylko z lewej strony i nie ma połączenia z prawą jezdnią." (Same URL, high.) In English: unless ended earlier by an end sign, the limit holds to the nearest intersection; this does not apply to an intersection on a dual carriageway where the side road joins only from the left and has no connection to the right-hand carriageway.
- **Technical regulation 3.1.2** says the same for road authorities: the signs hold "począwszy od miejsca ich umieszczenia do najbliższego skrzyżowania ..., chyba że na drodze dwujezdniowej droga odgałęziająca znajduje się tylko z lewej strony i nie ma bezpośredniego połączenia z prawą jezdnią", and "Jeżeli nie ma potrzeby utrzymywania zakazu aż do najbliższego skrzyżowania, wówczas należy zastosować znak uchylający zakaz." (https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2019/2311/text.pdf, 2026-10-29, high.)
- **Technical regulation 3.2.35**: "Znak B-34 umieszcza się na końcu odcinka drogi o ograniczonej prędkości, jeżeli ograniczenie to nie zostało uchylone przez skrzyżowanie." So B-34 is used only where no intersection ends the limit first (high).
- **From where**: § 15 ust. 2: "Zakaz wyrażony znakiem zakazu obowiązuje od miejsca jego ustawienia, chyba że przepisy szczególne stanowią inaczej." § 15 ust. 1: prohibition signs hold "na drodze, na której są umieszczone". (Same URL as the regulation, high.)

**What counts as an intersection.** Art. 2 pkt 10 PoRD (verbatim): "skrzyżowanie - część drogi będącą połączeniem dróg albo jezdni jednej drogi w jednym poziomie, z wyjątkiem połączenia drogi o nawierzchni twardej z drogą o nawierzchni gruntowej lub z drogą wewnętrzną" (https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/1251/text.html, 2026-10-29, high). So:
- **At grade only** ("w jednym poziomie"). A road crossing over or under on a bridge is not an intersection (high).
- **Not an intersection:** the junction of a paved road with an **unpaved** road, or with an **internal road** (*droga wewnętrzna*: car parks, petrol stations, private and estate roads, often signed D-46). These do not end a limit (high).
- **Junction of two carriageways of one road** at grade is an intersection, for example a gap in the central reservation where the two carriageways connect (high, by the wording "jezdni jednej drogi").
- **Roundabouts** are intersections (high, they join roads at grade).
- **Motorway and expressway interchanges** (*węzły*): the crossing of the two roads is on two levels, so it is not an intersection. Whether the point where a ramp (*łącznica*) joins or leaves the main carriageway is a *skrzyżowanie* ("połączenie ... jezdni ... w jednym poziomie") I could not settle: no case law or ministry answer found (search budget exhausted). Reading the wording, I lean to "yes, a ramp merge or diverge ends a B-33" (medium-low). In practice road authorities repeat limits after interchanges (low, not verified).

**Exceptions: when a B-33 does not end at the intersection** (high unless marked):
- **Under D-42.** § 15 ust. 4: "Znak zakazu umieszczony pod znakiem D-42 oznacza, że zakaz obowiązuje do znaku D-43 lub E-18a, z wyjątkiem odcinka drogi, na którym został on zmieniony lub odwołany innym znakiem." A B-33 on the D-42 post holds through the whole built-up area. Technical rule 3.1.2: it is placed "pod wszystkimi znakami D-42 wyznaczającymi dany obszar zabudowany" and needs no end sign.
- **Under E-17a.** § 15 ust. 3: under the locality sign it holds "na obszarze całej miejscowości", except where another sign changes it.
- **Under D-52** (traffic zone on internal roads): until D-53 (§ 15 ust. 4a).
- **Zone signs.** B-43 holds until B-44, not to the intersection (tech. 3.1.2: "Znaki B-39 i B-43 obowiązują aż do ich odwołania odpowiednio znakami B-40 i B-44"). D-40 holds to D-41.
- **Left-only side road on a dual carriageway** (§ 32 ust. 2, second half).
- **T-20 length plate.** § 15 ust. 5 pkt 1: T-20 gives "długość odcinka jezdni, na którym zakaz obowiązuje". The limit then ends after that length. If an intersection comes first, I read § 32 ust. 2 as ending it there anyway (medium).

**Repetition.** A limit that should continue past an intersection must be repeated after it (tech. 3.1.2 together with § 32 ust. 2, medium as an obligation). After D-42 or D-43 too: "Jeżeli za znakiem określającym początek lub koniec obszaru zabudowanego ma obowiązywać prędkość wyrażona umieszczonym wcześniej znakiem B-33, to znak z taką prędkością należy powtórzyć za znakiem D-42 lub D-43" (tech. 3.2.34.4, high).

**What applies after the limit ends.** The statutory limit of the place: 50 in a built-up area, 20 in a residential zone, otherwise 90 / 100 / 120 / 140 by road kind. Technical rule 3.2.34.4: "Jeżeli natomiast stosuje się znak B-34 ..., oznacza to, że za znakiem obowiązuje ograniczenie prędkości określone w ustawie – Prawo o ruchu drogowym." (High.) Inside a B-43 zone, the zone value (medium, see "Zones").

**Road-kind signs do not end a B-33 in § 27.** D-7, D-8, D-9 and D-10 are not in the list of § 27 ust. 1. By the letter, a B-33 read before a D-8 would continue past it to the next intersection (medium). In practice D-7/D-9 stand on entry ramps (after an intersection) and D-8/D-10 on exit ramps, so this rarely matters (low).

## Variable message signs

- **Same legal force.** § 1 ust. 3 pkt 1: vertical signs are "znaki pionowe w postaci tarcz, tablic z napisami lub symbolami, które występują również w postaci znaków świetlnych". § 14, applied to prohibition signs by § 34: light signs with white symbols on a black background "mają takie samo znaczenie, jak znaki w postaci tarcz z czarnymi symbolami". (https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2019/2310/text.html, 2026-10-29, high.) A variable B-33 therefore holds like a fixed B-33, **including the intersection rule** (medium: § 32 ust. 2 does not distinguish).
- **Look** (technical regulation 1.6, high): signs on gantries over lanes or beside the road, as prisms or rotating panels (same colours as fixed signs) or as LED/fibre "discontinuous" signs, whose colours are **inverted** (white symbol on black, red ring kept): "znaki te nadają sygnały będące odwrotnością znaków stałych pod względem barwy tła i symbolu lub napisu".
- **Blank sign.** Tech. 1.6: "W czasie gdy tablica nie nadaje żadnego znaku, nie może być na niej widoczny żaden symbol." This is a technical requirement only. No text gives a dark board any meaning, and it is **not** an end sign (high for the text, medium for "no meaning").
- **Lane signs.** § 2 ust. 1: a sign over an individual lane applies only to that lane ("znak dotyczy tylko kierujących znajdujących się na pasie, nad którym znak jest umieszczony"); a sign over the whole carriageway or on the right applies to all lanes (high).
- **Where they are used.** Not found. I could not verify which motorways or expressways have variable speed signs (for example under the national traffic management system KSZR). Low.

| **T-1a** under D-7 / D-9 | Distance: the expressway or motorway starts in 1000 m (advance notice) | Tech. 5.2.7, 5.2.9, high |
| **T-28** under D-7 / D-9 | Toll road; no speed effect | § 48 ust. 3, high |

- **No wet-road panel.** I found no plate meaning "when wet" in the signs regulation (medium; whole text read, no search for practice). A plate that warns of accidents in rain (T-15) is a warning only (medium).
- **B-33 above 50 in a built-up area** applies only to cars, motorcycles and vehicles up to 3.5 t, with listed exceptions (§ 27 ust. 3). For the comma this changes nothing (high).
- **Advance-notice boards.** § 70 ust. 1: F-5 "uprzedza o znaku zakazu umieszczonym na drodze w odległości wskazanej na znaku"; § 70 ust. 2: F-6 announces a prohibition **after the intersection, on another road**. A B-33 picture inside an F-5 or F-6 board is not yet a limit (high).
- **Painted reproductions.** § 2 ust. 6: horizontal markings that reproduce prohibition signs are a repetition of the vertical sign (high). A painted number repeats the limit; it does not start a new one by itself.
- **Section control.** D-51 (automatic speed control), D-51a and D-51b (start and end of average-speed control) only announce enforcement. They set no limit (signs regulation and its 2019 amendment, high).

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **The town sign is D-42, not the place name.** The white D-42 with building silhouettes and no text starts the 50 km/h built-up area. The green E-17a with the town name only names the place. A camera trained on Swiss or German town signs (name on a yellow or white board) will misread Poland if it takes E-17a as the town entry (high for the law; medium for the E-17a colour).
2. **A built-up area can be anywhere.** It is defined only by D-42 and D-43 (art. 2 pkt 15), and stretches less than 300 m apart are joined (tech. 5.2.48). Villages without D-42 are 90 (high).
3. **A B-33 on the same post as D-42 (or E-17a) holds for the whole area**, through all intersections, until D-43 (or E-18a) (§ 15 ust. 3–4). The code must not drop it at the next junction (high).
4. **D-43 under D-7 or D-9** on one post: built-up area ends and the expressway or motorway begins; the result is 120 or 140 (tech. 5.2.7, 5.2.9, high).
5. **D-7 or D-9 with a T-1a distance plate** is an advance notice 1000 m ahead; the road kind does not change yet (high).
6. **Single-carriageway expressway** (100) and **dual-carriageway ordinary road** with at least two lanes each way (100) have no sign of their own. A camera needs carriageway and lane detection, or the map (high for the law; detection is an inference).
7. **Left-only side roads on a dual carriageway** do not end a limit (§ 32 ust. 2). Exits to petrol stations, car parks and other internal roads, and junctions with dirt roads, do not end it either (art. 2 pkt 10) (high).
8. **Signs on the left** repeat the right-hand sign (§ 2 ust. 1); signs over one lane apply only to that lane (high).
9. **Light signs** are white on black with a red ring; they mean the same as the fixed signs (high).
10. **Graded limits.** Where a limit drops by more than 30 km/h, intermediate B-33 signs stand 70–100 m apart (tech. 3.2.34.4, table 3.2). Table 3.2 gives, for example, 130 → 100 → 70 → 40 and 90 → 70 → 50; it still uses the old 130 motorway value. Successive readings are normal, not misreads (high).
11. **Plausibility.** Outside built-up areas signs may only lower the limit (art. 21 ust. 3), and the highest general limit is 140. Inside built-up areas the technical rule allows raising to 70, or 80 (tech. 3.2.34.3). Values are multiples of 10, or of 5 below 30 near speed humps (tech. 3.2.34.1). A reading above 140 is a misread (high for the law; road authorities may still deviate, low).
12. **Lowest signed values.** 50 outside built-up areas and 30 inside are the usual minimums, with exceptions for ramp curves, damaged roads and roadworks (tech. 3.2.34.1, high).

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:**
  ```python
  "PL": {"town": 50, "rural": 90, "expressway": 120, "motorway": 140},
  ```
  - `"expressway"` = *droga ekspresowa dwujezdniowa* (D-7, dual carriageway): 120. `"motorway"` = *autostrada* (D-9): 140. `"town"` = *obszar zabudowany* (D-42): 50. `"rural"` = other roads outside built-up areas: 90.
  - **New keys are needed for two road kinds Poland has and the row cannot hold:**
    - `"expressway_single": 100`, an expressway with one carriageway. Same D-7 sign, so the road-type network (or the map) has to tell single from dual. Without it, 120 is too high on a single-carriageway expressway; the safe default would be 100 on every expressway, which under-reads dual ones by 20.
    - `"rural_dual": 100`, a dual carriageway with at least two lanes each way, outside built-up areas, that is not an expressway or motorway. There is no sign for it. Without detection, use 90 (under-reads by 10, safe).
  - **Residential zone**: D-40 → 20, until D-41. This is a zone value, not a road kind.
  - **ROAD_SIGNS mapping for Poland:** `motorway_start` = D-9, `motorway_end` = D-10, `expressway_start` = D-7, `expressway_end` = D-8, `town_entry` = **D-42**, `town_exit` = **D-43**. **E-17a and E-18a must not map to town entry or exit.** D-7/D-9 with a T-1a plate must not switch the road kind. After D-8 or D-10 the code's "rural" (90) is right unless a D-42 follows.
- **Intersection rule: YES.** § 32 ust. 2 signs regulation: a B-33 "obowiązuje ... do najbliższego skrzyżowania" unless ended earlier. Exceptions:
  - a B-33 placed under D-42 (whole built-up area, until D-43 or E-18a), under E-17a (whole locality) or under D-52 (until D-53) (§ 15 ust. 3–4a);
  - B-43 zones (until B-44) and the D-40 residential zone (until D-41);
  - a dual carriageway where the side road joins only from the left with no link to the right-hand carriageway;
  - junctions with dirt roads and internal roads (car parks, petrol stations), which are not intersections (art. 2 pkt 10);
  - grade-separated crossings are not intersections ("w jednym poziomie"). Ramp merges and diverges on motorways and expressways: open, medium-low that they end the limit.
  After the intersection the statutory limit of the place applies (50 / 90 / 100 / 120 / 140, or the zone value). This matches the code's MAP_SECONDS logic (written for Swiss OSStr art. 16), with one addition: a number read on the same post as D-42 must be kept as the town's limit until D-43 and not dropped at junctions.
- **Blank variable sign:** no legal meaning (tech. 1.6 only says nothing may show). Treat it as no sign: not an end sign. The last limit holds until the next intersection, end sign or new sign (medium).
- **Other rules for the code:**
  - **What ends a B-33:** a new B-33 or B-43, B-34, B-42, D-40, D-42, D-43, or the next intersection (§ 27 ust. 1 and 4, § 32). B-34 and B-42 restore the statutory limit of the road kind. D-7..D-10 do not end a B-33 by the letter of § 27 (medium); the code's current behaviour (a road sign replaces the held limit) is a harmless simplification because these signs stand on ramps.
  - **No night limit** since 2021-06-01 (the 23:00–05:00 60 km/h was repealed). **No novice limit.**
  - **Panels:** ignore a B-33 with a T-23 plate; delay a B-33 with a T-21 plate by the distance shown; end a B-33 with a T-20 plate after the length shown; apply a time plate only in its hours. F-5 and F-6 boards are advance notices, not limits.
  - **Plausibility:** maximum 140; outside built-up areas a signed value above the general limit of the road kind is very likely a misread.
  - A motorway or expressway inside a built-up area would be 50 by art. 20 ust. 1 unless signed. Road authorities normally end the built-up area at the entry (D-43 under D-7/D-9), so this should be rare (medium).
