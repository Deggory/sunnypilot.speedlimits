Conditions:

- **Wet road, fog, night, visibility:** no number in the act. There is only the general duty of § 16(1): adapt speed to the weather and to the state of the road, and be able to stop within the distance you can see. Certainty medium-high: the whole of § 16 was read, and the act was searched for other speed rules.
- **Novice drivers:** no separate limit found in zákon 8/2009. Keyword searches for "začínajúc" and "novic" find nothing related. Certainty medium.
- **Signs win:** a speed limit set by a sign or by a traffic device must be obeyed (§ 16(5)). The class limits of § 16(2) and (3) cannot be exceeded even when a sign allows more, except by a vehicle up to 3,500 kg without a trailer (§ 16(6)). So a car may lawfully follow a signed value above 130. The sk.wikipedia file list of sign 253 goes up to 140 [WIKI-LIST]. Whether any road actually carries a 140: not found.
- **History (for older data and maps):**
  - Until 31.3.2020 the act read "na diaľnici a na rýchlostnej ceste" 130, with 90 on those roads inside an obec [Z8-20191201].
  - From 1.4.2020 to 5.7.2022: 90 on a diaľnica inside an obec [Z8-20220701].
  - Since 6.7.2022: no special in-town motorway limit [Z8-20220706].

Facts from the law:

- **Motorway–expressway transitions without an end sign.** Annex 4, common note 6: "Ak diaľnica prechádza do cesty pre motorové vozidlá, značka Cesta pre motorové vozidlá zároveň označuje koniec diaľnice; ak cesta pre motorové vozidlá prechádza do diaľnice, značka Diaľnica zároveň označuje koniec cesty pre motorové vozidlá." (If a motorway turns into a road for motor vehicles, sign 311 also marks the end of the motorway, and the other way round.) [A4]. Certainty high.
- **Rýchlostná cesta is not a kind in the traffic rules any more.** Since 1.4.2020 the traffic act uses only "diaľnica" and "cesta pre motorové vozidlá". Law 393/2019 Čl. II bod 11 rewrote § 4 of the road act 135/1961, so that diaľnice are divided by construction into diaľnice and rýchlostné cesty [Z393]. Certainty high for the text. The traffic rules on a road therefore follow its sign: 309 means motorway rules (130), 311 means expressway rules (90 outside an obec). sk.wikipedia says that full-profile R sections carry 309 and half-profile R sections carry 311 [WIKI-CMV], [WIKI-D]. Secondary source, certainty medium.
- **Route numbers:** sign 352 shows "Číslo diaľnice alebo rýchlostnej cesty" (D… or R…) [A4] part IV item 3. The number says which network the road belongs to, not which rules apply: a half-profile R road can be a 311 road.
- **How a camera tells motorway from expressway:** 309 is green with the motorway symbol, 311 is blue with a car. Both are square. Swiss 4.01 and 4.03 are both green, so the SK 311 is a new colour variant for the class "expressway_start". Directional signs in the motorway layout that are used off a motorway have a blue base (annex 4, orientation signs item 25: "Značku možno použiť aj mimo diaľnice; základná plocha značky je v takom prípade modrej farby") [A4]. So green guide signs point to a motorway, blue ones to other roads. Interpretation, certainty medium.
- **Old signs:** signs placed before 31.3.2020 under the old decree 9/2009 stay valid "s významom podľa im zodpovedajúcich dopravných značiek" (with the meaning of the matching new signs) [V30] § 30(1). Their old drawings and codes may differ from the 2020 set; the OSM page lists the old codes [OSM]. Old temporary signs could be used only until 31.3.2022 (§ 30(6)). The colour of pre-2020 motorway signs: not verified.
- **Sign 342 "Informácie o všeobecných pravidlách"** stands at the border crossings. It informs about the general speed limits and daytime lights; it is not a limit to apply [A4] part III item 14.
- **Where signs stand:**
  - Right of the road, roughly perpendicular to it (§ 4(1)).
  - They may be repeated on the left or over the road, or stand only on the left or only over the road (§ 4(2)).
  - End signs of an obec, a zone or a special lane normally stand on the back of the start sign (§ 4(4)).
  - Warning signs stand 150–250 m before the hazard outside an obec, and directly before it inside one (§ 6(2), (3)).
  - Source: [V30].

## How long a speed sign holds

**Permanent rule.** A regulatory sign holds from where it stands (§ 7(4)) "po značku, ktorá ju ukončuje" (until the sign that ends it). [V30] § 7(5) continues: "Ak má regulácia … platiť aj za križovatkou, musí byť regulačná značka znovu umiestnená vo vhodnej vzdialenosti za križovatkou, inak musí byť vo vhodnej vzdialenosti za križovatkou umiestnená značka, ktorá ju ukončuje." (If it should hold past an intersection, the sign must be repeated after it; otherwise an end sign must stand after it.) This second sentence is a duty of the road authority. For the driver, the sign holds until an end sign. Certainty high for the text, medium for the reading.

**Transitional rule, in force until 31.3.2029.** [V30] § 30(2)(a): "najvyššia dovolená rýchlosť vyplývajúca zo značky umiestnenej pri okraji cesty platí najďalej po vzdialenejšiu hranicu križovatky, ak nie je ukončená alebo zopakovaná za križovatkou podľa § 7 ods. 5, alebo po začiatok obce". In English: a speed limit from a sign **at the roadside** holds at most to the far edge of the intersection, unless it is ended or repeated after it, or to the start of an obec. [V30] § 30a(a), in force since 30.3.2022, adds "alebo po koniec obce" (or to the end of an obec).

The deadline was extended to 31.3.2024 by 87/2022 [V87] and then to 31.3.2029 by 58/2024 [V58]. A press article saying that the transitional provisions ended in 2024 [TOUCHIT-2024] is contradicted by 58/2024. Certainty high.

So, today:

- **Roadside 253 signs:** end at the far edge of the next intersection unless repeated, and also at signs 305 and 306.
- **Signs over a lane (gantries):** not covered by § 30(2)(a). They hold for their lane (§ 7(2)) until an end sign (§ 7(5)). Certainty medium-high.
- **Speed signs drawn on a blue or yellow lane sign (pruhová značka):** hold to the far edge of the intersection, or to a lane sign of that colour without the speed sign (§ 30(2)(c)). Certainty high.

**What counts as an intersection.** [Z8] § 2(2)(k), since 1.9.2026: "miesto, kde sa cesty úrovňovo pretínajú alebo stýkajú" (a place where roads cross or meet at grade).

- It excludes accesses by an účelová cesta to:
  - a property, a closed area, a garage or a car park;
  - a bus turning area, a filling station or a similar service place.
- It also excludes the outlets of field roads, forest roads, cycle paths, obytné zóny and pešie zóny.
- Before 1.9.2026 the definition read "miesto, v ktorom sa cesty pretínajú alebo spájajú" (no "at grade"), amended by [Z131].
- So driveways, car parks and filling-station exits do not end a sign (certainty high).
- Whether a grade-separated interchange, or a ramp merging onto a motorway, is an intersection under the new "úrovňovo" wording: not found (see "Open points"). Certainty low-medium.

**Other limits on validity:**

- A regulatory sign placed together with a warning sign holds at most 100 m past the point where the hazard ends ([V30] § 7(6)).
- A regulatory sign may be repeated along a longer section ([V30] § 7(7)).
- Panel 502 gives the length the sign holds ([A5] item 3(b)).
- A new 253 ends the previous 253 ([A3] item 32: "Značka zároveň ukončuje platnosť predchádzajúcej značky Najvyššia dovolená rýchlosť").
- A sign carried on a vehicle (mobile roadworks) overrides the fixed signs. It holds until 100 m behind that vehicle, or behind the last vehicle of the group, even while moving ([V30] § 3(2)).
- A sign is temporarily invalid when fully covered or removed. Taping over a sign is not allowed, and the orange cross is only for parts of orientation signs ([V30] § 3(3)).

**Ramps and slip roads:** no explicit rule found. Traffic entering from a ramp has not passed the main carriageway's roadside sign. Under § 7(5) the authority must repeat the limit after the junction if it should hold there. Whether NDS (the motorway operator) repeats limits after each on-ramp: not verified.

**Old rule (decree 9/2009, until 31.3.2020)** [V9] § 5(13): "Zákaz, obmedzenie alebo príkaz vyplývajúci z príslušnej zvislej dopravnej značky sa končí na vzdialenejšej hranici najbližšej križovatky, ak nie je skôr skončený inak". In English: prohibitions ended at the far edge of the next intersection. Exceptions included the signs for an obec, the zones, the diaľnica and the rýchlostná cesta, which ended at their own end signs. Today's § 30(2)(a) continues this for roadside speed signs.

**Case law:** not found. Web searches of nsud.sk found only decisions that quote the pre-2022 "diaľnica v obci 90" wording, and none on how long a sign holds.

## Zones

- **268 / 269, speed-limit zone:**
  - A zone sign is a rectangle with "ZÓNA" at the top.
  - It holds on all roads of the zone, in both directions, until the end sign.
  - Zone signs must stand on every road into and out of the zone.
  - § 7(5)–(7), and with them the intersection repeat rule, do not apply to zone signs ([V30] § 7(8)). So **intersections do not end a zone**. Certainty high.
  - Values in the sk.wikipedia file list: 20 and 30 [WIKI-LIST]. Other values: not found.
- **Obytná zóna (315/316) and školská zóna (319/320):** 20 km/h. **Pešia zóna (317/318):** 10 km/h since 1.9.2026 ([Z8] § 59(3); [Z131] bod 75). These signs do not show a number: the limit comes from the act.
- **Bicyklová cesta:** 30 km/h ([Z8] § 59a(2)). Motor traffic only where a sign allows it.
- **Nízkoemisná zóna** (low-emission zone): not a speed zone.

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status:**
  - Devices that show variable traffic signs are traffic devices ([V30] § 13(7)).
  - "Premenná dopravná značka je zvislá značka zobrazená na zariadení na vyobrazenie premenných dopravných značiek" ([V30] § 13(8)): a variable sign *is* a vertical sign, binding like a fixed one.
  - § 16(5) of the act names a limit set "dopravnou značkou alebo dopravným zariadením" (by a sign or a traffic device) [Z8].
  - Certainty high.
- **Look:** on LED (light-point) panels the sign is shown "v zjednodušenom grafickom vyhotovení a v inverznom farebnom vyhotovení čiernej a bielej farby" (simplified drawing, black and white inverted). So the figures are light on a dark field, unlike fixed signs. Whether the red ring stays red is not stated ([V30] § 13(8)).
- **Gantries and lane-specific limits:**
  - A sign over a lane holds for that lane only ([V30] § 7(2)).
  - The transitional intersection rule § 30(2)(a) covers only roadside signs, so an overhead limit holds until an end sign or a new value (§ 7(5)).
  - **Line control on the D1 around Bratislava**, in operation since 26.8.2021 (press reports quoting NDS):
    - signs over each lane;
    - "Pri ideálnych podmienkach budú dopravné značky zhasnuté a vy môžete jazdiť rýchlosťou 130 km/h" (in ideal conditions the signs are dark and you may drive 130);
    - lamella (louvred) backup signs for failures.
    - Sources: [TOUCHIT-NDS], [NOVINY]. Secondary, certainty medium.
- **Blank or dark panels:**
  - No rule in the act or the decree: not found.
  - A dark panel is not one of the end signs (263, 267, a new 253; see "End signs").
  - In practice NDS uses "dark" for "the general limit applies" (130 on the motorway).
  - Legally, a limit shown on an earlier gantry continues until an end sign or a new value (§ 7(5)). Whether a dark gantry after a lit one is meant to lift the limit: not found.
  - Certainty low-medium; see "Open points".

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **A 253 with a 501 or 503 under it is a pre-sign.** The limit begins later, or on another road. Do not apply it at that point.
2. **A 253 with a 506 or 508** (lorry symbol, weight such as 3.5 t or 7.5 t) is for heavy vehicles. A car ignores it.
3. **Roadside limit and intersection (until 31.3.2029):** after a real at-grade intersection with no repeated sign, the general limit of the road applies again. Driveways, car parks, filling stations, and field or forest roads are not intersections (§ 2(2)(k)).
4. **Town signs end a roadside limit.** A 70 before a town ends at 305, so the town's 50 applies. A 70 inside a town ends at 306, so the rural 90 applies ([V30] § 30(2)(a), § 30a(a)).
5. **311 directly after a motorway** (no 310) means motorway rules end and expressway rules begin: 130 becomes 90. **309 after a 311 road** means 90 becomes 130 ([A4] common note 6).
6. **311 is blue, 309 is green** (Wikimedia Commons SVGs [C309], [C311]). A classifier trained on Swiss signs knows only the green Autostrasse sign.
7. **Motorway through a town:** motorways carry no 305 "Obec" sign. The 130 holds unless signed lower. Around Bratislava the operator signs lower limits or uses line control; the exact sections were not verified.
8. **LED variable signs are inverted** (light on dark) and simplified ([V30] § 13(8)). They differ in look from the fixed signs of the same value.
9. **A dark LED panel is not an end sign.** See "Variable message signs".
10. **Overhead lane signs** hold for their lane only (§ 7(2)). With different values per lane, the car's own lane counts.
11. **Speed signs on vehicles** (mobile roadworks, sometimes with arrow boards) override fixed signs. They end 100 m behind the vehicle, or behind the last vehicle of the group ([V30] § 3(2)).
12. **Covered signs** (fully covered or removed) are not valid ([V30] § 3(3)). A partly visible covered speed sign should not be taken.
13. **Warning sign plus speed sign** (e.g. a bend with a 60): the limit holds at most 100 m past the end of the hazard ([V30] § 7(6)).
14. **Railway level crossing:** 30 km/h within 50 m before it and over it, or 50 km/h while the white light flashes, all without any speed sign ([Z8] § 27(3)).
15. **Bilingual place-name plates** (307/308, e.g. Hungarian names in the south) stand under 305/306 or beside them on one board. They are the same town entry or exit, not a second sign.
16. **Sign 342 at the borders** lists the general limits. It is not a limit to apply.
17. **Signs before 31.3.2020** stay valid with the meaning of the new signs (§ 30(1)). Old drawings, including old E-series panels, can still stand by the road.
18. **Car with a trailer over 750 kg:** 90 also on the motorway (§ 16(3)). The comma does not know about trailers.

- In law it means nothing: it is not an end sign, and no rule on dark panels was found.
- NDS states that dark signs on the Bratislava D1 line control mean 130 under ideal conditions.
- Suggestion: keep the last read value past a dark gantry until a number, 263 or 267, a road-kind sign, or the map's change ends it. Do not treat a dark panel as "no limit".
- Certainty low-medium.

**Other rules the code should know:**

- 501 or 503 under a 253: pre-sign, do not apply it. 502: holds for the shown length. 505: time-limited. 506 or 508 with a lorry or a weight: ignore it.
- 263 and 267 both end a 253. 267 ends the speed limit too, not only overtaking bans.
- A speed sign paired with a warning sign ends 100 m past the hazard (§ 7(6)).
- A sign on a vehicle ends 100 m behind it (§ 3(2)).
- A railway crossing means an implicit 30, or 50 with the white light (§ 27(3)). The camera sees only the crossing's warning signs, so this is not in the code today.
- § 16(6) does not cap a car: a signed 140 would be lawful.
- There are no wet, night or novice-driver limits to model.

## Open points

1. **Ramps and interchanges under the new intersection definition** (since 1.9.2026, "úrovňovo"). Does a roadside 253 on a motorway or a 311 road end at a grade-separated interchange, or at the point where a ramp merges? Not found. The explanatory report (dôvodová správa) to 131/2026 was not read. Certainty low-medium.
2. **Motorway inside an obec** after 6.7.2022. § 16(4) still says "v obci 50", and § 16(2) says "na diaľnici 130". The reading here is 130, because motorways carry no 305 sign and the deletion removed the special in-town rule. The dôvodová správa to 455/2021 was not read. Certainty medium.
3. **Blank or dark variable signs:** no legal rule found. The NDS practice ("dark means 130") comes only from the press. Certainty low-medium.
4. **Look of signs not verified:**
   - not verified: 305 and 306 (colour), 310, 312, 263, 267, 268 and 269, and the old pre-2020 motorway sign, because the annex drawings could not be rendered and the web search budget ran out;
   - verified from Commons SVGs (secondary source): 309 is green and 311 is blue.
5. **311 inside an obec is 50 by law;** the code gives 90. This is rare.
6. **No wet, night or novice-driver limits** were found. Certainty medium-high.
7. **Old panel E 9** (bad weather) under signs from before 2020: which new panel it maps to under § 30(1) is not found.
8. **Whether § 30(2)(a) will be extended past 31.3.2029:** unknown.
9. **Whether NDS repeats speed limits after each on-ramp:** not verified.
10. **Case law** on how long a sign holds: not found.
11. **Signed values above 130** (e.g. 140): a sign file exists, but a real road carrying one was not found.
