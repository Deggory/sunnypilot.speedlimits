# Czechia (CZ): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: zákon č. 361/2000 Sb., o provozu na pozemních komunikacích (road traffic act, consolidated version 63, in force 01.01.2026–31.12.2026); vyhláška č. 294/2015 Sb., kterou se provádějí pravidla provozu na pozemních komunikacích (signs decree, consolidated version 5, in force since 01.07.2025); zákon č. 268/2015 Sb. (2016 reform of the motorway network).

Official consolidated texts: e-Sbírka, https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2000/361 and https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2015/294. These are JavaScript pages that could not be read here, so every quotation below comes from the consolidated texts on zakonyprolidi.cz (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361 and https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2015-294), checked 2026-10-29.

Certainty labels: **high** = the wording of the law says it; **medium** = a reading of the law that a court or ministry has not confirmed in a source I found; **low** = practice or an inference.

## Road kinds and their signs

The road kinds are defined **by the sign alone**. § 2 písm. r) zák. 361/2000: "dálnice je pozemní komunikace označená dopravní značkou „Dálnice“". § 2 písm. s): "silnice pro motorová vozidla je pozemní komunikace označená dopravní značkou „Silnice pro motorová vozidla“". § 2 písm. cc): *obec* "je zastavěné území, jehož začátek a konec je na pozemní komunikaci označen příslušnými dopravními značkami". (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361, 2026-10-29, high.)

**Telling motorway from SMV with a camera.** Only the IZ sign does it: green with the motorway pictogram means motorway (130), blue with the car means SMV (110). Direction signs do **not** tell them apart: destinations reached by a motorway *or* an SMV both use a **green** background (https://autotrip.cz/dp/smerova-navest-pro-odboceni/ via search, 2026-10-29, medium). Motorway numbers are in a red field ("Dálnice mají číslo v červeném poli", ČT24 URL above, medium).

**Where the signs stand.** Not found in the decree. I could not read the technical rules TP 65 (*Zásady pro dopravní značení na pozemních komunikacích*). In practice IZ 1a / IZ 2a stand on the entry ramp, after the junction with the ordinary road (low, not verified). According to a quote of TP 65 in a mailing list, IZ 1b / IZ 2b stand "150–250 m před stavebním ukončením dálnice", that is 150–250 m before the physical end of the road (https://lists.openstreetmap.org/pipermail/talk-cz/2022-November/024931.html, 2026-10-29, low: a secondary source).

## How long a speed sign holds

**Rule: a speed limit (B 20a) ends at the nearest intersection, unless an end sign comes first.**

- **§ 3 odst. 3 vyhl. 294/2015:** "Zákaz, omezení nebo příkaz je ukončen nejbližší křižovatkou nebo příslušnou dopravní značkou." (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2015-294, 2026-10-29, high.)
- **Signs that end B 20a.** The annex text of B 20a reads: "Platnost značky je ukončena také značkami „Dálnice“, „Konec dálnice“, „Silnice pro motorová vozidla“, „Konec silnice pro motorová vozidla“, „Obec“ a „Konec obce“." A new B 20a with another value also ends it, and so do B 20b and B 26. (Same URL, high.)
- **Repetition.** § 6 odst. 1: "Značku lze pro zdůraznění opakovat i v průběhu platnosti zákazu nebo omezení." A limit that continues past an intersection must therefore be signed again after it. (Same URL, high for the text; the obligation to repeat is medium.)
- **What counts as an intersection.** § 2 písm. w) zák. 361/2000: "křižovatka je místo, v němž se pozemní komunikace protínají nebo spojují; za křižovatku se nepovažuje vyústění polní nebo lesní cesty nebo jiné účelové pozemní komunikace na jinou pozemní komunikaci." So exits from field or forest roads and from *účelové komunikace* (private or access roads, car parks, petrol stations, driveways) do **not** end the limit. (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361, 2026-10-29, high.)
- **Near or far edge of the intersection.** The old decree 30/2001, § 6(3), said "končí na vzdálenější hranici nejbližší křižovatky", that is at the far edge of the intersection (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2001-30, 2026-10-29, high). The 2016 text says only "je ukončen nejbližší křižovatkou". Defendants in speeding cases have argued that a sign placed before an intersection no longer holds inside it; I found the arguments in Supreme Administrative Court (NSS) search hits, but could not read the courts' holdings (https://vyhledavac.nssoud.cz/DokumentOriginal/Index/443900, …/416773; 2026-10-29). Not found, low. For a camera this is a few metres and does not matter.
- **Zones are an exception.** § 3 odst. 6: "Platnost zónové dopravní značky je ukončena značkou informující o jejím konci, není-li stanoveno jinak." Zone 30, the residential zone and the pedestrian zone hold through intersections until their end sign. (Same URL, high.)
- **Mobile work site.** A sign carried on a slow work vehicle ends "100 m ve směru jízdy za tímto vozidlem" (§ 3 odst. 4), that is 100 m past the vehicle or the last vehicle of the group (high).

**Motorways and SMV: interchanges, ramps, merges.**
- **Interchanges.** A grade-separated interchange (*mimoúrovňová křižovatka*) is a place where roads "se ... spojují", so it is a *křižovatka* under § 2 w). A B 20a on the main carriageway therefore ends at the next interchange (exit or entry) and must be repeated after it. **Medium**: this follows from § 2 w) and § 3(3), but no court ruling or ministry statement was found.
- **Rest areas** (*odpočívky*) are part of the motorway. Whether their access counts as a *křižovatka*: not found, low.
- **Ramp limit at the merge.** The entry ramp is part of the interchange, so a B 20a on the ramp ends where the ramp joins the main carriageway, at the intersection. Medium, same reasoning.
- **Main-carriageway limit for traffic entering from a ramp.** A B 20a holds "od místa" where it stands (§ 6(1)) and ends at the interchange anyway. A driver who joins after a main-carriageway sign is therefore not bound by it, and the general limit (130 or 110) applies until the next sign after the merge. Medium.
- **Road-kind signs.** IZ 1a, 1b, 2a and 2b themselves end any B 20a (annex B 20a, high).

## Zones

- **Zone with a traffic restriction, IZ 8a / IZ 8b.** This is how a "zone 30" is made: IZ 8a carrying a B 20a 30 symbol. Annex 5: "Značka označuje oblast, zejména část obce, kde platí výstraha, zákaz, omezení nebo příkaz vyplývající z příslušných vyobrazení značky nebo značek, pokud místní úpravou provozu na pozemních komunikacích uvnitř oblasti není stanoveno jinak." Restrictions limited to certain vehicles or times are written in the lower part of the sign. If the sign shows a prohibition sign, the zone sign "má charakter ... zákazové dopravní značky". The zone holds until IZ 8b, through intersections (§ 3(6)). A local B 20a inside the zone overrides it until the next intersection or B 20b, and then the zone value returns (B 20b note). (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2015-294, 2026-10-29, high.)
- **Residential zone, IZ 5a / IZ 5b: 20 km/h.** The end sign "může být ... umístěna i jen po levé straně vozovky", that is on the left side only, on the back of the entry sign. A camera may see the zone end only on the left (high).
- **Pedestrian zone, IZ 6a / IZ 6b: 20 km/h** (§ 39(5)). The bottom of the sign says which vehicles may enter and when (high).
- **Built-up area, IZ 4a / IZ 4b: 50.** This is not a "zone" in the decree, but it acts like one: 50 holds from IZ 4a to IZ 4b through intersections, because it is the general limit of § 18(4), not a sign restriction (high).

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status.** "Svislé dopravní značky jsou stálé, proměnné a přenosné. Proměnná svislá dopravní značka je dopravní značka, jejíž činná plocha se může měnit." (§ 62 odst. 2 zák. 361/2000). A B 20a shown on a variable sign is therefore a full B 20a (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2000-361, 2026-10-29, high). They are used "zejména v systému řízení provozu" (§ 78 odst. 4, high). The ranking of variable signs against fixed signs is not stated: § 76 ranks only fixed, temporary and portable signs. Not found.
- **Look.** § 1 odst. 3 vyhl. 294/2015: the colours of a variable sign may differ from the fixed sign, "podklad proměnných dopravních značek je tmavý a nápisy, symboly a ohraničení světlé". A variable B 20a can therefore be a **light number and ring on a dark panel**, not necessarily red on white (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2015-294, 2026-10-29, high).
- **Gantries and lanes.** § 3 odst. 2: a sign above the carriageway holds for the whole carriageway in that direction. "Je-li však nad vyznačeným jízdním pruhem umístěna zákazová nebo příkazová značka, platí jen pro jízdní pruh, nad nímž je umístěna." So a B 20a over one lane holds for that lane only, and different lanes can have different limits (high).
- **Who sets them.** On D0 and D1 (lane control, *liniové řízení provozu*), the Police decides the limit shown on the variable signs. One example: 80 km/h (B 20a) for 68 minutes because of a broken-down vehicle (https://archiv.policie.gov.cz/clanek/nejvyssi-povolena-rychlost-na-promennem-dopravnim-znaceni-na-dalnici-ii-tridy-d0.aspx, 2026-10-29, high for that case).
- **D3 trial at 150 km/h.** Since 5 Oct 2025, 42 variable signs on the D3 between exits 84 (Planá nad Lužnicí) and 131 (Úsilné), about 47 km, show 150, 130 or 100 depending on weather, visibility and traffic. The legal basis is § 18(7), +20 on a motorway. The trial was extended to the end of 2026 (https://fdrive.cz/clanky/v-cesku-konecne-150-km-h-legalne-zatim-jen-na-d3-15375 and https://fdrive.cz/clanky/stopadesatkou-na-d3-legalne-i-dale-testovani-se-protahne-do-prosince-16679, 2026-10-29, high for the facts; news sources). What happens after 2026: not found.
- **Blank or dark panels.** No rule found that gives a dark variable sign any meaning. A dark panel has no *činná plocha* showing a sign, so it is **not** a B 20b. A limit shown on an earlier panel still ends at the nearest intersection or end sign (§ 3(3)), and otherwise the general limit applies. Medium: an inference, not found stated.
- **Information panels.** Information panels (ZPI, *zařízení pro provozní informace*) may also serve as variable signs ("Zařízení nebo jeho část může sloužit také jako proměnná dopravní značka", annex ZPI 1; https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2015-294, 2026-10-29, high).

## Supplementary panels

From annex 6 of vyhl. 294/2015 (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2015-294, 2026-10-29, high):

| Panel | Meaning for a speed sign |
|---|---|
| **E 3a Vzdálenost** | "vzdálenost k místu, od kterého platí značka". The limit starts *later*: do not apply it at the sign. |
| **E 4 Délka úseku** | Length of the section where the sign holds. Whether this overrides the intersection rule: not found (low). |
| **E 5 Největší povolená hmotnost** | The limit holds only for vehicles whose maximum permitted mass exceeds the value shown (e.g. 3.5 t). Not for a passenger car. |
| **E 6 Za mokra (za deště)** | "omezuje platnost značky ... na podmínky mokré vozovky": wet road only |
| **E 7a/E 7b Směrová šipka** | Direction (straight / turning) to the place where the sign holds, e.g. after turning off |
| **E 8a–E 8e Úsek** | Start, continuation and end of the section where the sign holds |
| **E 9 Druh vozidla** | Only for the vehicle kind shown (e.g. lorry, bus symbol) |
| **E 13 Text nebo symbol** | Narrows the sign by text, e.g. times of day or days |
| **E 11a–f** | Toll or vignette. Not about speed. |

Two B 20a signs on one post with different values, the lower one restricted by a panel such as E 5 "3,5 t", are explicitly allowed. The unrestricted value is the car's (https://autotrip.cz/dp/nejvyssi-dovolena-rychlost/, 2026-10-29, high).

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **Motorway or SMV inside an *obec*: 80, not 130 or 110** (§ 18(4)). Example: Prague, 5. května, which is signed IZ 2a: 80 km/h (cnn.iprima.cz URL above, medium). A camera that sets "expressway → 110" on an IZ 2a seen inside a town is wrong. It must remember that it is still in the *obec*, because no IZ 4b was passed.
2. **Border sign IP 28b „Nejvyšší dovolené rychlosti“.** A blue panel headed "CZ ČESKÁ REPUBLIKA" with four B 20a discs (50, 90, 110, 130) beside pictograms. It is purely informative and stands at border crossings. **Never read its numbers as a limit.** (https://autotrip.cz/dp/nejvyssi-dovolene-rychlosti/, 2026-10-29, high.)
3. **Crossed-out signs.** A sign made invalid by temporary traffic arrangements is struck through with orange-black stripes or a red cross (§ 3 odst. 8 vyhl. 294/2015), or covered. Ignore a struck-through B 20a (high).
4. **Portable signs** (*přenosné*, on red-and-white striped posts or on vehicles) "jsou nadřazeny všem dopravním značkám" (§ 76 odst. 4 zák. 361/2000). At roadworks a portable 80 beats a fixed 130 at the same place (high). The fluorescent yellow-green backing (§ 1(4) vyhl. 294/2015) is allowed on any sign and does not change its meaning (high).
5. **Inverted variable signs:** a light number on a dark panel, sometimes without a red ring (§ 1(3)), high.
6. **Lane-specific gantry signs** (§ 3(2)): pick the limit of the ego lane, high.
7. **Zone return after B 20b:** inside a zone 30, B 20b brings back 30, not 50 (B 20b note), high.
8. **Plausibility bounds by road kind.** By § 18(7) a signed limit can be at most 80 in an *obec* on ordinary roads, 90 on ordinary rural roads, 130 on a divided SMV, 150 on a motorway, and 110 on a motorway or SMV in an *obec* (80 + 30). A reading above these is probably a misread (high for the bounds).
9. **Field, forest, private and car-park exits** do not end a limit (§ 2 w); a map junction with such a road must not reset the limit (high).
10. **The residential-zone end** may be only on the left side (annex IZ 5b), high.

- **Blank variable sign:** no legal meaning found. Treat it as no sign: not an end sign, and it does not set a limit. A limit shown earlier still ends at the next intersection, which on a motorway is the next interchange (medium).
- **Other rules for the code:**
  - **What ends a limit.** B 20a is ended by IZ 1a, IZ 1b, IZ 2a, IZ 2b, IZ 4a, IZ 4b, B 20b, B 26, a new B 20a, or the next intersection. B 20b and B 26 restore the general limit, or the zone limit inside an IZ 8a zone.
  - **Ramps.** A ramp limit ends at the merge, and a main-carriageway limit does not bind traffic entering downstream (both medium). This is consistent with RAMP_SECONDS / MERGE_SECONDS.
  - **150 km/h** is valid only on a motorway (D3 trial, variable signs, until the end of 2026).
  - **Snow chains:** 50 (§ 18(8)). Not visible to the camera, so no action.
  - **No night limit and no novice limit.**
  - **Panels.** Ignore B 20a with E 5 (mass > x t) or E 9 (other vehicle kind). Apply E 6 only when wet. Delay a sign with E 3a by the distance shown.
