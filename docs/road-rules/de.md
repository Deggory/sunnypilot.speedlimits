# Germany (DE): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Straßenverkehrs-Ordnung (StVO 2013) §§ 3, 18, 39, 41, 42 with Anlagen 2 and 3; Autobahn-Richtgeschwindigkeits-Verordnung (BABRiGeschwV 1978); Allgemeine Verwaltungsvorschrift zur StVO (VwV-StVO, administrative guidance, not binding on drivers); case law of the Oberlandesgerichte.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Built-up area ("geschlossene Ortschaft") | 50 km/h | For all motor vehicles. The area runs from the town sign Z.310 to Z.311. | StVO §3(3) Nr.1; Anlage 3 lfd. Nr. 5, 6 |
| Outside built-up areas, ordinary road | 100 km/h | For cars and other vehicles up to 3.5 t. **Does not apply** on Autobahnen, on roads whose carriageways for each direction are separated by a central reservation or other structure, nor on roads with at least two marked lanes for each direction (Z.295 or Z.340). On those roads there is no general limit. | StVO §3(3) Nr.2 c |
| Kraftfahrstraße (expressway, Z.331.1), single carriageway, one lane per direction | 100 km/h | It is a road outside built-up areas, not excluded by §3(3) Nr.2 c. Certainty medium-high (reading of §3 and §18 together; no ruling looked up). | StVO §3(3) Nr.2 c; §18 |
| Kraftfahrstraße with separated carriageways or 2+ lanes each way | no general limit | Advisory speed 130 (see below). | StVO §3(3) Nr.2 c; BABRiGeschwV §1 |
| Autobahn (Z.330.1) | no general limit | Advisory speed ("Richtgeschwindigkeit") 130 km/h. Faster than 50 is allowed on Autobahnen even inside built-up areas. | StVO §3(3) Nr.2 c, §18(5); BABRiGeschwV §1 |
| Tempo-30 zone (Z.274.1) | 30 km/h (or less in "verkehrsberuhigte Geschäftsbereiche") | Until Z.274.2. | Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 50, 51 |
| Verkehrsberuhigter Bereich (Z.325.1) | walking speed ("Schrittgeschwindigkeit") | Until Z.325.2. | Anlage 3 lfd. Nr. 12, 13 (Z.325.1/325.2) |

Richtgeschwindigkeit (BABRiGeschwV §1): drivers of cars and vehicles up to 3.5 t are "recommended" not to exceed 130 km/h on Autobahnen, and outside towns on roads with separated carriageways or two or more marked lanes per direction. It does not apply where Z.274 sets a limit. It is advice, not a limit. Certainty high.

Conditions:

- **Visibility under 50 m** through fog, snow or rain: at most 50 km/h (StVO §3(1)). Certainty high.
- **Snow chains fitted:** at most 50 km/h (StVO §3(4)).
- **Wet road:** no general limit for cars. A wet-road panel under Z.274 ("bei Nässe", Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 49.1) makes a signed limit apply only on a wet road.
- **Novice drivers:** no speed limit found in StVO §3. Certainty medium (the driving-licence rules were not searched for a speed limit).
- **Other vehicles:** 80 km/h for 3.5–7.5 t vehicles, cars with trailers and buses outside towns, 60 km/h for heavier ones (§3(3) Nr.2 a, b); on Autobahnen and separated Kraftfahrstraßen 80 / 60 / 100 by vehicle (§18(5)). A car without a trailer is not affected.
- **Signs win** over the general rules (§39(2)). Inside towns a Z.274 above 50 applies to all vehicles; outside towns the vehicle-class limits stay when a Z.274 allows more (Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 49).
- **Signed values:** on Autobahnen and "autobahnähnliche Straßen" no more than 130 may be ordered (VwV-StVO zu Z.274, Rn. 8). Inside towns, raising to at most 70 only on priority roads with conditions (Rn. 10). Administrative guidance, not a rule for drivers. Certainty high for the text.

The colours are from the official sign images, not from the text of the StVO; they were not re-checked in the sign catalogue (VzKat). Certainty high (common knowledge), source not verified.

Facts from the law:

- "Ab der Ortstafel gelten jeweils die für den Verkehr innerhalb oder außerhalb geschlossener Ortschaften bestehenden Vorschriften" (Anlage 3, to lfd. Nr. 5 and 6). **In Germany the town sign itself sets the 50** (unlike CH). Certainty high.
- On an Autobahn you may enter only at signed junctions (Anschlussstellen); on a Kraftfahrstraße only at crossings or junctions (§18(2)).
- Z.330.1 stands at the start of the Autobahn and at every Anschlussstelle entry, as a rule at the start of the slip road (VwV-StVO zu Z.330.1, Rn. 1). Z.330.2 / 331.2 stand at the end and at all exits of Anschlussstellen (VwV zu Z.330.2 und 331.2, Rn. 1). Where an Autobahn passes straight into a Kraftfahrstraße (or the other way), the end sign is left out and the other start sign is placed (Rn. 2).
- Z.331.1 is repeated at all crossings and junctions (VwV zu Z.331.1, Rn. 2).
- Exit signs (Z.333): black on yellow on Kraftfahrstraßen and "autobahnähnlich ausgebaute Straßen" (also allowed on white) (Anlage 3 to Z.332–333); on Autobahnen they are blue (not checked in the text).

How a camera can tell Autobahn from Kraftfahrstraße:

- The symbol on the blue start sign: motorway with overbridge (Z.330.1) vs. car (Z.331.1).
- Exit and direction signs: yellow exit signs point to a Kraftfahrstraße or autobahn-like road, not an Autobahn (Anlage 3, Z.333).
- The layout decides the general limit on a Kraftfahrstraße (separated or 2+ lanes each way: none; else 100), so the sign alone is not enough.

Where signs stand:

- Prescription signs stand where the rule begins; a distance panel shows when it starts later (§41(2)).
- Signs stand on the right; signs for single lanes are as a rule placed above them (§39(2)).
- Z.274 should stand far enough before the danger: 30–50 m in towns, 50–100 m outside, 200 m on Autobahnen (VwV zu Z.274, Rn. 6).

## How long a speed sign holds

- **Not to the next junction.** The StVO marks the end of a section limit with Z.278–282; the end is unmarked only when a length panel is shown, or when the sign comes with a danger sign and the end of the danger is beyond doubt (Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 55). The StVO makes stopping bans end at the next crossing or junction (Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 61), but says nothing of the kind for Z.274. Certainty high.
- **Case law:** OLG Hamm, 2 Ss OWi 524/01, 5 Jul 2001: a section rule does not hold only "bis zur nächsten Straßeneinmündung"; a Z.274 prohibition ends only at a Z.278. Summary at https://www.iww.de/quellenmaterial/id/2556 (court, date and file number from that page; full text not read). Secondary sources describe this as the settled view (citing OLG Hamm and OLG Celle), with LG Bonn NZV 2004, 98 contrary. Certainty high for the rule; the LG Bonn decision was not read.
- **Traffic turning in after the sign:** the same OLG Hamm decision says that the visibility principle requires the sign to be repeated after crossings and junctions, but "dies gilt jedoch nur für den Einbiegeverkehr": a driver who turned in after the sign and saw no repeat is not bound; a driver who passed the sign is. Certainty medium (from the summary).
- **Repetition (administrative):** Z.274, 276, 277 "sollen hinter solchen Kreuzungen und Einmündungen wiederholt werden, an denen mit dem Einbiegen ortsunkundiger Kraftfahrer zu rechnen ist"; on Autobahnen as a rule every 1000 m (VwV zu Z.274, 276, 277 und 277.1, Rn. 5). A remaining-length panel (Zusatzzeichen 1001) may be added (Rn. 4).
- **Danger signs:** a Z.274 placed with a danger sign ends where the danger clearly ends (Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 55). OLG Celle, 3 Ss (OWi) 190/18, 8 Nov 2018: with a general danger sign (Z.101) the end of the danger was not clear from the place, so the limit held until it was lifted. Source: https://www.weka.de/ordnungsamt-gewerbeamt/ende-einer-geschwindigkeitsbeschraenkung/ (secondary; decision itself not read). Certainty medium.
- **Steps on Autobahnen:** a step should be at most 40 km/h; the distance in metres between steps should be ten times the difference in km/h; after unrestricted sections the first limit should as a rule be 120 (VwV zu Z.274, Rn. 8).
- **Ramps:** Not found: a rule or ruling on whether a Z.274 on an entry slip road continues on the main carriageway. By Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 55 it would hold until Z.278/282; the VwV repetition rule implies the main carriageway carries its own signs. Certainty low; open point.
- **Interchanges (Autobahnkreuz / Autobahndreieck):** no rule ends a limit there. Countdown beacons Z.450 (300/200/100 m) announce exits and interchanges (Anlage 3). Certainty high that no special rule exists in the StVO.
- **Town signs and Z.274:** not found whether a Z.274 limit ends at a town sign. Certainty low; open point.

## Zones

- Z.274.1 / 274.2: Tempo-30 zone; with Z.274.1 zones below 30 are possible in verkehrsberuhigte Geschäftsbereiche (Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 50). Inside towns, away from priority roads (Z.306), drivers must expect Tempo-30 zones (§39(1a)).
- Z.274.1 should be placed so that it is seen before entering, and may stand back from the junction or on both sides (VwV zu Z.274.1/274.2, Rn. 2). The zone holds through junctions until Z.274.2.
- Z.325.1 / 325.2: walking speed in the whole area.

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status:** signs may be produced by lights as Wechselverkehrszeichen; then white and black may be inverted (black background, white symbols and border) (StVO §39(4); VwV zu §39, Rn. 23 and to §§ 39–43). They are traffic signs with full effect. OLG Düsseldorf, 2 Ss (OWi) 420/95, 5 Dec 1995: a limit shown on a gantry binds even if the driver did not consciously notice it (secondary source: https://www.anwaltonline.com/verkehrsrecht/urteile/42403/geschwindigkeitsbegrenzung-per-wechselzeichen-ist-wirksam-bei-geschwindigkeitsueberschreitung-droht-bussgeld-und-fahrverbot; not read in full). Certainty high for the StVO; medium for the ruling.
- **Lane-specific limits:** a sign for single lanes is as a rule placed above them (§39(2)); Z.274, 278, 282 inside a lane panel apply only to that lane (Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 49, 56, 60).
- **Details** are in the "Richtlinien für Wechselverkehrszeichen an Bundesfernstraßen (RWVZ)" and "RWVA" (VwV zu §39, Rn. 23). Not read.
- **Dark (blank) panel:** not found in the StVO or VwV. The StVO end of a section limit is Z.278/282, a length panel, or the end of a clearly marked danger (Anlage 2 lfd. Nr. 55); a dark panel is none of these. Press sources quoting ADAC lawyers say a limit shown on a gantry stays in force when the next gantry is dark, until it is lifted (e.g. https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/verkehr/tempolimit-auf-wechselverkehrszeichen-gilt-das-noch-bei-ausfall/, https://www.leinetal24.de/leben/auto/ausgeschaltete-schilderbruecke-wird-dadurch-ein-tempolimit-aufgehoben-zr-93337523.html). No court ruling found. Certainty medium.
- Note: StVO §39(5) says lit marker studs apply only when switched on; that sentence concerns markings, not signs.

## Supplementary panels

- Zusatzzeichen are traffic signs; black on white with a black border; directly, as a rule below the sign they belong to (§39(3)).
- "bei Nässe" (lfd. Nr. 49.1): the limit applies only on a wet road.
- Length panel (lfd. Nr. 54.3): length of the limit; no end sign follows.
- Distance panel: the rule starts later (§41(2)).
- Vehicle-class panels restrict a sign to that class; with "frei" that class is exempt (VwV zu §41, Rn. 4). Time panels exist. Their full catalogue (VzKat) was not read.
- Signs on vehicles (e.g. roadworks trailers) apply and take precedence over fixed signs (§39(6)).

## Special cases for a camera system

- **Truck limits:** a Z.274 with a weight or truck panel does not bind a car.
- **"bei Nässe" limits:** need rain or road-wetness information; without it, show the limit as conditional or apply it (conservative).
- **Time panels:** a limit with hours (e.g. noise limits at night) applies only then.
- **Signs on vehicles** (mobile roadworks): they take precedence (§39(6)) and move.
- **Advisory speed signs:** a blue square sign with a number ("Richtgeschwindigkeit") is advice, not a limit. It is not in Anlage 3 of the current StVO (no Z.380 found there); whether old signs still stand: not found.
- **Inverted matrix signs:** a white number on black is a valid limit (§39(4)).
- **No general limit on Autobahnen:** after Z.278 / 282 on an Autobahn the car has no limit, so the display shows none.
- **Dual-carriageway rural roads:** after Z.311 or Z.278 on such a road there is no limit, not 100.

Other rules the code should know:
