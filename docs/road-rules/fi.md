# Finland (FI): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Road Traffic Act (Tieliikennelaki) 729/2018 as amended, with its annexes 3.1–3.9 (the traffic signs) [L]; Traficom regulation TRAFICOM/59914/03.04.03.00/2026 on colours, construction and dimensions of traffic control devices [T]; the government bill HE 180/2017 vp that explains the act [HE].

Every source below was checked on 2026-10-29. The links in square brackets point to the list under **Sources**. Certainty marks: **high** = the rule is in the text; **medium** = a reading of the text, or a single non-legal source; **low** = a guess with no text behind it.

Sign codes: Finland renumbered its signs on 1 June 2020. The current codes are used here; the old codes, still common in older documents and the HE, are in brackets (C32 = old 361, C33 = 362, C34 = 363, C35 = 364, E22 = 571, E24 = 573) [HE], [L].

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Inside a *taajama* | 50 | Unless a traffic sign shows another limit. The *taajama* exists only where E22 signs it (§ 2 point 19: "an area of dense building shown by traffic signs"). | Act § 9(2), § 2(19) [L], [LA] (high) |
| *Moottoritie* (motorway, E15) | none of its own | The act has no motorway limit: § 58–61 give motorway rules but no speed. Without a sign, § 9 gives 80 (or 50 inside a *taajama*). In practice motorways are always signed with C32: 120 in summer, 100 in winter; some stay at 100. | [L] (high that no own limit exists); winter values [V], [F1] (high) |
| *Moottoriliikennetie* (expressway, E17) | none of its own | Same as the motorway: § 9 applies, the real limit is signed. Typical values were not verified. | [L] (high); typical values: not found |
| *Pihakatu* (residential street, E24) | 20 | Walking pace, never over 20. | Act § 43 [L] (high) |
| *Kävelykatu* (pedestrian street, E26) | 20 | Access only; walking pace, never over 20. | Act § 44 [L] (high) |
| *Pyöräkatu* (cycle street) | speed adapted to cycling | § 45 was not read in full. A number for cars was not found. | [L] (not found) |

## Road kinds and their signs

- **Speed limit C32** (*Nopeusrajoitus*): a round sign with a yellow background, a red ring and black numerals (Traficom drawing C32: yellow reflective, red reflective, black) [T] (high). Plates under prohibition and restriction signs have a yellow background and a red border (§ 82) [L] (high).
- **End of speed limit C33** (*Nopeusrajoitus päättyy*): yellow with the number in grey and black bars (drawing C33: yellow, grey, black) [T] (high).
- **Speed limit zone C34 / end of zone C35** (*Nopeusrajoitusalue* / *päättyy*): square yellow panels with the round sign; C35 has the grey number and bars (drawings C34, C35) [T] (high).
- **Taajama E22 / end E23**: yellow background, black symbol; E23 adds a red bar (drawings E22, E23) [T] (high for E22, medium for the E23 bar because the drawing text overlaps the next sign). E22 can carry a place-name plate H24, also in two languages (drawings E22_2, E22_2_2) [T] (high).
- **Moottoritie E15 / end E16; Moottoriliikennetie E17 / end E18**: the motorway and the expressway signs. The E15 and E17 rules cover the road **and its slip roads** ("liittymis- ja erkanemistie") [L] annex 3.5 (high). The colour (green, white symbol) was not read from the drawing text (medium).
- **Pihakatu E24 / E25, Kävelykatu E26 / E27**: rule areas with a 20 km/h maximum [L] § 43, § 44, annex 3.5 (high).
- **I18 Yleinen nopeusrajoitus** (general speed limits): a large blue-and-white information board at border crossings and car-ferry ports, listing Finland's general limits [L] annex 3.9; drawing I18 blue and white [T] (high). It is **not** a limit.
- **F25 Enimmäisnopeussuositus** (recommended maximum speed): a blue square with a white number, for a dangerous spot in normal conditions [L] annex 3.6; drawing F25 blue and white [T] (high). It is **advisory**, not binding.

Telling them apart for a camera:
- E15 shows the motorway symbol and E17 shows a car [L] annex 3.5 (high).
- A motorway that turns into a *moottoriliikennetie* is marked with E17 at the end of the motorway, not with E16; the reverse is marked with E15 [L] annex 3.5 (high).
- With plate H4 (distance) the E15–E18 signs are **advance signs**, not the start or the end [L] annex 3.5 (high).
- E22 stands on the right. A second E22 is placed on the left when there are two or more lanes in the direction of travel [L] annex 3.5 (high).
- E23 may stand **only on the left** on a single-carriageway, two-lane, two-way road [L] annex 3.5 (high).

## How long a speed sign holds

- **C32 holds "on that road" until the next C32–C34, E23, E24 or E26.** It also ends at E22, unless a new limit is signed right after the E22 [L] annex 3.3 C32 (high). It does **not** end at an intersection, at E15–E18, or after a distance. The text never lists those signs.
- HE 180/2017: "In practice speed limit signs have to be repeated on the road after an intersection, because a driver coming from the crossing road could not otherwise know" the limit [HE] (high). Repeaters after junctions are a placement practice, not an end rule.
- At a *taajama* entry with a limit other than 50, the C32 is placed 20–30 m after E22, so that both signs are visible at the same time [HE] (high, as a placement rule from the HE; current placement guidance not read).
- **A C32 over one lane** gives the limit of that lane and its shoulder [L] annex 3.3 C32 (high).
- **C33 with two-lane two-way roads** may stand on an island, on the left or overhead [L] annex 3.3 C33 (high).
- **Slip roads and interchanges**: the motorway and expressway rules cover the slip roads (E15/E17 text) [L] (high). No text says when a slip-road C32 ends at the merge. Reading "on that road" literally, the slip road's C32 holds on the slip road; once on the main carriageway, the main carriageway's signed limit applies (medium-low). For a car leaving the motorway, the exit ramp's C32 holds until the next C32 or the end of that road (medium).
- **Case law**: no KKO (Supreme Court) or KHO decision on how far a speed sign holds was found (not found).

## End signs

| Sign | Ends | After it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| C33 *Nopeusrajoitus päättyy* | the C32 limit | the general limit (50 or 80) per the current annex | [L] annex 3.3 (high) |
| C35 *Nopeusrajoitusalue päättyy* | the C34 zone | the general limit (or the next zone) | [L] annex 3.3 (high) |
| C34 (another zone) | the previous zone | the new zone value | [L] annex 3.3 C35 (high) |
| E23 *Taajama päättyy* | the *taajama* and any C32 | 80 unless a C32 follows | [L] annexes 3.3, 3.5 (high) |
| E22 *Taajama* | any C32 | 50 unless a C32 follows right after | [L] annex 3.3 (high) |
| E16 / E18 | motorway / expressway rules | **not** the speed limit | [L] annexes 3.3, 3.5 (high) |
| C32 + plate "Yleisrajoitus" | the previous limit | the number on the sign, which is the general limit (used when the limit before was over 80) | [L] annex 3.3, [HE] (high) |
| C32 + plate "Alue" | the road limit inside a zone | the number, which is the zone value | [L] annex 3.3, [HE] (high) |

C33 inside a zone: the current annex says C33 returns to the **general** limit. HE 180/2017 (describing the old rules) said 362 returns to "the general **or zone** limit" and noted that drivers misunderstand this, and that the zone officially ends only at 364 or another 363 [HE], [L]. Since C34 is not ended by C33, the zone value is the more likely legal result (medium). The "Alue" plate exists to avoid exactly this case.

## Zones

- **C34** sets a speed for an area. Inside it, C32, E24 or E26 may set another limit [L] annex 3.3 (high).
- **The zone ends only at C35 or at another C34** [L] annex 3.3 C35, [HE] (high). E23 is not listed as ending a zone (high on the text; in practice zones lie inside a *taajama*, medium).
- Zones of 30 or less may contain speed bumps and raised crossings without separate warning signs [L] annex 3.3 (high).
- **Rule areas**: *pihakatu* E24–E25 and *kävelykatu* E26–E27 (20 km/h), and the *pyöräkatu* (§ 45, not read) [L] (high for the first two).

## Variable message signs

- A variable sign whose content can change may show **a light symbol on a black background** (§ 78) [L] (high). HE 180/2017: instead of yellow the background is black and the numbers are white [HE] (high). Traficom annex 18: the black background is allowed only for warning, prohibition and restriction signs and their plates [T] (high).
- A variable C32 is a normal C32: **binding** and with the same validity ("on that road" until the next end sign) [L] § 78, annex 3.3 (high).
- Annex 18 counts mechanical prism signs and LED signs as variable signs. **Fixed signs turned by hand** to show another number (*käännettävä nopeusrajoitusmerkki*, used for winter limits) are **not** variable signs: they are ordinary yellow C32 [T] annex 18 (high).
- The variable motorway limits are run by the Fintraffic road traffic centre. They follow traffic and weather [F1], [HE] (high). On variable motorway sections, November and March are transition months: 120 can still be shown, in daylight and in good conditions [V], [F2] (high on the policy; medium on the exact conditions).
- **Blank / dark sign**: no rule found in the act, the annexes or the HE (not found).
  - Reading: a dark sign is not a sign. The last C32 passed (fixed or variable) holds until the next end sign (medium-low).
  - When lightning darkened the digital signs on Pohjantie (Oulu), temporary fixed signs were put up. So the authorities do not rely on the dark state meaning anything [K] (medium, news).

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **Winter and dark-season limits**: the signs themselves change.
   - The 2026–27 season starts on 28 Oct 2026, with all signs changed by 30 Oct 2026 in Northern Finland [E1]; Central and South-East Finland are similar [E2], [E3] (high).
   - The change is made by swapping or turning signs [T], [V] (high). The camera reads the real value.
   - A map may carry the summer value: in winter, prefer the camera over a map limit that is higher than the last sign read (medium; OSM winter tagging in Finland not checked).
2. **Taajama comes only from the signs.** A built-up look without an E22 is not a *taajama*, and an E22 can stand far from buildings [L] § 2(19) (high). A road-type network that guesses "town" from the scene can be wrong in law.
3. **Advisory boards with numbers**: I18 at borders and ports and F25 (blue square) must not become a limit [L] annexes 3.6, 3.9 (high).
4. **Lane signs on gantries**: each lane can have its own C32 [L] annex 3.3 (high). Take the ego lane's sign.
5. **Two-language plates and text**: E22 can carry bilingual H24 plates [T] (high). This does not change the limit.
6. **E15–E18 do not touch the speed limit.** A motorway sign at an on-ramp carries no number. The limit comes from the C32 placed after it (medium on placement; high that E15 sets no limit).
7. **Who decides**:
   - On state roads (*maantie*), the road authority places the signs; on streets, the municipality (§ 71) [L] (high).
   - The municipality places the *taajama* sign (§ 72) [L] (high).
   - About 40,000 of the 78,000 km of state roads have a signed limit; the rest are at 80 [V] (high).
   - The regional road tasks moved from the ELY centres to the *elinvoimakeskus* centres. The 2026 press releases come from those centres [E1], [E3] (medium on the exact legal transfer).
8. **90 km/h**: the Väylävirasto memo planned a 90 km/h pilot (from spring 2025) [V]. A news item reports a pilot [VU]. Sites and current status were not verified (low). The detector should accept 90 as a Finnish value.

## For the comma

**IMPLICIT_LIMITS row** (km/h):

```python
"FI": {"town": 50, "rural": 80, "expressway": 80, "motorway": 80},
```

- Finland has **no** motorway or *moottoriliikennetie* general limit. § 9 (80 outside a *taajama*) covers them [L] (high).
- In `vision.py`, `None` means "no general limit" and gives 0 (no limit). That is right for a German Autobahn and **wrong** for Finland. So use 80, not `None`.
- Inside a *taajama* § 9 gives 50 even on a motorway. This is rare and always signed (medium).

**Extra kinds and seasonal limits**:
- *pihakatu* and *kävelykatu* = 20 (E24, E26) [L] § 43, § 44 (high). Map E24/E26 to 20 if the detector learns them; their end signs E25 and E27 return to the general limit or the zone (medium).
- C34 zone = its number until C35 or the next C34.
- Seasonal limits need no rule: the signs change. Only the map preference in winter (special case 1) matters.

**Intersection rule: partly (no end at junctions).**
- C32 holds "on that road" until C32–C34, E23, E24, E26, or E22 (Act § 78 with annex 3.3, sign C32) [L] (high).
- Going straight through a junction keeps the limit.
- Turning onto another road leaves the road the sign was on. The general limit applies there unless that road has its own C32, which by practice stands after the junction [HE] (medium on applying "kyseisellä tiellä" to turns).
- The existing MAP_SECONDS logic (a map change means another road) matches this. Its comment cites Swiss OSStr art. 16; for Finland the basis is annex 3.3.

**Blank variable sign**: no legal meaning found. Keep the limit held; do not reset to the general limit (medium-low; see Variable message signs).

**Other rules for the code**:
- `ROAD_SIGNS` must **not** reset the held limit for `motorway_start`, `motorway_end`, `expressway_start` or `expressway_end` in Finland: C32 does not end at E15–E18 [L] annex 3.3 (high). This needs a per-country flag; the signs should only set `self.road`.
- The ramp logic should also be checked: when merging, it sets the limit to `_general("motorway")`. With 80 that is safe and low; the motorway C32 repeater after the junction then corrects it (medium).
- `town_entry` (E22) → 50 and `town_exit` (E23) → 80 are correct. A C32 right after E22 must win. It does in `update()`, because numbers are applied after road signs in the same update.
- C33 (end, value 0) → the general limit of the current kind (50/80) is correct outside a zone. Inside a C34 zone the result should be the zone value (medium).
- H4 advance plates: do not apply the number yet. H12 "Ei koske" with cars: ignore the sign (high).
- Ignore I18 and F25.
- Variable signs show white or light numerals on black with a red ring; they must be detected as normal C32.
