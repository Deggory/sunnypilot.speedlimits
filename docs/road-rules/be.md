# Belgium (BE): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Arrêté royal of 1 December 1975, the "règlement général sur la police de la
circulation routière" (AR 1975, the Code de la route / Wegcode), as amended. Since the sixth state reform, speed limits
are regional, so art. 11 has separate Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon versions. Sign placement follows the
Arrêté ministériel of 11 October 1976 (AM 1976).

Every source below was read on 2026-10-29. Links point to the text that was read. Certainty marks apply to
interpretations only: **high** means the text says it, **medium** means it follows from the text but is not stated in
those words, **low** means it is a guess. "Not found" means it could not be verified.

Note on sources:

- The AR 1975 was read in its consolidated text on codedelaroute.be ([AR 1975 FR][ar]; Dutch, [AR 1975 NL][arnl]). That
  site is not the Moniteur belge; the official consolidation is [Justel][justel], which was not read.
- Regional amendments were read in the PDFs listed on the [amendments page][mods].
- **Transition:** on 1 June 2027 the AR 1975 is replaced by four texts, all starting that day ([codedelaroute 2027
  page][y2027]; [federal Code, final article][cvp]):
  - the federal Code de la voie publique (AR of 3 June 2024, [CVP][cvp]);
  - the Brussels Code ([Brussels code][brucode]);
  - the Flemish Code ([Flemish code][vlcode]);
  - the Walloon Code ([Walloon code, AGW 17.7.2025][wal]).

  Old signs keep their meaning until 1 January 2045 ([CVP][cvp]). The speed values below do not change in 2027; see
  [Open points](#open-points).

Conditions:

- **Wet roads, rain, snow, fog or night:** no general limit of its own in art. 11 ([AR 1975 FR][ar]; certainty high
  for art. 11). The overview table lists none ([table][table]). A weather-dependent limit was not found elsewhere.
- **Novice drivers:** no speed rule in art. 11 ([AR 1975 FR][ar]). A novice limit was not found.
- **Brussels in practice:** the official table notes that the 120 general limit "n'est pratiquement jamais
  appliquée" in Brussels, because signs set lower limits almost everywhere ([table][table]).
- **Other vehicles,** outside agglomérations ([AR art. 11.2 and 11.3][ar]; [table][table]):
  - over 3.5 t, and buses: 90 on autoroutes and on 120 roads;
  - coaches with a 100 km/h limiter: 100 on autoroutes;
  - buses and coaches elsewhere: 75;
  - over 7.5 t elsewhere: 60;
  - class B mopeds: 45; class A mopeds: 25.
- **Low-speed parts** ([AR art. 11.1, 22ter, 22quinquies, 22octies, 22novies][ar]):
  - 30 on reserved paths (F99/F101), all regions;
  - Wallonia: 30 on the parts reserved to pedestrians and cyclists shown by D9 or D10 (Walloon art. 11.1;
    [table][table]);
  - 30 when crossing raised devices (A14 + F87);
  - at most 30 in cycle zones (F111).

## Road kinds and their signs

Sign appearance: the AR 1975 shows the signs only as images. Their colours and layout were **not verified** from a
written source (the Wikimedia sign files could not be downloaded). The names below are the legal ones.

- **Agglomération (built-up area), F1a/F1b to F3a/F3b.**
  - Definition: "un espace qui comprend des immeubles bâtis" whose accesses carry F1, F1a or F1b and whose exits carry
    F3, F3a or F3b (art. 2.12, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - The older F1 and F3 could be kept only until 1 June 2015 (same article).
  - F1a/F1b set the region's town limit: 30 in Brussels, 50 in Flanders and Wallonia (art. 71, F1a/F1b, with
    regional versions, [AR 1975 FR][ar]; certainty high).
  - Placed on the right at every access; may be repeated on the left.
  - Brussels uses a regional entry sign saying 30 holds in the whole Region ([DH 2020][dh]; appearance not verified).
  - From 2027 the town sign becomes R25/R27. "La vitesse maximale qui s'y applique peut être indiquée à titre informatif
    sur le signal en bas à droite" ([CVP, art. 70][cvp]; certainty high).
- **Autoroute (motorway), F5 to F7.**
  - Definition: the public road whose start or access carries F5 and whose end carries F7 (art. 2.3,
    [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - F5 means "Commencement d'une autoroute ou accès à une autoroute". The motorway rules apply from the sign, so an F5
    stands on each on-ramp (art. 71, F5, [AR 1975 FR][ar]; certainty high).
  - F5 may be reproduced on direction signs (F25, F27, F29, F31, F39, F41) to show that the route uses a motorway. A
    camera must not read that small symbol as a motorway start (certainty high for the text; medium for the risk).
  - Limit 120; minimum 70 (art. 11.2.1° a), 21.2).
  - Motorway limits stay federal after 2027: "Sur autoroute, la vitesse maximale autorisée est de 120km/h"
    ([CVP art. 24 § 2][cvp]). The Brussels and Flemish codes set no motorway value of their own
    ([Brussels code][brucode]; [Flemish code][vlcode]; certainty medium, from reading their speed articles).
- **Route pour automobiles (motor road), F9 to F11.** This is the "expressway" kind.
  - Definition: art. 2.4 ([AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - Art. 22 only reserves the road to motor vehicles and applies the motorway bans (no U-turn, no reversing, no
    stopping). **It sets no speed.**
  - The limit follows the layout (art. 11.2): 120 when there are at least 2 lanes per direction physically separated;
    otherwise the rural limit of the region (70 Brussels and Flanders, 90 Wallonia). Certainty high.
  - From 2027 the sign becomes R33/R35. R33 reads: "début d'une route pour automobiles. La réglementation particulière
    vaut jusqu'au prochain carrefour" ([CVP, art. 70][cvp]; certainty high for the text).
- **The 120 road with no sign.** Outside agglomérations, a road with 4+ lanes (2+ per direction) whose directions are
  separated "autrement qu'avec des marques routières" is 120 by law (art. 11.2.1° b), all three regions).
  - No sign marks it. The camera cannot tell it from a normal rural road by signs.
  - The same layout separated only by paint is 70 (Brussels, Flanders) or 90 (Wallonia). Certainty high.
- **How to tell F5, F9 and the 120 road apart:**
  - F5: the motorway sign, on every access ramp.
  - F9: the motor-road sign; its limit depends on the layout.
  - 120 road: no sign at all, only the layout (2+2 lanes and a central reservation or barrier).
  - None of the three has a speed number on the start sign (art. 71, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
- **Zones**: see [Zones](#zones).

## How long a speed sign holds

- **The rule (art. 68.3, sign C43), all regions.** "A partir du signal jusqu'au prochain carrefour inclus, ou jusqu'à
  chaque signal C43 avec ou sans validité zonale, ou jusqu'au signal indiquant le début ou la fin d'une agglomération,
  d'une zone résidentielle, d'une zone de rencontre ou d'une zone piétonne, interdiction de circuler à une vitesse
  supérieure à celle qui est indiquée" ([AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - In Dutch: "Vanaf het verkeersbord tot en met het volgend kruispunt" ([AR 1975 NL][arnl]). Certainty high.
- **Carrefour** (kruispunt): "le lieu de rencontre de deux ou plusieurs voies publiques" (art. 2.9,
  [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - A private driveway is not a public road, so it is not a carrefour (certainty medium).
  - Whether a field track or a motorway on-ramp counts under the current text: no court ruling found.
  - From 1 June 2027 the definition adds: "Le lieu où une entrée rejoint la voie principale d'une autoroute est
    considéré comme un carrefour" ([CVP art. 2, I, 10][cvp]; certainty high).
- **Inside an agglomération,** after the next carrefour the town limit returns: "Lorsqu'à l'intérieur d'une
  agglomération un signal C43 indique une autre vitesse, la vitesse maximale de 30 km/h [Brussels] / 50 km/h
  [Flanders, Wallonia] est de nouveau en vigueur à partir du carrefour suivant" (art. 71, F1a/F1b, [AR 1975 FR][ar];
  certainty high).
  - Brussels' C43 50 signs on main roads (about 1350 placed in 2020, [DH 2020][dh]) therefore hold only to the next
    carrefour each. They must be repeated after it.
- **On motorways:** the limit holds to the next on-ramp. The Flemish traffic centre: "een snelheidsbeperking is geldig
  vanaf het bord tot het volgende kruispunt. Op een snelweg betekent dat: vanaf het bord tot de volgende oprit"
  ([Verkeerscentrum FAQ][vcfaq]). Certainty high that this is the authority's reading.
  - AM 1976 art. 9.11 confirms the idea: C46 is used only when the end of the ban "ne coïncide pas avec un carrefour ou
    un accès à une autoroute" ([AM 1976][am]).
- **End signs are used only between carrefours.** AM 1976 art. 9.10 says C45 "n'est placé que si la fin de
  l'interdiction ne coïncide pas avec un carrefour" ([AM 1976][am]). So a limit that ends at a carrefour has no end
  sign (certainty high).
- **Exceptions to the carrefour rule:**
  - A C43 "30" placed above F1/F1a/F1b "vaut sur l'ensemble des voiries comprises dans les limites de l'agglomération"
    (art. 68.3, C43, [AR 1975 FR][ar]). It ends with a C45 "30" above F3.
  - A zonal C43 holds to the end-of-zone sign (art. 65.5.10, see [Zones](#zones)).
  - The Flemish C43 "90" is an ordinary C43. Legally it holds to the next carrefour; after that the 70 applies again
    (art. 11.2.2° and 68.3; certainty high for the text). Whether Flanders repeats every 90 after each junction was
    not checked.
- **Repetition:** for fixed signs, no general rule requiring a C43 to be repeated after each junction was found in
  the AM 1976. The obligation exists for variable signs (AM 6.5.3). Given art. 68.3, an unrepeated limit has ended.
- **Advance notice:**
  - A prohibition sign may be announced by the same sign with a distance panel (art. 68.2, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - Outside agglomérations, the first C43 must be announced this way when it lowers the limit by more than 20 km/h
    (AM 9.9.3°, [AM 1976][am]).
- **Chamber of Representatives,** bill 52K1653: describes the same C43 rule and the confusion of drivers about
  consecutive C43 signs ([Kamer 52K1653][kamer]). Background only.
- **Case law:** no Cassation ruling on the C43 and the carrefour was found.

## End signs

All in art. 68.3 and art. 71 ([AR 1975 FR][ar]); placement in AM 1976 art. 9.10 and 9.11 ([AM 1976][am]).

- **C45, end of the limit imposed by C43.**
  - "La mention km sur le signal est facultative."
  - When C43 30 stands above F1, a C45 30 must stand above F3 of that agglomération.
  - After C45, the general limit of the road kind applies (certainty high: art. 11 holds wherever no C43 applies).
- **C46, end of all local prohibitions on moving vehicles.** It ends C33, C35, C39 and C43. It is used only where the
  end does not coincide with a carrefour or a motorway access (AM 9.11). Same effect as C45 for speed (certainty high).
- **Another C43** ends the previous one (art. 68.3).
- **Road-kind ends:**
  - F3a/F3b, end of agglomération: the rural limit of the region (70 or 90), or 120 on a divided 2+2 road;
  - F7, end of autoroute, and F11, end of route pour automobiles: the limit of the road that follows, by layout and
    region (certainty medium; the AR names no value for them).
- **Zone ends:**
  - F4b, end of zone 30;
  - the zonal end sign of a C43 speed zone (art. 65.5.3);
  - F12b, end of woonerf/erf (zone résidentielle/de rencontre);
  - F105, end of pedestrian zone;
  - F113, end of cycle zone.

  Each one ends that zone's limit, and the limit of the road around it returns. A C43 placed at a zone sign ends at the
  sign of an agglomération, woonerf or pedestrian zone (art. 68.3).
- **From 2027:** end-of-zone signs (Z3) show the zonal sign "dans des tons gris" ([CVP, art. 71][cvp]).

## Zones

- **Zone 30, F4a to F4b.** "Dans les zones délimitées par les signaux routiers F4a et F4b, la vitesse est limitée à
  30 km à l'heure" (art. 22quater, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
- **School surroundings (abords d'école).**
  - Delimited by F4a/F4b, with an A23 sign (art. 2.37, [AR 1975 FR][ar]). So 30 km/h.
  - They may be shown by variable signs. A variable school limit "ne peut apparaître que pendant les périodes
    d'arrivée et de départ des enfants à l'école" (AM 9.9.1°, [AM 1976][am]).
- **Zonal C43 (zone de vitesse).**
  - Allowed values (AM 6.7.1, [AM 1976][am]):
    - 30;
    - 50, outside agglomérations, in built-up areas or with many pedestrians or cyclists;
    - 70, outside agglomérations.
  - The limit holds from the zone sign to the end-of-zone sign (art. 65.5.10, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - Inside the zone, a plain C43 with another speed holds to the next carrefour; then "la vitesse de la zone est de
    nouveau en vigueur". No C43 higher than the zone speed may be placed in it.
  - Inside the zone, after a woonerf, encounter zone or school area ends, the zone speed applies again without a new
    sign.
  - Brussels and Flanders: if an agglomération or another speed zone lies inside the zone, the zone sign must be placed
    again after it.
  - Zone stickers or recognition signs on poles that repeat the zone speed "n'ont en eux-mêmes aucune conséquence
    obligatoire" (65.5.11). A camera may read them; they do not set a limit (certainty high).
- **Woonerf / erf (zone résidentielle) and zone de rencontre, F12a to F12b.** 20 km/h (art. 22bis 3°,
  [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
- **Pedestrian zone, F103 to F105:** walking pace for admitted drivers (art. 22sexies).
- **Play street (rue réservée au jeu):** walking pace (art. 22septies).
- **School street (rue scolaire):** walking pace ("au pas"; art. 22undecies).
- **Cycle zone, F111 to F113:** "La vitesse ne peut jamais y être supérieure à 30" (art. 22novies).
- **Cycle street (rue cyclable):** its limit was not checked.
- **Ville 30 in Brussels** is not a zone. It is the general Brussels town limit (art. 11.1), so it needs no zone sign.
  Bruxelles Mobilité planned to remove about 6000 old zone-30 signs after the change ([DH 2020][dh]).

## Variable message signs

- **Meaning:** "Les signaux gardent leur signification" (art. 65.3, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
  - Dark backgrounds and light symbols are allowed. The red of the symbol and its border may not change.
  - A variable C43 is a C43 and follows art. 68.3: it holds to the next carrefour, or on a motorway to the next
    on-ramp ([Verkeerscentrum FAQ][vcfaq]). Certainty high.
- **Per lane:** a sign placed above a lane "ne vaut que pour la bande de circulation ... concernée" (art. 65.4,
  [AR 1975 FR][ar]). Certainty high.
- **Repetition and end** (AM 1976 art. 6.5.3, [AM 1976][am]): variable signs "sont répétée[s] après chaque carrefour ou
  chaque accès à une autoroute". An end sign is required on a motorway, or where the limit does not end at a
  carrefour. Certainty high.
- **Where used:** in principle only on roads with at least 2 lanes per direction. School areas are the exception
  (AM 6.5.2). Zonal validity is not allowed on variable signs, except F4a/F4b for school areas (AM 6.5.3).
- **Lane lights** (art. 62bis, [AR 1975 FR][ar]):
  - red cross: lane closed;
  - green downward arrow: lane open;
  - orange oblique arrow: leave the lane in the direction shown.

  "Een groene pijl heft een voorgaande snelheidsbeperking in geen geval op" ([Verkeerscentrum FAQ][vcfaq]; certainty
  high).
- **Peak lanes (spitsstroken):** their use is set by the lane signs or lights. If neither works, driving on them is
  forbidden except in the cases of art. 9.7, to enter or leave the motorway, or to change direction
  (art. 22decies, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
- **Blank panels:** **not found.** No text says what a blank gantry means.
  - Interpretation, certainty medium: a blank panel is no sign. A limit shown earlier still holds to the next carrefour
    (on a motorway, the next on-ramp), because art. 68.3 ends a C43 only at a carrefour, at another C43 or at a
    road-kind sign.
  - A blank gantry after an on-ramp means the limit has ended and the general limit (120 on a motorway) applies.
  - AM 6.5.3 requires repetition after each access and an end sign on motorways, so a properly run system shows either
    the number again or an end sign (certainty medium).

## Supplementary panels

General rule: a blue rectangular panel with white inscriptions under a sign "complète, précise ou limite" its meaning
(art. 65.2, [AR 1975 FR][ar]). Validity periods are black on white or white on blue (same article).

- **Mass:** "Lorsqu'une masse est indiquée sur un panneau additionnel, l'interdiction n'est applicable qu'aux
  véhicules dont la masse maximale autorisée excède la limite fixée" (art. 68.3, C43). A C43 with "3,5 t" does not
  apply to a car. Certainty high.
- **Distance:** an identical sign with a distance panel announces where the limit starts (art. 68.2; AM 9.9.3° type Ia
  panel). Zone signs too (65.5.7). The limit is not yet in force at the panel. Certainty high.
- **"Rappel" (reminder),** with a "Rappel" panel (AM 9.9.2° and 9.9.4°, [AM 1976][am]):
  - Inside agglomérations, C43 50 may not be placed. But a C43 50 "Rappel" must be placed where a higher-speed section
    ends. This is in the AM text read, which has no regional variant there; how it applies to Brussels' 30 was not
    checked.
  - On 2+2 roads separated by markings (11.2.2° a)), a C43 90 may only be used with "Rappel". This rule refers to the
    old federal numbering; how it maps onto the regional versions was not checked.
  - Zone reminders carry "Rappel" (65.5.8).
- **Exit only (art. 65.6):** when a sign concerns only a right-hand exit of a multi-lane carriageway, it carries an
  additional panel. A C43 with that panel applies to the exit, not the main carriageway (certainty high; panel image not
  verified).
- **Lane signs:** see art. 65.4 under [Variable message signs](#variable-message-signs).
- **School times:** a variable school C43 shows only at school times (AM 9.9.1°). Fixed signs with a time panel: their
  period is on the panel (art. 65.2).
- **Cyclist panels (M1 to M24)** concern cycles, mopeds and speed pedelecs. They do not change a car's limit.
- **Weather panels (rain, snow):** not found in the AR 1975 or the AM 1976.

## Special cases for a camera system

- **C43 30 above the town sign (F1a/F1b).** It holds for the whole agglomération, not just to the next carrefour
  (art. 68.3).
  - In SpeedLimitMemory, if `town_entry` is confirmed in a later frame than the 30, the town's general limit (50 in
    Flanders or Wallonia) overwrites the 30. The two should be read as one (certainty high for the law, medium for the
    code risk).
- **Brussels: two numbers mean "30 unless signed".** The 50 signs on main roads end at each carrefour. After a junction
  with no new sign, the limit is 30 again (F1a/F1b Brussels text). A camera that holds 50 until the next number keeps
  50 too long. Certainty high.
- **Flanders: a 90 sign holds to the next carrefour, then 70.** Same issue. Certainty high for the text.
- **Divided rural roads are 120 with no sign.** Outside agglomérations, a 2+2 road with a physical separation is 120
  in every region, and 70 or 90 if the separation is only paint. Only the layout tells, not a sign.
- **Wallonia, chaussée à voie centrale: 70 with no sign.** Recognisable only by its markings: two parallel dashed
  lines of short dash pairs on each side (art. 75.3, [AR 1975 FR][ar]).
- **F5 on direction signs.** The motorway symbol may appear on direction signs for routes that use a motorway (art. 71,
  F5). It is not a motorway start.
- **Ramps.** F5 stands on each access ramp. The 50/70 numbers on a ramp end at the junction where the ramp joins the
  main carriageway. That junction is a carrefour (on-ramp per [Verkeerscentrum FAQ][vcfaq]; carrefour explicitly from
  2027, [CVP art. 2, I, 10][cvp]). This matches the code's ramp logic (certainty medium-high).
- **Exit-only panels (65.6)** on motorway and ring-road gantries: a number for the exit lane must not be taken for the
  main carriageway.
- **Per-lane gantries (65.4):** each lane may show its own number. Take the one above the car's lane.
- **Zone recognition stickers (65.5.11)** repeat the zone speed. They are not legal signs but agree with the zone; reading
  them is harmless.
- **Zone signs vs plain C43:** a zonal C43 (on a white rectangular panel, art. 65.5.3) holds to the zone's end across
  junctions. A plain C43 ends at the next carrefour. The camera must tell them apart (`zone` kind).
- **Truck signs:** a C43 with a mass panel is not ours (art. 68.3).
- **Bilingual names:** town signs carry place names in one or two languages. This does not affect the limit.
- **From 2027:** sign codes change (R25/R27 town, R29/R31 motorway, R33/R35 motor road, Z1-C43/Z3-C43 zone) and the
  town sign may show its speed as information ([CVP][cvp]). Old signs stay valid until 2045. A model trained on today's
  signs should still read them (certainty medium; appearance of the new signs not verified).

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS rows.** The limit depends on the region, so one "BE" row is wrong somewhere. Recommended: one row
  per region, chosen by GPS (certainty high for the values; the region keys are a proposal):

  ```python
  "BE-BRU": {"town": 30, "rural": 70, "expressway": 70, "motorway": 120},
  "BE-VLG": {"town": 50, "rural": 70, "expressway": 70, "motorway": 120},
  "BE-WAL": {"town": 50, "rural": 90, "expressway": 90, "motorway": 120},
  ```

  - Values: art. 11.1 and 11.2, regional versions ([AR 1975 FR][ar]; [table][table]).
  - **`expressway`** is the route pour automobiles (F9). It has no limit of its own (art. 22), so the row gives the
    region's rural value. A 120 for F9 would be wrong on an undivided F9. **Never use None:** `_general()` turns None
    into 0, which means "no limit".
  - **Divided roads (120 by layout):** the format cannot say "120 if physically divided". Proposed fifth key
    `"rural_divided": 120`, used when the road-type network or the map says 2+ lanes per direction with a physical
    separation. It applies on an F9 road and on unsigned rural roads alike. Without such a key, the map's limit must
    correct it (certainty medium).
  - **Fallback "BE"** when the region is unknown: `{"town": 50, "rural": 70, "expressway": 70, "motorway": 120}`. It
    is too high in Brussels towns (30) and too low on Walloon rural roads (90). Certainty high for that consequence.
  - Region by GPS: the Brussels-Capital Region is the 19 municipalities. Flanders and Wallonia are split by the
    language border. Polygons are needed; no source was checked for them.
  - `town` is F1a/F1b (`other_sign:town_entry`) and F3a/F3b (`other_sign:town_exit`). `motorway` is F5/F7.
    `expressway` is F9/F11.
  - Extra kinds:
    - zone 30, F4a: 30, until F4b;
    - school area, F4a + A23: 30;
    - woonerf or encounter zone, F12a: 20, no number on the sign;
    - pedestrian zone, play street, school street: walking pace;
    - zonal C43: the number, until the zone end.
  - Motorway minimum: 70 (art. 21.2). It is not a maximum.
  - Novice drivers and rain: none found, nothing to encode.
- **The intersection rule: yes, art. 68.3** ([AR 1975 FR][ar]; certainty high). A C43 holds "jusqu'au prochain
  carrefour inclus". It also ends at another C43, or at the start or end of an agglomération, woonerf, encounter zone
  or pedestrian zone.
  - After the carrefour, the general limit of the road kind returns.
  - On a motorway, the limit holds to the next on-ramp ([Verkeerscentrum FAQ][vcfaq]). From 2027 the on-ramp is a
    carrefour by definition ([CVP art. 2, I, 10][cvp]).
  - Exceptions:
    - C43 30 above the town sign: the whole agglomération;
    - zonal C43: to the zone end;
    - inside a zone or an agglomération, a different C43 holds to the next carrefour, then the zone or town speed
      returns.
  - What it means for the code: drop a plain C43 at the first junction after it (from the map), unless a new number is
    read, and give `_general(road)`. This is the same rule the code comment cites for CH.
- **Blank VMS:** not found in the law. Recommended, certainty medium:
  - a blank panel is no sign; keep the limit held until the next carrefour or on-ramp, then use the general limit;
  - a blank panel is not an end sign (value 0), and not a reason to keep a gantry limit past the next on-ramp;
  - a green arrow does not cancel a limit; a red cross closes the lane and is not a speed.
- **Other rules the code should know:**
  - A C43 with a mass panel (e.g. "3,5 t") is for heavy vehicles only: ignore it.
  - Distance panels are advance notices: do not apply the number at the panel.
  - The 65.6 exit panel means exit lane only: take it as the ramp's limit, not the carriageway's.
  - C45 and C46 both give value 0 (the general limit of the known road). Most limits end at a carrefour with no end
    sign, so the junction rule above matters more than end signs.
  - A sign over another lane (65.4) is not ours.
  - Zone stickers and "Rappel" panels repeat an existing limit. Reading them is harmless.
  - School-area variable signs show 30 only at school times. When blank, the road's normal limit applies.
