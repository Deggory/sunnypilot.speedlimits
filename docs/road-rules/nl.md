# Netherlands (NL): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Reglement verkeersregels en verkeerstekens 1990 (RVV 1990), version in force from
1 July 2026, art. 1, 19–22a, 45, 62–67, 73, 86a and Bijlage 1 (the sign catalogue); Besluit administratieve bepalingen
inzake het wegverkeer (BABW), version from 1 January 2026, art. 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 16, 48; Wegenverkeerswet 1994 (WVW 1994)
art. 20a; Uitvoeringsvoorschriften BABW inzake verkeerstekens (UV, version from 1 October 2023: ministerial rules for
road authorities on when and where to place signs, not rules for drivers).

Every source was read on 2026-10-29; links point to the text that was read. Certainty marks apply to interpretations:
**high** = the text says it; **medium** = it follows from the text but is not said in those words; **low** = a guess.
"Not found" = could not be verified. The official consolidated texts on wetten.overheid.nl were read directly. The web
search budget of this session ran out before case law and secondary sources could be searched: see
[Open points](#open-points).

No amendment of the speed articles is pending: the RVV change log lists only art. 35 and 61a as amended without an entry
date ([RVV information page][rvvinfo]).

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inside the built-up area ("bebouwde kom", H1 to H2) | 50 km/h | Motor vehicles. No wet, night, visibility or novice rule. | [RVV art. 20 a][rvv20] |
| Outside the built-up area, "other roads" | 80 km/h | Motor vehicles. | [RVV art. 21 a][rvv21] |
| Autoweg (G3 to G4, the expressway kind) | 100 km/h | Only **outside** the built-up area: art. 21 covers only roads "buiten de bebouwde kom". An autoweg inside a kom falls under art. 20 (50), certainty medium-high. Rijkswaterstaat: "Op de N-wegen geldt een maximumsnelheid van 80 of 100 km/h", so many autowegen are signed 80. | [RVV art. 21 a][rvv21]; [RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max] |
| Autosnelweg (G1 to G2, motorway) | 130 km/h by law | On most motorways a sign sets **100 km/h from 06:00 to 19:00** (A1 "100" with a "6-19 h" time panel); outside those hours 120 or 130 where allowed. Some signs carry two windows, usually 100 and 120. A 130 by night is not signed because it is the default. "In 2025 is op enkele trajecten de maximumsnelheid tussen 06.00 en 19.00 uur verhoogd naar 130 km/h." | [RVV art. 21 a][rvv21]; [RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max] |
| Erf (G5 to G6, residential "woonerf") | 15 km/h | "Bestuurders mogen binnen een erf niet sneller rijden dan 15 km per uur." A sign may not raise it (art. 63b(1)(c)). | [RVV art. 45][rvv45]; [RVV art. 63b][rvv63b] |
| 30 / 60 / 15 zones | as signed | Only with A1 + the word "zone"; there is no unsigned 30. | [RVV art. 66][rvv66]; [BABW art. 9][babw9] |
| Oil-supply emergency | 90 km/h | On autosnelwegen and autowegen, only when a ministerial regulation says so (lapses after at most about 5 months). Signs showing more give way to it. | [RVV art. 86a][rvv86a]; [RVV art. 63b(1)(b)][rvv63b] |

Conditions:

- **Wet road, rain, fog, night, novice drivers:** no speed rule. RVV art. 19–22a contain none ([art. 20][rvv20],
  [21][rvv21], [22][rvv22]). Certainty high. Art. 19 is the general duty to be able to stop within the distance the
  driver can see and that is free ([art. 19][rvv19]).
- **Vehicle classes** ([art. 22][rvv22]): car with a trailer up to 3,500 kg: 90; trucks, buses (non-T100) and heavy
  motorhomes: 80; T100 buses: 100. When a sign shows more than these class limits, "geldt de laagste aangegeven
  snelheid" ([art. 63b(1)][rvv63b]). A plain car is not affected.
- **Signs beat rules:** "Verkeerstekens gaan boven verkeersregels" ([art. 63][rvv63]).
- **Built-up area boundary:** set by the municipal council ([WVW 1994 art. 20a][wvw20a]; [BABW art. 48][babw48]) and
  shown by H1/H2. UV: the boundary is where continuous development starts, and the road should change character there
  ([UV II art. 4, "Borden H1 en H2"][uv4]).
- **Motorways and the kom:** the UV allow G1 only on a road "gelegen buiten de bebouwde kom" ([UV II art. 4, G1][uv4]),
  so a motorway is outside the kom by design. Certainty medium (UV binds road authorities, not drivers).

## Road kinds and their signs

Looks below are from the official drawings in [RVV Bijlage 1][rvvb1] (images listed under [Sources](#sources)).

| Kind | Begins | Ends | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Built-up area | **H1** "Bebouwde kom" | **H2** "Einde bebouwde kom" | H1: blue landscape board, white border, place name in white. H2: the same with a red diagonal bar across the name. |
| Autosnelweg (motorway) | **G1** | **G2** | G1: blue portrait rectangle, white motorway pictogram (two carriageways under a bridge). G2: the same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Autoweg (expressway) | **G3** | **G4** | G3: blue square, white car seen from the front. G4: the same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Erf | **G5** | **G6** | G5: blue landscape rectangle with a house, a walking adult, a child with a ball and a car. G6: the same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Zone (30, 60, 15) | A1 with "zone" above it | "einde zone" board | No drawing of the zone board in Bijlage 1; the rule is text only ([RVV art. 66][rvv66]; [BABW art. 9][babw9]). |

Definitions: an autosnelweg is a "weg, aangeduid door bord G1", an autoweg a "weg, aangeduid door bord G3"; parking
areas, fuel stations and bus stops alongside are not part of them ([RVV art. 1][rvv1]). Certainty high.

**Motorway or autoweg for a camera:**

- The pictogram is the only legal test: motorway symbol (G1) or car (G3). Certainty high.
- Layout (UV, binding on authorities only; [UV II art. 4][uv4]):
  - G1 roads: at least 2.5 km long, outside the kom, priority road, grade-separated junctions, separated carriageways,
    flowing ramps.
  - G3 roads: at least 2.5 km, priority road, no property exits, at least 7.00 m paved width, left-turn pockets at
    junctions. An autoweg can therefore be single-carriageway with at-grade junctions and traffic lights.
- Route shields: A-numbers are white on red, N-numbers black on yellow (drawings K1, K4, K5 in
  [Bijlage 1][rvvb1]). Rijkswaterstaat manages "autosnelwegen (wegen met een A-nummer) en een deel van de autowegen
  (wegen met een N-nummer)" ([RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max]). Use the shield as a hint only: whether every A-road is
  a motorway was not verified (low).
- Direction signs are blue on both kinds (K1 motorway, K5 other roads), so the guide-sign colour does not tell them
  apart. Certainty high (drawings).

**Where signs stand** ([UV II art. 2][uv2], items 9–11; [UV II art. 4][uv4]):

- Signs go on the right, above a lane when they hold only for that lane, or on the left when they hold only for the
  left side. They may also stand above the carriageway, or be repeated on the left for visibility.
- On motorway sections with two or more lanes and on dual-carriageway autowegen, A1 stands **on both sides** of the
  carriageway it applies to. On any road wider than 5 m or with 2+ lanes per direction, A1 is also placed on the left
  where possible. Expect the same number twice in one frame.
- At most two signs (not counting panels) on one post outside the kom. Signs stack in Bijlage 1 order: a sign listed
  earlier goes **under** one listed later, so a speed sign (chapter A) sits under an H1 (chapter H) at a town entry.
  Certainty medium for the inference.
- H2 may stand on the left or the right.
- Where G1 stands (start of the on-ramp or at the merge): not found.

## How long a speed sign holds

- **No intersection rule.** Neither RVV 1990, nor BABW, nor UV contains a rule ending an A1 at the next junction
  ([RVV art. 62–67][rvv66]; [BABW art. 3–16][babw]; [UV][uv]). An A1 holds on the road where it stands until another
  A1, A2, F8, the end of its zone, or a change of road kind (H1/H2, G1–G4) that brings a different general limit.
  Certainty medium: the rule is absent; no court ruling was read.
- **Repeats:** UV define a "herhalingsbord" as a sign repeating "eenzelfde bord dat aan het begin van een en hetzelfde
  wegvak geplaatst is" ([UV I art. 1][uv1]). Repeats need no traffic decision ([BABW art. 16][babw16]). No duty to
  repeat after each junction was found.
- **Turning into another road:** the sign applies to the road it stands on. A driver turning into another road has
  passed no sign for that road, so its general limit applies. Certainty medium (follows from art. 62 and 65; no
  ruling read).
- **Zones** hold through junctions in the whole area between the zone sign and the end-of-zone signs
  ([RVV art. 66(2)][rvv66]). Certainty high.
- **Time panels:** an A1 "100" over a "6-19 h" panel holds only 06:00–19:00; outside it the default (130) or the value
  in a second window holds ([RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max]; [RVV art. 67(2)][rvv67]). Certainty high.
- **Ramps and slip roads:**
  - RVV art. 1 separates the "invoegstrook" and "uitrijstrook" from the "doorgaande rijbaan" ("rijbaan zonder de invoeg-
    en uitrijstroken") by a block marking ([RVV art. 1][rvv1]). The ramp is part of the autosnelweg once G1 is passed.
  - No rule says that a limit signed on a ramp ends at the merge (not found). Legally it holds until the next sign.
    Medium: in practice the main carriageway has its own signs on both sides (see above).
  - Whether a limit on the main carriageway binds traffic that joined after the sign: no rule found; by the
    turning-in reasoning above it does not until the next sign (medium).
  - Whether Rijkswaterstaat repeats A1 after each on-ramp or interchange: not found.
  - An exit ramp ends at G2 (motorway) or G4 (autoweg), where art. 20 or 21 applies.
- **Speed steps:** UV forbid an A1 that lowers the limit by more than 30 km/h at once, and an A1 too close after a
  higher one for drivers to adapt ([UV II art. 4, A1 item 5][uv4]). So a drop from 100 goes in steps
  (for example 100 → 70 → 50). Rule for authorities; certainty high for the text.
- **Fixed and electronic together:** "Indien zowel door verkeerstekens op borden als door elektronische
  signaleringsborden een maximumsnelheid wordt aangegeven, geldt de laagste aangegeven maximumsnelheid"
  ([RVV art. 63b(2)][rvv63b]). Certainty high.
- **Case law:** not searched (see Open points).

Whether an A1 inside the kom (say 70) ends at H2 without an A2: no rule found. Legally the A1 holds until ended
(medium); in practice a new A1 or the general 80 follows. The code's town_exit → rural mapping is the practical
choice.

## Zones

- **Zone signs:** "zone" can be put above A1 (and some other signs), with or without a description of the area
  ([BABW art. 9][babw9]). Without a description, an end-of-zone board stands at the zone boundary ([BABW art. 9(2)][babw9]).
  The sign holds in the whole area ([RVV art. 66][rvv66]). Certainty high.
- **Values allowed** ([UV II art. 4, A1][uv4]):
  - inside the kom: 70, 30, 15 on sections, 30 or 20 at danger points, plus 50 as a reminder of the general limit;
  - outside the kom: autowegen 80 (70 at danger points); autosnelwegen 130, 120, 110, 100, 90, 80, 70, 60, 50;
    other roads 60 or 30 (70 at traffic lights, 60 or 50 at danger points);
  - 30 and 60 (zone or road) only where the road has mainly a residential function and has been adapted.

  These bind road authorities. A reading outside this set (say 40 or 90 on a rural road) is unlikely; use it only as
  a plausibility check (medium).
- **Erf:** G5 to G6, 15 km/h ([RVV art. 45][rvv45]). An A1 "15" (or "15 zone") may be added in a woonerf as a
  reminder ([UV II art. 4, A1 item 4][uv4]). G6 stands at every exit ([UV II art. 4, G6][uv4]).
- **Built-up area default:** still 50 by law (RVV art. 20 as of 1 July 2026). Towns that want 30 must sign it (A1 or
  A1 zone). Certainty high.

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status:** "Verkeersborden mogen op een elektronisch signaleringsbord worden weergegeven"
  ([RVV art. 64a][rvv64a]). Bijlage 1 has two signs of its own for it: **A3** "Maximumsnelheid op een electronisch
  signaleringsbord" and **F9** (end of all electronic prohibitions) ([Bijlage 1][rvvb1]). Certainty high.
- **Values and placement of A3** ([UV II art. 4, A3][uv4]): only 130, 120, 110, 100, 90, 80, 70, 60, 50, on motorway
  sections. Above the carriageway it hangs **above each lane**; it may also stand on the right, and then also on the
  left when the carriageway is wider than 5 m.
- **Look:** the official A3 drawing is a number in white dots on a black field, without a red ring ([Bijlage 1][rvvb1]).
  UV allow a white-on-black symbol on electronic boards ([UV II art. 3, item 15][uv3]). Whether today's boards add a
  red ring: not verified (low).
- **Lowest wins:** fixed and electronic limits together give the lower one ([RVV art. 63b(2)][rvv63b]); Rijkswaterstaat:
  "geldt de laagst getoonde snelheid zoals weergegeven op de blikken borden en de verkeerssignalering"
  ([RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max]). Certainty high.
- **Lanes:** a sign may be limited to one or more lanes ([RVV art. 65(1)][rvv65]). Lane lights ([RVV art. 73][rvv73]):
  - green arrow, or a speed shown with A3: the lane may be used;
  - red cross: the lane may not be used (the hard shoulder only in emergencies);
  - white arrow: advance warning of a red cross.

  An A3 above a lane therefore applies to that lane. Certainty medium-high.
- **When it is used** ([RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max]): 70 and 50 behind queues, lower speeds at incidents, and 80
  on section-control stretches.
- **Red cross:** Rijkswaterstaat says there are about 16,500 matrix boards. Ignoring a red cross has been a criminal
  offence since 2015 ([RWS nietvoorniX][rws-x]).
- **Peak lanes ("spitsstroken"):**
  - The lane is open under a green arrow and closed under a red cross; C23-01 "Spitsstrook open", C23-03 "Einde
    spitsstrook" ([RVV art. 1][rvv1]; [Bijlage 1][rvvb1]).
  - When it is open, the limit is 100 and on some stretches 80. It is shown on rotating roadside signs ("draaiborden")
    ([RWS spitsstroken][rws-spits]; [RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max]).
- **Black signs:** where speeds change often, Rijkswaterstaat puts up "zwarte verkeersborden" so the new speed stands
  out ([RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max]). The exact look was not verified (low).
- **Blank or dark board:** no provision found. Under art. 63b(2) the fixed signs keep applying when nothing is shown.
  Whether an A3 limit continues past a dark gantry: not found. F9 exists as the explicit end, which suggests that
  blank is not an end sign (low-medium).

## Supplementary panels

- **Effect** ([RVV art. 67][rvv67]):
  - a panel may explain the sign;
  - a panel with only symbols: the sign holds only for those road users or that behaviour;
  - "uitgezonderd" + symbols: the sign does not hold for them;
  - text: the behaviour follows from the panel.

  Panels may limit a sign's scope ([BABW art. 8(2)(b)][babw8]); the traffic decision must state them
  ([BABW art. 14][babw]). Certainty high.
- **Time:** "6-19 h" under A1 100 on motorways, sometimes with a second window, usually 120 ([RWS maximumsnelheid][rws-max]).
- **Vehicle class:** a symbol from Bijlage 1, or a mass in tonnes with "t", for class limits ([UV II art. 4, A1
  "Categorale maxima"][uv4]). A car ignores a truck or trailer panel.
- **Lane:** L11 and L12 "Verkeersbord geldt alleen voor de aangegeven rijstrook" (blue boards with lane arrows and
  the sign drawn in them) ([Bijlage 1][rvvb1]).
- **Distance ahead:** a "voorwaarschuwingsbord" is the same sign placed in advance with a distance panel
  ([UV I art. 1(1)][uv1]). It does not apply where it stands.
- **Danger:** UV prefer an A1 at a danger point combined with a sign or panel showing the danger ([UV II art. 4, A1][uv4]).
- **Wet road:** no wet-road panel found in RVV, BABW or UV (not found).

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **Time-window panel on motorways** (100 "6-19 h", sometimes a second window): by far the most common Dutch case. A
   "100" read at 20:00 without the panel is a false low limit.
2. **Advisory A4** (blue square, white number + "km/h") is not a limit; A5 ends it. The classifier must not take it
   as a maximum ([Bijlage 1][rvvb1]).
3. **Advance-warning A1** with a distance panel applies only from that distance on.
4. **Duplicates:** A1 on both sides of motorways and wide roads ([UV II art. 2, item 11][uv2]); same reading twice.
5. **Gantries:** A3 per lane; lowest of fixed and electronic wins (art. 63b(2)); F9 ends; red cross and arrows are not
   speeds.
6. **Road-surface numbers** are reminders: markings may be laid "ter herinnering aan de ter plaatse geldende
   maximumsnelheid" ([BABW art. 6(2)][babw6]). They do not set a limit on their own (medium-high).
7. **Zone vs single sign:** the word "zone" makes the limit hold through junctions until the end-of-zone board.
8. **Erf** (G5) means 15 with no number on the sign.
9. **Autoweg inside a kom:** the 100 does not apply there; 50 unless signed (medium-high).
10. **Class panels** (trucks, trailers, tonnage): ignore for a car.
11. **Spitsstrook draaiborden** at the roadside show the open-lane limit (100 or 80).
12. **Speed step rule** (at most 30 lower per sign): a reading 40+ below the held limit outside a road-kind change is
    suspect (medium).
13. **Oil-crisis 90** (art. 86a): by regulation, not by sign. The camera cannot see it; ignore.

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:**

  ```python
  "NL": {"town": 50, "rural": 80, "expressway": 100, "motorway": 130},
  ```

  - town 50 ([art. 20][rvv20]), rural 80, expressway = autoweg (G3) 100, motorway = autosnelweg (G1) 130
    ([art. 21][rvv21]). Certainty high for the law.
  - **Motorway by day:** 130 is the legal default, but most motorways carry A1 100 with a "6-19 h" panel. The code
    needs a **time condition** on a reading, for example `{"value": 100, "hours": (6, 19)}`, active 06:00–19:00 local
    time, else the default (130) or the second window's value. Without reading the panel, the comma keeps 100 at
    night: wrong but low (safe).
  - **Expressway inside a kom:** 50, not 100. When the road-type network says "town" or an H1 was passed, town
    should win over expressway.
  - **Erf:** add a road sign `other_sign:woonerf_start` → 15 (`"residential": 15`) and `woonerf_end` → town. ROAD_SIGNS
    has no erf today.
  - **Zones:** a reading with the "zone" word should not be dropped by the MAP_SECONDS rule at a junction inside the
    zone.
- **Intersection rule: no.** No article ends a speed sign at the next intersection (RVV art. 62–67, BABW, UV; certainty
  medium, no case law read). A sign still binds only on the road it stands on, so the MAP_SECONDS rule (the map wins
  after a change of road) fits a turn into another road. It must not end a limit while the car stays on the same road
  through a junction.
- **Blank variable sign:** not an end sign (no provision makes it one; F9 is the end sign; low-medium). Keep the last
  limit; end it on F9, on a fixed A1/A2, or on a gantry showing another value. Any timeout is an engineering choice,
  not law.
- **Other rules for the code:**
  1. Fixed and electronic at once: take the **lower** ([art. 63b(2)][rvv63b]). This matches the code's "lower on a tie"
     only when both are read; a later fixed sign does not cancel a lower gantry value on its own.
  2. A2 and F8 → general limit of the current road kind; F9 → end of A3 limits.
  3. A4 / A5 are advisory: never a limit.
  4. H2 → rural (80); G2 / G4 → rural or town by location; G6 → town.
  5. Values a fixed A1 may show ([UV II art. 4][uv4]) can serve as a plausibility filter, not a hard rule.
