# Luxembourg (LU): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Arrêté grand-ducal of 23 November 1955 *portant règlement de la circulation sur toutes les voies publiques* ("AGD 1955"), consolidated in the official *Code de la route* of 12 June 2026 [CR]; Règlement grand-ducal of 7 May 2021 on derogatory limits on the State's normal roads outside built-up areas (as amended on 24 January 2024) [CR]; Règlement grand-ducal of 23 May 2022 on traffic on the *grande voirie* and service areas [CR].

Every claim below was checked on 2026-10-29 against the source named in brackets; the keys are listed with their URLs under **Sources**. Page numbers ("p.") are the printed pages of the [CR] PDF. Statements marked *interpretation* carry a certainty (high / medium / low). The rest is the text of the law.

Luxembourg sign codes are its own. They are **not** the French or Belgian ones: C,14 = maximum speed, C,17a = end of all restrictions, C,17b = end of a speed limit, E,9a/E,9b = built-up area begins/ends, E,15/E,16 = motorway begins/ends, E,17/E,18 = *route pour véhicules automoteurs* begins/ends, H,1/H,2 = zone begins/ends.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

AGD 1955 art. 139 §2 sets these limits "même en l'absence d'une signalisation spécifique", "sans préjudice de limitations de vitesse dérogatoires indiquées par le signal C,14" (p. 261) [CR].

Derogatory limits that are always signed by C,14 (no implicit rule):

- RGD of 7 May 2021: limits of 50, 70 or 110 on named State roads outside built-up areas. 110 applies on sections of the N7.
- On other N7 sections (Diekirch–Hoscheid) the limit is "110, or 90 in rain or other precipitation". This is signed by a C,14 "110" plus a C,14 "90" with the panel "en cas de pluie ou d'autres précipitations" (art. 1 and §4, p. 459–467) [CR].
- RGD of 23 May 2022, art. 3.7: 70 on deceleration lanes to service areas. Definitions (art. 2) [CR]:

- **Autoroute** (1.6, p. 65): separate carriageways for the two directions, no level intersections, access and exit ramps, and "indiquée comme autoroute par le signal E,15".
- **Route pour véhicules automoteurs** (1.7, p. 65): another public road reserved for motor traffic, with no access to adjoining properties, "dont les entrées et les sorties sont signalées comme telles". It may have level junctions; the definition does not require separated carriageways.
- **Ramps** (1.9 and 1.10): access and exit ramps "sont considérées comme faisant partie de l'autoroute ou de la route pour véhicules automoteurs".
- **Agglomération** (1.31, p. 67): at least 10 houses close together, its limits marked by E,9a/E,9b within 100 m of the first and last houses. *Lieux-dits* that meet the same criteria count as built-up areas.

Listed roads, art. 111 §5 (p. 251) [CR]:
- Motorways: A1, A3, A4, A6, A7, A13.
- RVA: B3 (giratoire Gluck – CR224), B7 (Schieren – N27A), B40 (liaison Micheville).

Motorway or expressway? For a camera:
- Only the sign tells them apart: E,15/E,16 (motorway pictogram) against E,17/E,18 (car pictogram).
- The general limit differs: motorway 130 (110 in rain), RVA 90.
- Road numbers help too: A-numbers are motorways and B-numbers are RVA roads, as listed in art. 111 §5. *Interpretation, medium*: the art. 111 list is short, and other B-roads exist (the RGD 2022 annex mentions B4 and B4A) whose status was not checked.

Where the signs stand:
- Motorway entry is only via ramps or carriageways signed E,15; exit is via E,16 (art. 156 §2, p. 269) [CR]. RVA entry is via E,17 and exit via E,18 (art. 156ter, p. 272–273) [CR].
- The official plans for ramps (RGD 2022, annex 1, p. 472–475) [CR]:
  - E,15 stands at the start of the access ramp, right after the junction with the normal road. No speed sign is shown on the access ramp.
  - E,16 stands part-way up the exit ramp.
  - Where the exit ramp ends at traffic lights, a C,14 "70" and then a C,14 "50" follow E,16, each at least 50 m apart.
- Signs stand on the right and may be repeated on the left or overhead (art. 108 §1, p. 245) [CR].
- Minimum sign diameters: 500 mm in town, 700 mm outside, 900 mm on motorways (art. 107, general provisions on prohibition signs, p. 201) [CR].

Town entry practice:
- An administrative instruction of the Ponts et Chaussées (SV1, 25 September 1996, annexed to the Commission de circulation's avis on speed) describes the town-entry assembly on State roads [CC p. 34–35]:
  - on its face: the speed-limit sign, a road-number plate and the yellow name panel;
  - on its back: the end-of-speed-limit sign, the road number and the crossed-out name.
- It also says where two localities follow each other, the left-hand panel may drop the end sign and the right-hand panel the limit sign.
- *Interpretation, medium*: on State roads a C,14 "50" often stands with E,9a and a C,17b "50" with E,9b. The law does not require this; 50 holds from E,9a alone.
- Current practice was not verified on site.

## How long a speed sign holds

- **C,14** "indique aux conducteurs que la vitesse maximale est limitée au nombre inscrit sur le signal" (art. 107 III.14, p. 199) [CR]. The definition gives no end point.
- **C,17b** "indique la fin de la vitesse maximale autorisée qui est inscrite sur le signal" (p. 200). **C,17a** ends all prohibitions for moving vehicles (p. 200) [CR].
- **No intersection rule for speed.** The AGD 1955 ties the scope of signs to the next intersection only for parking and stopping signs. C,18, C,19 and C,20a/b are "applicables à partir de l'aplomb du signal jusqu'à la prochaine intersection" (art. 107 VI, p. 233) [CR]. C,14 has no such clause. *Interpretation, medium-high*: a C,14 holds through intersections until:
  - a C,17b or C,17a,
  - another C,14, or
  - a sign that changes the road kind (see below).
- **Turning onto another road** is not addressed. Art. 108 §1 makes signs apply "sur toute la largeur de la voie publique ouverte à la circulation" (p. 245) [CR], i.e. to the public road where they stand. *Interpretation, medium*: after turning onto a different road, the previous road's C,14 no longer applies, and the new road's general limit or signs do.
- **Repetition after junctions**: no rule found that requires or forbids repeating a C,14 after a junction.
- **Change of road kind** (E,9a/E,9b, E,15/E,16, E,17/E,18): the text does not say whether these end a C,14 limit. *Interpretation, medium*: each one changes the general regime (art. 139 §2 refers to "agglomération", "autoroute" and so on). The 1996 SV1 practice also pairs C,17b with E,9b. A camera should therefore reset to the new road kind's general limit.
- **Access ramps** belong to the motorway or RVA (art. 2 1.9). The standard ramp plan has no speed sign on an access ramp, so the motorway's 130 (110 in rain) applies from E,15 (p. 472–475) [CR].
  - Some ramps carry their own derogatory C,14 (50/70 under RGD 2022 annex 3).
  - Whether such a ramp limit ends where the ramp merges was **not found**. No text or plan shows a C,17b at the merge.
  - *Interpretation, low-medium*: the ramp is its own carriageway, and the main carriageway's regime applies once merged.
- **Main-carriageway limit and entering traffic**: not found. No text says whether a C,14 placed on the main carriageway before a junction binds traffic that joins from the ramp after it. *Interpretation, medium*: it does not, because the sign was not placed for that traffic.
- **Exit ramps**: E,16 is followed by C,14 "70" and "50" at signal-controlled ends (annex 1, p. 473, 475) [CR]. These limits end at the junction with the normal road, where another road begins (*interpretation, medium*).
- **Interchanges** (*échangeurs*, art. 2 1.8) are part of the motorway. No special rule for the scope of limits there.
- **Case law**: **not found**. The 2026 consolidated Code reproduces no ruling on the scope of C,14. Luxembourg's case-law databases were not searched (see Open points).

## Zones

- **Zone signs (H,1 / H,2)**:
  - Start, H,1: **yellow** background with a black border and "ZONE" in black letters at the top, carrying the sign that applies, e.g. a C,14 "30".
  - End, H,2: **white** background, the sign drawn in black or light grey, crossed by a black or dark-grey diagonal band.
  - Neither sign is required where another zone begins (art. 107 VIII and general provisions, p. 237–238) [CR].
  - A zone 30 is an H,1 carrying C,14 "30". Art. 139 sets no general zone-30 limit [CR]; [TP] lists "30 in zones 30 and rues cyclables".
- **20 km/h zones** (art. 139 §2 a), p. 261) [CR]:
  - residential zone: E,25a … E,25b;
  - *zone de rencontre*: E,26a … E,26b;
  - pedestrian zone: E,27a … E,27b.
  - Art. 2 1.27–1.29 defines these zones as signed at their entries and exits.
- **Rue cyclable** E,18a … E,18aa: 30 km/h (art. 139 §2 a bis) [CR].
- **Service areas**: 50 on the approach, 30 inside, signed by C,14 (RGD 2022 art. 4) [CR].

## Variable message signs

- **Legal basis**: art. 156bis (p. 270–272) [CR] governs the motorway traffic-control system, CITA.
  - Restrictions apply when traffic, infrastructure or weather (including precipitation and visibility) make traffic on the *grande voirie* difficult or dangerous.
  - They are triggered and lifted by the authorities running the *Centre de contrôle du trafic* (loi of 16 August 1967, art. 6).
  - They are lifted "dès que les circonstances qui les justifient ne sont plus données".
- **Limits**: 90, 70 or 50 km/h on the *grande voirie* (§1 b). On normal roads next to access ramps, 70 or 50 (§1, last paragraph).
  - §3: an ozone episode above 160 µg/m³ (1 h) gives 90 km/h, shown by an adapted C,14.
  - §4bis: where safety requires, the limit may be displayed **permanently**.
- **Where a VMS limit starts and ends**, art. 156bis (5) [CR]:
  - The restrictions "s'appliquent à partir du support porteur de panneaux à signalisation dynamique ou des signaux colorés lumineux précités le plus approprié".
  - "Elles prennent fin à partir du premier support porteur approprié ou à partir de tout autre endroit approprié situé en amont du premier support, moyennant la mise en place d'une signalisation conforme au paragraphe 1er".
  - The limit may be lowered in steps upstream.
  - *Interpretation, medium*: a CITA limit ends at the next gantry that no longer shows it, or earlier where compliant signs say so.
- **Blank or dark panels**: the law gives a blank panel **no meaning of its own**. It is not listed as an end sign (not found in art. 107, 109 or 156bis).
  - *Interpretation, medium-low*: under (5), the next gantry that no longer shows the restriction ends it. A blank gantry **after** a gantry that showed a limit therefore most likely marks the end, and the road kind's general limit comes back.
  - A blank first gantry imposes nothing.
- **Sign look on VMS**: prohibition signs (C,14, C,17b) on dynamic panels must match the regulation. Where technically needed they may appear "en teinte claire sur fond foncé", i.e. light on dark, inverted (art. 107, general provisions on prohibition signs, p. 201) [CR]. The same applies to G lane signs and to additional panels on VMS (p. 236, 239) [CR].
- **Lane-specific signs**: art. 108 §1 (p. 245) [CR] says signs apply to the whole width of the road, "toutefois, un signal peut ne s'appliquer qu'à une ou plusieurs voies de la chaussée" under art. 107 ch. VII (lane signs G,1a–G,5b) and ch. IX (additional panels).
  - G signs show one arrow per lane; the sign applies to the lanes that carry it. Colours: white on blue on motorways, black on yellow elsewhere, black on white at worksites.
  - Lane signals over lanes "s'appliquent aux seules voies au-dessus desquelles ils sont placés" (art. 109 §1, p. 246) [CR].
  - *Interpretation, medium*: a C,14 shown on a gantry over a single lane binds only that lane.
- **Red X and arrows** (art. 109 §1, p. 246–247) [CR]:
  - Red crossed bars: the lane must not be used and must be left before the signal.
  - Green arrow pointing down: the lane may be used.
  - Flashing orange oblique arrow: leave the lane in the direction shown.
  - Hard-shoulder running (art. 156bis (1bis)): opened by a green lane signal and closed by a red one.
- **HGV ban on VMS**: "circulation interdite poids lourds > 13m" (art. 156bis §2). Not relevant for cars.
- **Planned A3 gantries**: dynamic gantries for the A3 in 2026 are reported only by the press and were **not verified**.

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **Two C,14 on one post with a condition.**
   - N7: "110" and "90 + rain" (RGD 2021).
   - A3: "110" and "70 + Mon–Fri hours" (RM 2025).
   - The camera must read the panel. The conditional value applies only when its condition holds; otherwise the plain value does.
   - The code's tie-break ("the lower limit on a tie") would pick 90 or 70 wrongly in dry weather or outside the hours.
2. **Weight panels**: a C,14 with a model 1 panel ("7,5 t", goods-vehicle symbol) does not apply to cars. The same applies to C,14 with the hard-shoulder bus lane (D,10).
3. **Town signs are yellow** (E,9a/E,9b). E,9b has the old name crossed in red and may show the next place with an arrow.
4. **Suspended signs**: a red St Andrew's cross of retro-reflective tape can suspend E,1a–E,10a (so **E,9a/E,9b town signs**), E,21d–E,22ca, the lane signs G,1a–G,5b and additional panels (art. 102 §2 h, p. 182) [CR]. A taped-over town sign must not trigger the 50.
5. **Inverted VMS**: C,14 shown light on dark on dynamic panels (art. 107, p. 201) [CR].
6. **Signs on the back**: C,17a–C,17d may be on the back of signs for the opposite direction. E,9b may be on the back of E,9a; H,2 on the back of H,1; E,25b–E,27b on the back of their start signs. The camera only sees the face turned to it, so this is harmless (art. 108 §1, p. 245) [CR].
7. **Rain rule on motorways**: 130 drops to 110 in "pluie ou d'autres précipitations" **without any sign** (art. 139 §2 d) [CR].
8. **Motorway tunnels**: 90 without a C,14, once a tunnel is "signalé comme tel" (E,28a) [CR].
9. **Zones**: H,1 is a yellow rectangle with "ZONE" plus C,14. Do not read the C,14 inside it as an ordinary sign that ends at the next junction or that an end sign must cancel; H,2 or another zone ends it. Inside a zone 30, E,18aa (end of cycle street) returns to 30, not 50.
10. **Lieux-dits**: a place-name sign for a *lieu-dit* that does not count as a built-up area is not E,9a and gives no 50.
    - The 1996 SV1 instruction describes such signs as white on blue (F,14 at that time) or black on white, and says "il n'existe pas de limitation de vitesse générale pour les lieux-dits" ([CC] p. 36–37). 70 is the Commission's recommendation there, and it must be signed.
    - The current code number for these signs was **not found** (*low*).
11. **Ozone 90**: a temporary C,14 "90" on motorways (art. 156bis §3). Read as a normal sign.
12. **Advance signs**: E,16, E,17 and E,18 with a distance announce a change but do not make it. The code must not switch the road kind on an announcing sign (p. 215) [CR].

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row** (*high*):
  ```python
  "LU": {"town": 50, "rural": 90, "expressway": 90, "motorway": 130},
  ```
  - "expressway" is the *route pour véhicules automoteurs* (E,17/E,18), which falls under art. 139 §2 c), so 90.
  - "motorway" is 130 in dry weather (art. 139 §2 d).
  - Extra conditions the four keys cannot hold:
    - **Rain**: motorway 110 "en cas de pluie ou d'autres précipitations".
      - This needs a precipitation input, e.g. a `"motorway_wet": 110` entry used while the wipers run continuously. That openpilot exposes the wiper state on every car was **not verified** here.
      - Without such an input, the row's 130 is the legal dry value. The open choice is whether to show 130 or the conservative 110 when rain is unknown (see Open points).
      - The same input decides the N7 "90 + rain panel" signs.
    - **Motorway tunnels**: 90 from E,28a to E,28b. This would need a `tunnel_start`/`tunnel_end` sign class mapped like ROAD_SIGNS, applied on motorways only (*medium*).
    - **Zones**: 20 (E,25a/E,26a/E,27a) and 30 (E,18a, H,1 "30") are read as limits from the signs. They are not road kinds.
  - **Novice drivers**: no speed limit, so nothing to encode (art. 83 and art. 139) [CR] (*high*).
  - **Trailers and vehicles over 7.5 t**: 75 outside town and 90 on motorways. Not needed for a car without a trailer.
- **The intersection rule: no.**
  - C,14 (art. 107 III.14, p. 199) has no "until the next intersection" clause, while C,18–C,20 explicitly do (art. 107 VI, p. 233) [CR].
  - A C,14 holds through junctions until C,17b/C,17a, a new C,14, or a change of road kind (*interpretation, medium-high*).
  - After a **turn onto another road**, the old limit lapses because signs apply to the road where they stand (art. 108 §1) (*medium*).
  - The code's MAP_SECONDS rule (built for CH OSStr art. 16) is acceptable for LU when the map change reflects a turn. It has no legal basis when the car goes straight on through a junction.
  - Case law: not found.
- **Blank variable sign**: no legal meaning in the AGD 1955, and not an end sign.
  - Under art. 156bis (5) a CITA limit ends at the first appropriate gantry that no longer shows it (*medium-low*).
  - So a blank gantry following a limit = end of the VMS limit, and the general limit of the road kind applies (motorway 130 / 110 in rain).
  - A blank first gantry changes nothing.
- **Other rules**:
  1. ROAD_SIGNS mapping: E,15 → motorway, E,16 → rural, E,17 → expressway, E,18 → rural, E,9a → town, E,9b → rural. Ignore these signs when they carry a distance (advance signs) or a red tape cross.
  2. The general limit holds from the first E,15 on the access ramp: the ramp is part of the motorway (art. 2 1.9). A number read on an access ramp is a derogatory ramp limit (RGD 2022 annex 3). The code's "ends at the merge" heuristic is a reasonable reading but **not in the text** (*low-medium*).
  3. On exit ramps: E,16, then C,14 70 and 50. These are ordinary numbers after a road-kind change and need no ramp logic.
  4. Conditional panels (rain, days and hours, weight) must make a C,14 conditional, not the lower value by default.
  5. Hard-shoulder or bus-lane limits (D,10 + C,14) are not for cars.
