# Italy (IT): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Codice della strada (D.Lgs. 30 aprile 1992, n. 285; "CdS"), text in force on Normattiva, art. 2, 3, 4, 117, 141, 142 (art. 142 in the version in force since 14-12-2024); Regolamento di esecuzione (D.P.R. 16 dicembre 1992, n. 495; "Reg."), art. 77, 78, 81, 83, 104, 116, 119, 122, 131, 135, 170.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roads in a centro abitato (built-up area) | 50 km/h | May be raised to at most 70 on urban roads whose design allows it, with signs. | CdS art. 142(1) |
| Strada extraurbana secondaria (type C) and extraurbana locale (F) | 90 km/h | | CdS art. 142(1) |
| Strada extraurbana principale (type B, dual carriageway, blue signs) | 110 km/h | **90** with precipitation of any kind. | CdS art. 142(1) |
| Autostrada (type A, green signs) | 130 km/h | **110** with precipitation of any kind. The owner may raise it to **150** on three-lane motorways (plus hard shoulder) with approved average-speed systems, with signs. | CdS art. 142(1) |
| Strada urbana di scorrimento (type D) | 50 km/h | It is an urban road; 70 only if signed. | CdS art. 142(1), art. 2(3) D |
| Strada urbana ciclabile (E-bis) | at most 30 km/h | Defined by signs. | CdS art. 2(3) E-bis |

Conditions:

- **Rain, snow, any precipitation:** 110 on autostrade, 90 on extraurbane principali, without any sign (CdS art. 142(1), last sentence). Certainty high.
- **Novice drivers:** for the first three years after a category A2, A, B1 or B licence: at most 100 on autostrade and 90 on extraurbane principali (CdS art. 117(2)). Certainty high.
- **Fog / visibility below 100 m:** not found in CdS art. 141 or 142 (I expected a 50 km/h motorway rule; it is not in these articles). Art. 141(3) only requires reducing speed in poor visibility. Certainty high that art. 141/142 contain no number; the source of the 50 km/h rule was not found.
- Vehicle-class limits (trailers 70/80, heavy vehicles, buses): CdS art. 142(3). Not relevant for a car without a trailer.
- Within these maxima the road owner may set lower (or minimum) limits on given roads or sections, with signs (CdS art. 142(2)).
- The general limits are shown at the borders on the sign "LIMITI DI VELOCITÀ GENERALI" (fig. II.352, Reg. art. 135(27)): town, outside towns, extraurbane principali, autostrade.

Facts from the law:

- **The town sign itself sets the 50:** "Il segnale di INIZIO CENTRO ABITATO … ha valore anche per segnalare per i centri abitati il limite di velocità … di cui … all'articolo 142, comma 1 … Pertanto non è necessario aggiungere i due segnali di prescrizione di LIMITE DI VELOCITÀ…" (Reg. art. 131(4)). Certainty high.
- Other rules for the whole centro abitato may be shown with the matching sign mounted together with the INIZIO CENTRO ABITATO sign (Reg. art. 131(4)). So a 30 (or other) on the town-sign post is a town-wide limit.
- Centro abitato = a group of at least 25 buildings, delimited along the access roads by the start and end signs (CdS art. 3(1)(8)); the municipality fixes the limits by resolution (CdS art. 4).
- Autostrada: separate carriageways, at least two lanes each, no level crossings, fenced, with start and end signs (CdS art. 2(3) A). Strada extraurbana principale: separate carriageways, at least two lanes each, no level crossings, with start and end signs (CdS art. 2(3) B).
- Background colours of guide signs: green for autostrade and for directions to them, blue for extraurban roads, white for urban roads (Reg. art. 78(2)). The colour of direction signs tells a camera the kind of road the sign points to, not always the road the car is on.

How a camera can tell motorway from expressway: the start sign is the same picture, green for autostrada, blue for extraurbana principale (Reg. art. 135(23)). Certainty high.

## How long a speed sign holds

- **Start:** the limit applies "sul tratto di strada interessato dal segnale" (Reg. art. 116(1)(e)); prescription signs stand at, or as close as possible to, the point where the rule begins; with a distance panel II.1 they may be repeated in advance (Reg. art. 81(8)).
- **Repeat after every intersection:** "Lungo il tratto stradale interessato da una prescrizione i segnali di divieto e di obbligo, nonché quelli di diritto di precedenza, devono essere ripetuti dopo ogni intersezione. Tale obbligo non sussiste per i segnali a validità zonale." (Reg. art. 104(2)). The speed limit sign is a "segnale di divieto" (Reg. art. 116(1)(e)).
- **No end sign needed at an intersection:** "L'installazione [of the end sign] non è necessaria se il divieto o l'obbligo cessa in corrispondenza di una intersezione." (Reg. art. 81(10)).
- **Reading for the code:** a non-zonal limit lasts until the next intersection unless it is repeated after it; a limit that ends at an intersection may have no end sign. Certainty high from the text. Court rulings (Corte di Cassazione) on fines where the sign was not repeated: not searched (web search budget used up). Whether "intersezione" includes grade-separated junctions ("svincolo", CdS art. 3(1)(25),(53)): the article says "ogni intersezione" without distinction. Certainty medium.
- **Repetition:** a sign may be repeated in reduced size with a "continuation" panel II.5/a2 or II.5/b2 (Reg. art. 104(4)).
- **Length:** a panel II.2 gives the length of the section where the rule applies (Reg. art. 83(5)).
- **Zonal signs** (town sign, zone signs) are exempt from repetition and hold until their end sign (Reg. art. 104(2), 131(4), 135(14)).
- **Ramps:** a "rampa" is a road that connects two branches of an intersection (CdS art. 3(1)(43)). No specific speed rule for ramps found. Not found: whether an autostrada ramp counts as autostrada for the 130 (CdS art. 2(3) A does not say whether ramps are part of the autostrada). Certainty low.

## Zones

- **ZONA A VELOCITÀ LIMITATA** (fig. II.323/a): "indica l'inizio di un'area nella quale non è consentito superare la velocità indicata nel cartello"; ends with FINE ZONA A VELOCITÀ LIMITATA (fig. II.323/b) (Reg. art. 135(14)). Holds through intersections (zonal, Reg. art. 104(2)).
- **Zona residenziale** (fig. II.318/319), **area pedonale** (II.320/321), **zona a traffico limitato** (II.322/a, b): special rules shown on panels; no fixed speed in these articles (Reg. art. 135(12)–(14); CdS art. 3(1)(2),(54),(58)).
- **Zona scolastica** and **zona ciclabile**: defined in CdS art. 3(1)(54-bis),(58-bis); speeds not checked.
- **Centro abitato** behaves as a zone for the 50 (Reg. art. 131(4)).

## Variable message signs

- Variable message signs ("a messaggio variabile") may show prescriptions where they can change over time; they must use the regulation figures, shapes, sizes and colours, even if built from points (Reg. art. 77(4), art. 170(1)(a),(2),(3)). So a limit on a variable sign has the same force as a fixed one. Certainty high.
- **Dark (blank) sign:** not found in the CdS or Reg. articles read. Treat as "no information" and keep the last limit. Certainty low on the law; keeping is the safe side.
- Lane-specific signs: a sign above one lane, with a downward arrow panel II.6/n, applies only to that lane (Reg. art. 81(6), 83(10)).
- Specific dynamic-limit systems on Italian motorways (e.g. Brennero A22): not searched.

## Supplementary panels

Reg. art. 83(3): II.1 distance; II.2 length ("estesa"); II.3 time of validity; II.4 limitation to (II.4/a) or exception for ("eccetto", II.4/b) vehicle classes; II.5 start / continuation / end (a1, a2, a3; b1, b2, b3); II.6 explanation symbols (including II.6/i "strada sdrucciolevole per pioggia" and II.6/h ice, II.6/n lane arrow); II.7 course of the main road. Sign and panel may be one composite sign (art. 83(13)). Restriction of a prescription to vehicle classes: Reg. art. 104(6).

## Special cases for a camera system

- **VELOCITÀ CONSIGLIATA** (fig. II.312): advisory speed on a square sign, used on extraurban roads and autostrade, ended by FINE VELOCITÀ CONSIGLIATA (II.313) (Reg. art. 135(8)). Not a limit. Look (blue square): not verified from the figure.
- **LIMITE MINIMO DI VELOCITÀ** (fig. II.85, round mandatory sign): a minimum speed, ended by II.86 (Reg. art. 122(1)(e),(7)). Must not be read as a maximum.
- **Rain:** autostrada 110 and extraurbana principale 90 apply without signs whenever it rains or snows (CdS art. 142(1)). The comma could use the wiper state.
- **150 on autostrade:** lawful only when signed (CdS art. 142(1)); a plausibility filter must allow it.
- **70 in towns:** lawful when signed (CdS art. 142(1)).
- **Town-wide limits** on the town-sign post (Reg. art. 131(4)).
- **Novice drivers:** 100 / 90 (CdS art. 117(2)); a user setting could cap these.

`expressway` must mean strada extraurbana principale (blue start sign), `rural` the extraurbane secondarie and locali (CdS art. 142(1)).

Intersection rule: **yes** for ordinary limit signs, **no** for zonal signs. A non-zonal limit must be repeated after every intersection and needs no end sign where it ends at one (Reg. art. 104(2), 81(10)). The town 50 (Reg. art. 131(4)), the speed zone (Reg. art. 135(14)) and other zonal signs hold until their end signs. Certainty high on the text; case law not checked; whether grade-separated junctions count: medium.

Blank variable sign: not found in the law. Keep the last limit; never raise because of a dark sign.

Other rules the code should know:

1. Rain: 110 / 90 on autostrada / extraurbana principale (CdS art. 142(1)).
2. Advisory and minimum-speed signs are not limits (Reg. art. 135(8), 122(7)).
3. FINE LIMITAZIONE DI VELOCITÀ (II.71) restores the general limit of the road type, which `_general(road)` does.
4. After a junction with no repeated sign, the limit should fall back to the general limit of the road type (the opposite of Austria and Germany).
