# Liechtenstein (LI): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Verkehrsregelnverordnung (VRV, LGBl. 1978 Nr. 19, LR 741.11, version of 01.09.2025) and Strassensignalisationsverordnung (SSV, LGBl. 1980 Nr. 65, LR 741.21, version of 01.09.2025), both from gesetze.li. Liechtenstein's rules copy the Swiss ones almost word for word; the differences that matter are listed below. Read ch.md first.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Built-up area (densely built part of a town) | 50 km/h | Starts at 2.30.1 "50 generell", ends at 2.53.1. Also without a sign for drivers entering from unimportant side roads, once dense building begins. Holds in the whole densely built area. | VRV art. 6(1)(a), 6(2); SSV art. 16(2a), 22(3) |
| Town roads with "30 generell" or "40 generell" | 30 or 40 km/h | Only on municipal roads, or main roads in places without through traffic. Holds from its 2.30.1 to the matching 2.53.1. Liechtenstein-specific. | SSV art. 16(2a), 22(3a), 98(5)(d) |
| Outside built-up areas | 80 km/h | Applies from 2.53.1 or 2.53. | VRV art. 6(1)(b), 6(3) |
| Expressway (Autostrasse) | no separate limit: 80 km/h | The LI VRV has no Autostrasse or Autobahn limit; the word "Autobahn" does not occur in it. Outside towns 80 applies to every road. | VRV art. 6(1) |
| Motorway (Autobahn) | no separate limit: 80 km/h | As above. Not found: any Autobahn or Autostrasse in Liechtenstein. | VRV art. 6(1) |
| Tempo-30 zone / meeting zone / pedestrian zone | 30 / 20 / walking pace | As in CH. | SSV art. 2a, 22a, 22b, 22c |

Conditions:

- All general limits apply "unter günstigen Strassen-, Verkehrs- und Sichtverhältnissen" (VRV art. 6(1)).
- Wet road, fog, night, novice drivers: no fixed limit found in the VRV. Certainty medium (VRV read; licence rules not searched).
- Vehicle-type limits (80 for heavy vehicles, trailer trains, articulated vehicles): VRV art. 7. Not relevant for a car without a trailer.
- Signed limits take precedence (VRV art. 6(4)).
- **Lawful signed values** (SSV art. 98(5)): outside towns only below 80 (10 km/h steps); in towns 60, or below 50 (10 km/h steps); zones 30 / 20; 30 or 40 generell. So no lawful signed value in Liechtenstein is above 80. Certainty high.

## Road kinds and their signs

| Kind | Begins | Ends | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Town limit 50 | 2.30.1 "Höchstgeschwindigkeit 50 generell" | 2.53.1 | As in CH: round 50 with "GENERELL" in the red ring. |
| Town limit 30 / 40 generell | 2.30.1 "Höchstgeschwindigkeit 30 (40) generell" | 2.53.1 "Ende Höchstgeschwindigkeit 30 (40) generell" | Same sign with 30 or 40. Not in the Swiss SSV. |
| Town (place name) | Ortsbeginn, blue (main road) or white (side road) | Ortsende: the name crossed by a red diagonal | Near the border, the next-place field shows the name of the country instead. |
| Autobahn / Autostrasse signs | 4.01 / 4.03 exist in the LI SSV | | Art. 44(1) says only that they "weisen auf die dem Motorfahrzeugverkehr vorbehaltenen Strassen hin" (point to motor-traffic roads). Unlike CH art. 45(1), the LI text does not say they lift earlier restrictions. |

- The town sign must not stand after the 50 sign (SSV art. 49(4)), as in CH.
- The 50 begins and ends with 2.30.1 / 2.53.1, not with the town signs (VRV art. 6(2)); same remarks as in ch.md.
- How a camera can tell motorway from expressway: not applicable inside Liechtenstein; across the Rhine (Swiss A13) Swiss rules apply.

## How long a speed sign holds

- **Same rule as CH:** a 2.30 limit holds until its end sign (2.53 or 2.58), "höchstens aber bis zum Ende der nächsten Verzweigung" (SSV art. 16(2)). To continue after a junction it must be repeated.
- **Junction (Verzweigung):** defined as in CH: crossings, forks and joins of carriageways; garage, car-park, farm and similar exits do not count (VRV art. 1 g)).
- **Exceptions:** 2.30.1 "50 generell" holds in the whole densely built area; "30 generell" and "40 generell" hold until their own 2.53.1 (SSV art. 16(2a)), so through junctions.
- **Case law:** not found for Liechtenstein. Swiss rulings (BGE 128 IV 30, 6S.510/1999) interpret the same wording; they are not binding in Liechtenstein but are the closest guide. Certainty medium.
- **Repetition:** on long sections with panel 5.04 or a length panel 5.03 (SSV art. 16(4)).

## End signs

- 2.53 "Ende der Höchstgeschwindigkeit", 2.53.1 "Ende der Höchstgeschwindigkeit (50/30/40) generell" (SSV art. 22(1), 22(3a)), 2.58 "Freie Fahrt" and zone end signs (SSV art. 32): as in CH.
- After 2.53.1 of "50 generell" or after 2.53: 80 (VRV art. 6(3)).
- After 2.53.1 of "30/40 generell": not stated in the law. Usually the road is still inside the town, so 50 (if 2.30.1 50 is in force) or the next sign. Certainty low.

## Zones

- As in CH: zone signals only innerorts (SSV art. 2a); 30-zone (art. 22a), meeting zone 20 (art. 22b), pedestrian zone (art. 22c). The zone holds until its end sign.

## Variable message signs

- Matrix signs may have a black background with white symbols (SSV art. 16(1)).
- Not found: any provision on Wechselsignalanlagen (variable sign systems) in the LI SSV. No motorway traffic-control system found in Liechtenstein.
- A dark panel is not an end sign (no provision makes it one). Certainty medium.

## Supplementary panels

- As in CH: 5.01 distance, 5.03 length, 5.04 repetition (SSV art. 16(3),(4)); vehicle-class panels restrict a sign to that class. Not checked article by article against the Swiss list.

## Special cases for a camera system

- **30 generell / 40 generell:** a classifier trained on Swiss data may read the 30 or 40 but treat it as a plain 2.30, which ends at the next junction. In Liechtenstein it holds until its own end sign. A car on such a road keeps the limit through junctions.
- **Border:** Liechtenstein borders Switzerland (Rhine, A13 on the Swiss side) and Austria. The country must come from the position, not from signs. Austrian rules differ (100 outside towns, junction rule different, see at.md).
- **Readings above 80** in Liechtenstein are not lawful signed values (SSV art. 98(5)); they are misreads or belong to a Swiss road.
