# Hungary (HU): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: KRESZ, 1/1975. (II. 5.) KPM–BM együttes rendelet a közúti közlekedés szabályairól (consolidated text in force since 2026-04-11, no end date) [S1]; 20/1984. (XII. 21.) KM rendelet with its annex, the Forgalomszabályozási Műszaki Szabályzat (FMSZ, rules for authorities on how to regulate traffic) [S3]; 83/2004. (VI. 4.) GKM rendelet on designing and placing road signs [S4].

All sources were checked on 2026-10-29. Section numbers (§) are KRESZ unless another act is named. Hungarian law names a sign by its KRESZ figure number ("30. ábra" = figure 30). The technical code (letter plus three digits, e.g. C-033) is the number in the road technical specification (útügyi műszaki előírás) [S4 point 1.2]. Wikipedia/Commons give the code-to-sign mapping [S5] (certainty medium).

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

KRESZ 26. § (1) a) covers passenger cars, motorcycles and other cars up to 3,500 kg [S1].

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions (wet, night, visibility, novice drivers, other) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| In a built-up area (lakott terület) | 50 km/h | None by weather or time. No novice-driver limit (26. § lists none). | 26. § (1) a) ad) [S1] |
| Outside built-up areas, other roads | 90 km/h | Same as above. No separate class for dual carriageways or 2+2 lanes. | 26. § (1) a) ac) [S1] |
| Autóút (motor road, the expressway type) | 110 km/h | Same as above. | 26. § (1) a) ab) [S1] |
| Autópálya (motorway) | 130 km/h | Same as above. | 26. § (1) a) aa) [S1] |
| Lakó-pihenő övezet (residential zone) | 20 km/h | All vehicles. | 39/A. § (2) [S1] |
| Road section after a "Gyermekek" (children) warning sign with a length panel | 30 km/h | Only on the marked length, and only in the time period if a panel gives one, "unless the road signs say otherwise". | 26. § (6) [S1] |
| Car with snow chains | 50 km/h | Only while chains are fitted. | 26. § (1) d/1. [S1] |
| Car (or other vehicle) towing a trailer | 80 motorway / 70 outside / 50 town | 26. § (1) b): "other motor vehicles, and motor vehicle plus trailer". | 26. § (1) b) [S1] |

- **Signs may also raise the limit.** If a speed sign shows more than 50 in a built-up area, or more than 90 outside, a passenger car may drive up to the signed speed (26. § (2)) [S1]. Authorities may raise a built-up-area limit only up to 80 (FMSZ 30.2) [S3]. Certainty: high.
- **No wet, night or visibility limits exist.** "Night" is defined (Annex 1, III g) but carries no speed rule. Lower limits for wet roads or snow come only from a speed sign with a weather panel (see Supplementary panels) [S1]. Certainty: high.
- **Draft new KRESZ.** A draft published on 2026-01-20 would allow up to 140 on some motorway sections and 120 on autóút [S6]. In the latest news found (May 2026) it was still a draft [S7]. The text in force on 2026-10-29 still has 130/110 [S1]. Certainty that it is not in force: medium-high.

Definitions:

- A built-up area is only "the area between the signs marking its start and end" (Annex 1, III f) [S1]. It has no implicit start at buildings.
- An autópálya is a divided road signed as autópálya (Annex 1, I l). An autóút is a road signed as autóút (I m) [S1].
- **An autóút may have at-grade intersections.** FMSZ 2.2 a) requires turning lanes "at its at-grade crossing with another road" [S3]. FMSZ 2.15 speaks of an "osztottpályás autóút" (dual-carriageway motor road), which implies single-carriageway ones also exist [S3]. Certainty: high for at-grade junctions, medium for single carriageways.
- **An autópálya has no at-grade crossings.** It has at least two lanes per direction, a hard shoulder, and acceleration and deceleration lanes (FMSZ 2.1) [S3].

**Where the signs stand** (83/2004 GKM) [S4]:

- **Autópálya:** at the start of the entry ramps at interchanges, "so that whoever may not enter can still choose another road" (18.13 b). So the entry ramp already counts as motorway. On a road that turns into a motorway, the sign is pre-announced before the last junction (18.13 c).
- **Autópálya vége:** on exit ramps, before the first junction (18.14 c). At the real end of a motorway it is pre-announced twice, at 500 and 1,000 m (18.14 b). If the motorway continues as an autóút, the "Autópálya vége" sign is placed under the "Autóút" sign (18.14 b).
- **Autóút:** the same rules as for motorways, plus a repeat after every at-grade intersection on the autóút (18.15 aa).
- **Lakott terület kezdete:**
  - The sign with a name (131/a, 131/b) stands where the road first reaches the built-up area.
  - 131/b, 131/c, 132/b and 132/c are used when the built-up rules stop and start again inside the same municipality (FMSZ 10.1) [S3].
  - Where two settlements have grown together, an entry sign with the next settlement's name stands at the border, with no exit sign for the one left behind (83/2004 18.2 b) [S4].

**How a camera tells motorway from autóút** (certainty high for the pictograms, medium for the rest):

- The start signs differ only by pictogram: overpass and divided road for the motorway, car front for the autóút. Both are blue squares.
- Both use blue direction signs. KRESZ 17. § (1) v/1 and y/1 define the blue 139/a and 145/a signs "on motorways and motor roads" [S1][S2]. Route numbers starting with "M" appear on both: the M0 at km 65 is called an autóút [S11]. Neither the colour nor the "M" number tells them apart.
- An at-grade junction, or a repeated "Autóút" sign after one, means autóút.

## How long a speed sign holds

**Rule: until the next intersection.** KRESZ 14. § (6) [S1]:

> "Az (1) bekezdés c)–g) pontjában említett jelzőtábla hatálya a táblánál kezdődik és a következő útkereszteződés kezdetéig tart, kivéve, ha a jelzőtábla alatt elhelyezett kiegészítő tábla ennél rövidebb távolságot jelöl meg, vagy a (7) bekezdésben említett jelzőtábla a tilalmat előbb feloldja."

That is: the sign takes effect at the sign and holds until the start of the next intersection, unless a panel gives a shorter distance or an end sign (56 or 59) lifts it earlier. Point d) is the "Sebességkorlátozás" sign (30. ábra, C-033: red ring, black number on white). Certainty: high.

Exceptions and extensions:

1. **Railway crossing.** A speed sign placed with a level-crossing warning sign (90, 91, 92 or 95. ábra) holds only up to the start of the crossing (14. § (6), second sentence) [S1]. Certainty: high.
2. **Town-wide limit.** If the speed sign is placed together with the "Lakott terület kezdete" sign, it holds for the whole built-up area (14. § (8)) [S1]. Intersections do not end it; the built-up-area end sign does. Authorities must put it on every paved road into the area, possibly as one combined sign (83/2004 14.7) [S4], and FMSZ 10.2.1.2 confirms this [S3]. Certainty: high.
3. **Zone.** The "Korlátozott sebességű övezet" sign holds until the "Korlátozott sebességű övezet vége" sign (14. § (1) z/1) [S1]. Intersections do not end it. Certainty: high.
4. **Shorter distance.** A panel can give a length shorter than the distance to the junction (14. § (6)) [S1].

**What counts as an intersection.** "Útkereszteződés: két vagy több útnak azonos szintben való kereszteződése, egymásba torkollása, illetőleg elágazása" (Annex 1, I k) [S1]: two or more roads crossing, joining or branching at the same level. "Út" includes public roads and private roads not closed to public traffic (Annex 1, I a). A "földút" (unpaved or dirt road) is a road (Annex 1, I sz) [S1].

- A junction with a dirt road or with a public private road ends the limit. Certainty: medium; this is a reading of the definitions, with no court ruling found.
- A driveway into a property closed to the public is not a road, so it does not end the limit. Certainty: medium.
- A grade-separated crossing is not an intersection. Certainty: high.

**Repeats after junctions.** Authorities must repeat a speed sign "after every intersection". Outside built-up areas, when the limit is longer than 1,000 m, they must also repeat it at least every 1,000 m. When a limit ends somewhere other than at a junction or road connection, they must place an end sign (83/2004 GKM 13.4) [S4]. Certainty: high.

**Road-kind signs do not end a speed sign.** 14. § (7) names only 56. ábra and 59. ábra as lifting a speed sign [S1]. So "Autópálya", "Autóút", "Lakott terület kezdete/vége" or "Főútvonal" signs do not end it; only the next junction or an end sign does. A KRESZ blog reads it the same way [S8]. Certainty: medium-high; this is a literal reading, with no official guidance found. In practice a junction usually comes first.

**Ramps and slip roads:**

- **The entry ramp is motorway (or autóút) road** from the "Autópálya"/"Autóút" sign at its start (83/2004 18.13 b) [S4]. Unless a sign says otherwise, the general limit there is 130 (110). Certainty: medium-high.
- **A limit signed on a ramp ends where the ramp joins the main carriageway.** That join is a "torkollás" (joining) at the same level, so it is an intersection. A driving-instructor article treats entry ramps, exit ramps and rest-area connections as junctions that end a motorway limit [S12]. Forum answers agree, but cite no law [S10]. Certainty: medium.
- **A limit on the main carriageway does not carry over to traffic entering from a ramp.** For the entering driver the sign was never passed. For through traffic it ends at the junction too, by the same reading. Authorities must repeat it after the junction if it continues (83/2004 13.4) [S4]. Certainty: medium.
- **Exit ramp:** the "Autópálya vége" sign stands on the exit ramp before the first junction [S4]. A limit signed on the exit ramp holds until that junction.
- **Motorway interchanges:** no official interpretation and no court ruling found on whether a ramp diverge or merge counts as an "útkereszteződés" (see Open points).

**Lane signs.** A speed sign applies to the whole carriageway. Where there are two or more marked lanes, a sign above a lane applies only to that lane (10. § (4)) [S1][S9].

**Case law:** no Kúria (supreme court) or Constitutional Court ruling found on how far a speed sign holds.

Notes on speed zones:

- **They are rules for authorities, not for drivers.** A speed zone should have only equal-priority junctions or roundabouts, and no priority road (FMSZ 10.2.1.3) [S3]. Seasonal speed zones exist for event grounds and holiday areas (FMSZ 10.2.1.4) [S3].
- **A lower local limit is allowed inside a zone or a town-wide limit** (FMSZ 30.3) [S3]. Certainty: high.

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status.** KRESZ treats a light-emitting sign as a normal road sign. On such a sign "the white and black parts may appear with colours swapped" (10. § (8)) [S1]. So an LED speed sign with white digits on black inside a red ring is a binding speed limit. No separate provision on variable signs was found. Certainty: high that they bind, medium that the 14. § (6) scope (next intersection) applies to them as written.
- **Gantries and lanes:**
  - Where there are two or more marked lanes, a sign above a lane applies only to vehicles in that lane (10. § (4)) [S1].
  - Signs meant only for one lane must be mounted above that lane (83/2004 5.3) [S4].
  - Signs may be repeated above the carriageway or on the left (10. § (3); 83/2004 5.5) [S1][S4].
- **Lane-control lights** (9. § (1) e), (8)) [S1]:
  - Three lamps above each lane: red X, flashing yellow diagonal arrow, green down arrow. They close or open lanes and carry no speed value.
  - On motorways and divided autóút they may be used to close lanes (FMSZ 2.15) [S3].
- **Advisory, not binding:**
  - "Ajánlott sebesség" (recommended speed, G-003 [S5]) may for now be shown only as a variable matrix of light points (83/2004 22.2 b) [S4]. Its exact look was not verified.
  - A black square with a white number before traffic lights (160. ábra) is the advisory speed for a green wave (20. § (5)) [S1][S2]. It is not a limit.
- **Blank or dark panel:** no provision found in KRESZ, FMSZ or 83/2004 [S1][S3][S4]. A dark panel is not an end sign. Certainty: medium; this rests on the exhaustive list of end signs in 14. § (7). Read literally, a limit shown earlier holds until the next junction or an end sign.

**Panel rules:**

- Normally only one panel goes under a sign. If several are stacked, they are read top to bottom (83/2004 24.1, 24.4) [S4].
- A panel never stands alone (83/2004 24.2) [S4].

**Exit-only panels:** no exit-only panel for speed signs was found in KRESZ. On ramps the sign simply stands on the ramp.

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **A speed sign on the same post as a town-entry sign, or a combined town-plus-speed sign, is a town-wide limit.** It holds until "Lakott terület vége", not to the next junction (14. § (8); 83/2004 14.7) [S1][S4].
2. **A speed pictogram inside the arrow of a direction pre-sign is not for the current road.** On the 138–139. ábra pre-signs, a prohibition sign drawn into the arrow announces the limit on the road after the junction (17. § (1) v)) [S1].
3. **A town-entry sign seen while already in a town keeps "town".** Where two settlements have grown together there is no exit sign between them (83/2004 18.2 b) [S4].
4. **The "Helynévtábla" (133. ábra) is not a built-up area sign.** It is a green plate with a white name of a county, river, etc. (17. § (1) u)) [S1][S2]. Only the white 131 plate and the red-bordered 131/a–c plates start a built-up area.
5. **Advisory numbers:**
   - Black square with a white number before traffic lights (160. ábra): green-wave advice, not a limit (20. § (5)) [S1].
   - Recommended-speed VMS (G-003): advisory [S4].
   - Only a red-ring roundel is a limit.
6. **LED signs may invert colours:** white on black inside a red ring (10. § (8)) [S1]. Signs may also sit on a yellow backing board to be seen better, with no change in meaning (10. § (9)) [S1].
7. **A "Gyermekek" warning triangle (84. ábra) with a length panel (97. ábra) means 30 km/h on that length** (26. § (6)) [S1]. No number is shown on any sign. It applies only "if the road signs do not say otherwise" and possibly only in a time period.
8. **A signed limit can be higher than the general one,** e.g. 60–80 in town or 100–110 on rural roads. The sign wins (26. § (2)) [S1].
9. **A speed sign together with a level-crossing warning ends at the crossing** (14. § (6)) [S1].
10. **An autóút can have at-grade junctions.** A speed limit ends at each one, and the "Autóút" sign is repeated after each one (83/2004 18.15 aa) [S4]. That repeat is a good cue to re-assert "expressway".
11. **The motorway sign stands at the start of the entry ramp** (83/2004 18.13 b) [S4]. A camera that sees it is already on motorway road. "Autópálya vége" on an exit ramp precedes the first junction.
12. **The "Sebességkorlátozás vége" sign (56. ábra) shows the old number in grey under a black bar.** The classifier must not read it as a new limit.
13. **Unpaved side roads also end a limit** (Annex 1, I sz; certainty medium). The map may not show them. A sign repeated after the junction (required by 83/2004 13.4) restores the limit.

- Optional zone values the code could use: lakó-pihenő övezet = 20, from the 122/123 signs (39/A. § (2)). The speed zone (53/a) shows its own number.
- Autópálya/autóút inside a built-up area: 26. § (1) a) lists autópálya/autóút before "lakott terület" and gives no rule for overlap. Motorways normally carry no town signs, so the road kind should win. Certainty: medium.

**ROAD_SIGNS for HU:**

- E-016 → motorway, E-017 → rural (but expressway when posted with E-018), E-018 → expressway, E-019 → rural.
- 131–131/c → town, 132–132/c → rural.
- Zone 53/a → the number on it, 53/b → town general.
- 122 → 20, 123 → town general.

**The intersection rule: yes.** A speed sign ends at the start of the next intersection (KRESZ 14. § (6)):

- **Junctions that count:** junctions with dirt roads and public private roads (Annex 1, I a, k, sz). Property driveways do not count (certainty medium).
- **Exceptions, where the sign does not end at the junction:**
  - a speed sign placed with a town-entry sign: until the town exit (14. § (8));
  - a zone sign: until the zone end (14. § (1) z/1);
  - a shorter length on a panel (14. § (6));
  - a level-crossing combination, which ends at the crossing (14. § (6)).
- **Gap in the code:** the current code ends a sign only when the map's limit changes (the CH logic with MAP_SECONDS). In Hungary the sign legally ends at any junction, even one where the map's limit stays the same. Using map junction nodes, falling back to the general limit of the road kind, would be closer to the law. Authorities must repeat the sign after each junction where the limit continues (83/2004 13.4).
- **Motorway interchanges:** the ramp merge and diverge most likely count as junctions (certainty medium; see Open points). The current ramp logic matches this: a ramp limit ends at the merge.

**A blank variable sign** is not an end sign; no legal provision was found. Keep the limit already held until the next junction or an end sign. Certainty: medium.

**Other rules the code should know:**

- Rain panel (97/b): the limit applies in rain or on a wet surface. Snow panel (97/a): only while it is snowing.
- Time and vehicle-class panels limit the sign.
- No night or novice limits exist.
- Signs may raise limits above the general one (26. § (2)).
- A car with a trailer has 80/70/50 (26. § (1) b)).
- The draft new KRESZ (140 on some motorway sections, 120 on autóút) was not in force on 2026-10-29. Re-check before release.

## Open points

1. **Motorway interchanges.** Does a ramp diverge or merge count as an "útkereszteződés" that ends a motorway speed sign? The definition covers joining and branching "at the same level". A practitioner says yes [S12] and forums agree [S10]. No official guidance or court ruling was found. Certainty: medium.
2. **Dirt roads and driveways.** A junction with a dirt road ending a limit, and a property driveway not ending it, is a reading of the Annex 1 definitions. No Kúria ruling or police guidance was found. Certainty: medium.
3. **Blank or dark variable sign.** No provision found; treated as "not an end sign". Certainty: medium.
4. **Draft new KRESZ.** The 2026-01-20 draft has 140 on some motorway sections and 120 on autóút. As of the latest news found (May 2026) it was not adopted, and the consolidated KRESZ (from 2026-04-11) has no end date. Re-check magyarkozlony.hu before release.
5. **Autópálya/autóút inside a built-up area.** 26. § (1) a) does not say whether the road kind or the built-up area wins. Assumed: road kind. Certainty: medium.
6. **End signs inside a town-wide limit or zone.** After a "Sebességkorlátozás vége" (56) or a junction inside a town-wide limit (14. § (8)), the town-wide value presumably returns, not 50. Not found explicitly. Certainty: low-medium.
7. **Sign codes.** The exact mapping of E-020…E-025 to 131/131/a–c (and 132 variants) and the look of the G-003 recommended-speed sign were not verified against the technical specifications ÚT 2-1.127 / 2-1.129 / 2-1.130, which are not freely available.
8. **Distance panel under a speed sign** (96. ábra style, "100 m"): its meaning as "applies after X m" is defined only for warning signs (16. § (2)). For speed signs it is an inference from 83/2004 5.8. Certainty: medium.
9. **Case law:** no Kúria or Constitutional Court ruling found on the reach of speed signs.
