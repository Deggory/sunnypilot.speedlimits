# Austria (AT): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Straßenverkehrsordnung 1960 (StVO 1960, consolidated version of 29.10.2026 in RIS) §§ 2, 20, 43, 44, 44a–44d, 46–48, 51–54, 76a–76c; Immissionsschutzgesetz-Luft (IG-L, version of 29.10.2026) § 14.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ortsgebiet (built-up area) | 50 km/h | The road network between the town sign "Ortstafel" (§53 Z 17a) and "Ortsende" (§53 Z 17b). The authority may set a lower limit for a whole Ortsgebiet (e.g. 30), signed together with the Ortstafel. | StVO §20(2), §20(2a), §2(1) Z 15, §44(4) |
| Freilandstraße (outside built-up areas) | 100 km/h | Every road outside an Ortsgebiet that is not an Autobahn. | StVO §20(2), §2(1) Z 16 |
| Autostraße (expressway, §53 Z 8c) | 100 km/h | No separate limit in §20(2): an Autostraße outside an Ortsgebiet is a Freilandstraße. Certainty high. | StVO §20(2), §47 |
| Autobahn (§53 Z 8a) | 130 km/h | Includes the entry and exit roads, which are declared part of the Autobahn. | StVO §20(2), §43(3)(a) |
| Wohnstraße | walking speed | §53 Z 9c to 9d. | StVO §76b(3) |
| Begegnungszone | 20 km/h (30 if so ordered; the sign then shows 30) | §53 Z 9e to 9f. | StVO §76c(2), §53 Z 9e |
| Fußgängerzone | walking speed (where driving is allowed) | §53 Z 9a to 9b. | StVO §76a(6) |
| Fahrradstraße | 30 km/h | | StVO §67(3) |

- The authority may lower the limits (by sign, §43(1)) or **raise** them (§43(4): "die gemäß § 20 Abs. 2 erlaubten Höchstgeschwindigkeiten erhöhen"). So a signed value above the general limit can be lawful in Austria. Certainty high.
- Weather and condition limits: §20(1) only requires speed adapted to conditions; no fixed rain or fog number found in the StVO. Limits for wet roads or snow are signed with panels (§54(5)(f),(g)). Certainty high for the StVO.
- Novice drivers ("Probeführerschein"): no speed limit found in the StVO. The driving-licence law (FSG) and the vehicle law (KFG, limits for trailers and heavy vehicles) were not searched. Certainty low.
- Ministerial orders for heavy traffic periods (§20(3)) and IG-L air-quality limits (see below) can lower limits on whole sections.

Facts from the law:

- **The town sign sets the 50 in Austria** (Ortsgebiet = the network inside the Ortstafel, §2(1) Z 15). Certainty high.
- The Ortstafel stands at the start of the built-up area ("Beginn des verbauten Gebietes"). It must not stand on Autobahnen, except at the end of an exit road (§53 Z 17a).
- Autobahn: declared by order; roads without level crossings, suitable for fast traffic, with special junctions, including their entry and exit roads (§43(3)(a)). Autostraße: roads suitable for fast traffic that do not meet those conditions (§43(3)(b)); they are priority roads, and part of the Autobahn rules apply (§47).
- Entry and exit only at the signed entry and exit roads (§46(2)).
- These signs are a valid way to publish speed orders (§44(1)): passing a Z 8a, 8b, 8c, 8d, 17a or 17b changes the general limit.

How a camera can tell Autobahn from Autostraße: the symbol on the sign (motorway with overbridge vs. car). Not found: other reliable features in the law (both may have two carriageways). Many "Schnellstraßen" (S roads) are declared Autobahnen; their signs show which. Certainty medium.

Where signs stand:

- Signs stand on the right or above the carriageway; on Autobahnen, prescription signs stand on both sides or above, except where the direction has only one lane (§48(2)).
- Prescription signs stand before the place where they apply (§51(1)); a distance panel (§54(5)(a)) announces a rule ahead.
- Signs may be on vehicles of the road service during works; they apply only to the work area and no end sign is shown (§48(3)).

## How long a speed sign holds

- **§52 Z 10a:** the limit applies "ab dem Standort des Zeichens" (from the sign on).
- **End:** "Gilt die Vorschrift für eine längere Straßenstrecke, so ist das Ende der Strecke durch ein gleiches Zeichen, unter dem eine Zusatztafel mit der Aufschrift „ENDE" anzubringen ist, kenntlich zu machen" (§51(1)). The end sign §52 Z 10b "ist nach jedem Zeichen gemäß Z 10a anzubringen"; it may be left out only where a new limit begins, "sei es auch nicht aufgrund dieses Bundesgesetzes" (e.g. an IG-L limit) (§52 Z 10b). A length panel (§54(5)(b)) gives the length of the section.
- **Junctions:** nothing in the StVO ends a limit at a junction. §51(1) asks for repetition within the section only "wenn es die Verkehrssicherheit erfordert". §51(5) lets the authority show the limit already on a side road that joins the limited section (sign with arrow panel), which presumes the limit continues past the junction. Certainty high from the text; VwGH case law was not searched (web search budget used up).
- **Repetition and length:** for an overtaking ban or a limit over 1 km, the length is given on a panel when safety requires it, also on repeat signs (§51(1)).
- **Ramps:** entry and exit roads belong to the Autobahn (§43(3)(a)), so 130 applies on them unless signed. A limit signed on a ramp holds until its end sign or a new limit (§52 Z 10b). Not found: whether practice places a 10b at the merge. Certainty medium.
- **Interchanges:** no special rule found.
- **Town-wide limits:** a limit ordered for a whole Ortsgebiet is published by the sign "in unmittelbarer Verbindung mit dem Hinweiszeichen Ortstafel" (§44(4), §51(4)). It holds in the whole Ortsgebiet, through junctions, until Ortsende. Certainty high.

## Zones

- §52 Z 11a "Zonenbeschränkung": the restriction shown inside the sign applies in the whole zone; Z 11b ends it (may be on the back of the opposite sign). Certainty high.
- Wohnstraße (walking speed), Begegnungszone (20 or 30), Fußgängerzone (walking speed): §53 Z 9a–9f, §76a–76c.
- Town-wide limits with the Ortstafel (§44(4)) behave like a zone that ends at Ortsende.

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status:** signs may be shown on optical (fibre) or electronic displays, also with inverted colours (black background, white symbols) (§48(1a)). Orders published by a traffic-control system that switches signs on for the duration of conditions such as rain, snow or heavy traffic are valid; the system must log content, time and duration (§44(1a)).
- **§44c Verkehrsbeeinflussung:** for conditions that cannot be foreseen, the authority orders the measures in advance; they are published only by the traffic-control system, and "der örtliche und zeitliche Umfang … wird … durch die Anzeige der betreffenden Straßenverkehrszeichen … bestimmt" (§44c(3)). The same wording applies to IG-L limits on Autobahnen and Schnellstraßen (IG-L §14(6c)).
- **Lane-specific limits:** a panel (§54(5)(k)) under a sign above a lane means that the sign applies only to that lane; §54(5)(l) for signs on a traffic island (left lane only).
- **Dark (blank) panel:** not found as an end sign in the StVO. Two readings are possible: (a) §52 Z 10b requires an end sign after every limit, so a dark panel does not end it; (b) under §44c(3) and IG-L §14(6c) the extent of a system limit is set by where it is displayed, which could mean it ends where the displays stop showing it. No VwGH ruling was checked. Certainty low. For the code: treat dark as "no information", keep the limit (safe side).
- **IG-L limits (air quality):** signs carry a panel "Immissionsschutzgesetz-Luft" or "IG-L"; on traffic-control systems the panel may be elsewhere in the cross-section (IG-L §14(6)). **They do not apply to battery-electric and hydrogen fuel-cell vehicles with the green plate marking (KFG §49(4) Z 5) on Autobahnen and Schnellstraßen, where signs say so** (IG-L §14(2a) Z 2). Certainty high.

## Supplementary panels

Panels are traffic signs; rectangular and white (§54(3)). Those relevant to speed (§54(5)):

- (a) distance to where the sign applies;
- (b) length of the section;
- (f) applies with snow or ice on the road;
- (g) applies on a wet road;
- (k) applies only to the lane below;
- (l) applies only to the lane left of the island.

Vehicle-class, time and IG-L panels also exist (§54(1), IG-L §14(6)). Panel content may instead be written in the red ring of the sign (§51(3)).

## Special cases for a camera system

- **IG-L panel:** a limit with "IG-L" does not bind a battery-electric car with green-marked plates on Autobahnen and Schnellstraßen, where signed (IG-L §14(2a) Z 2). The code needs to know whether the car is electric.
- **Snow / wet panels:** conditional limits (§54(5)(f),(g)).
- **Town-wide 30 (or other) signed together with the Ortstafel:** holds through the whole town until Ortsende (§44(4)).
- **Raised limits:** values above 100 on Freilandstraßen or above 130 on Autobahnen are possible by order (§43(4)), so a plausibility filter must not reject them outright.
- **Signs on road-service vehicles** apply only to the work area (§48(3)).
- **Liechtenstein, Switzerland, Germany, Italy, Slovenia, Hungary, Slovakia, Czechia** border Austria; the country must come from the position.

Intersection rule: **no.** A Z 10a limit holds until Z 10b, a sign with "ENDE", Z 11, a new limit, or the end of a length panel (§51(1), §52 Z 10a, 10b). Junctions do not end it (no provision; §51(5) presumes it continues). Source certainty high; case law not checked. The MAP_SECONDS rule fits a turn into another road, but must not end a limit when the car stays on the same road.

Blank variable sign: **not an end sign by any provision**, but §44c(3) leaves room for doubt (see above). Keep the last limit; never raise because of a dark panel. Certainty low on the law, high that keeping is the safe side.

Other rules the code should know:

1. `town_entry → 50` and `town_exit → 100` match Austrian law (§2(1) Z 15, §20(2)).
2. A number on the same post as the Ortstafel is a town-wide limit (§44(4)); it should hold until Ortsende, not end at the next junction.
3. IG-L limits and EVs (above).
4. A limit on an Autobahn ramp: ramps are part of the Autobahn (§43(3)(a)).
