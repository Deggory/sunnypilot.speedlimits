# Portugal (PT): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Código da Estrada (CE, Decreto-Lei 114/94, consolidated text after Lei 24/2025) and
Regulamento de Sinalização do Trânsito (RST, Decreto Regulamentar 22-A/98, last amended and republished by Decreto
Regulamentar 6/2019 of 22 October 2019, in force April 2020).

Every source below was read on 2026-10-29. Links point to the text that was read. Certainty marks apply to
interpretations only: **high** means the text says it, **medium** means it follows from the text but is not stated in
those words, **low** means it is a guess. "Not found" means it could not be verified.

Note on sources: the official consolidated texts on diariodarepublica.pt render only with JavaScript and could not be
read. The CE was read on the Procuradoria-Geral Distrital de Lisboa site ([PGDL CE][ce]). The pre-2019 RST was read as a
PDF of DR 22-A/98 "alterado pelo DR 41/2002" ([RST pre-2019][rst98]). The 2019 amendments were read in the amending text of
DR 6/2019 ([informador DR 6/2019][dr6]; mirror [segurancarodoviaria.pt][dr6m]).

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

Source: CE art. 27 n.º 1, table for "automóveis ligeiros de passageiros e mistos" without trailer ([CE art. 27][ce27]).

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inside a locality (localidade, between N1 and N2 signs) | 50 km/h | none | [CE art. 27][ce27]; [CE art. 1 j)][ce] |
| Zona de coexistência (residential / shared zone, H46 sign) | 20 km/h | inside localities only | [CE art. 27][ce27]; [RST art. 35 n.º 2 (2019)][dr6] |
| Outside localities ("restantes vias públicas") | 90 km/h | none | [CE art. 27][ce27] |
| Via reservada a automóveis e motociclos (H25 sign; the expressway kind) | 100 km/h | none | [CE art. 27][ce27]; [CE art. 1 z)][ce] |
| Autoestrada (motorway, H24 sign) | 120 km/h; minimum 50 km/h | none | [CE art. 27 n.º 1 and n.º 6][ce27] |

Conditions:

- **Wet roads, rain, snow, night or fog:** no general limit of its own was found in CE art. 27. CE art. 24 and
  art. 25 require a speed adapted to the conditions, with no number ([CE][ce]). Certainty high that art. 27 holds no
  weather figure. A weather-dependent general limit was not found anywhere else.
- **Novice drivers:** CE art. 122 (probationary regime, first 3 years) holds no speed restriction ([CE art. 122][ce122]).
  No novice speed limit was found in the current CE. A 90 km/h limit for new drivers existed historically (Decreto
  424/70, [DRE][d424]); when it was abolished was not found. No mandatory novice plate exists either
  ([Observador fact-check][obs]). Certainty medium that there is no novice limit today.
- **Other vehicles,** from the same table ([CE art. 27][ce27]), as coexistence zone / locality / autoestrada / via
  reservada / other roads:
  - car with trailer: 20 / 50 / 100 / 80 / 70;
  - light goods vehicle: 20 / 50 / 110 / 90 / 80;
  - motorcycle over 50 cm³: 20 / 50 / 120 / 100 / 90;
  - heavy passenger vehicle: 20 / 50 / 100 / 90 / 80;
  - heavy goods vehicle: 20 / 50 / 90 / 80 / 80.
- **Special limits:** CE art. 28 lets the authorities set lower or higher maxima and minima on given roads, sections or
  periods. They must be signed, or published by other means when temporary and signing is impossible ([CE][ce]).
- EU summary for cross-checking: 50 / 90 / 120, and 100 on "vias reservadas" ([Your Europe, Portugal][eu]).

## Road kinds and their signs

- **Localidade (built-up area).** Defined as a zone with buildings whose limits are signed (CE art. 1 j), [CE][ce]).
  - Signs: N1a/N1b "início de localidade" and N2a/N2b "fim de localidade" (RST art. 42, as amended, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
  - From where these signs stand, the rules for traffic inside or outside localities apply ([DR 6/2019 art. 42][dr6];
    certainty high).
  - So a town sign alone sets 50 km/h, and its end sets 90 km/h.
- **Autoestrada (motorway).** H24 starts it and H38 ends it (RST art. 34, [RST pre-2019][rst98]).
  - Definition, CE art. 1 a): fast traffic, physically separated carriageways, no level crossings, controlled access,
    "and signed as such" ([CE][ce]).
  - Signed in blue: direction signs on autoestradas have a blue background (RST art. 18 n.º 4, [RST pre-2019][rst98]).
- **Via reservada a automóveis e motociclos (expressway kind).** H25 starts it and H39 ends it (RST art. 34,
  [RST pre-2019][rst98]; H25 confirmed in [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
  - Motorway traffic rules apply on it (CE art. 75 and art. 1 z), [CE][ce]).
- **Itinerário principal (IP) and itinerário complementar (IC).** These are network categories, not a speed category.
  - IP direction signs are green (RST art. 18 n.º 4, [RST pre-2019][rst98]).
  - Without H24 or H25 they are ordinary "restantes vias públicas" at 90 km/h. Certainty medium: art. 27 knows only the
    kinds in the table.
- **Advance notices.** H24, H25, H38 and H39 with a distance panel (model 1) are advance notices, not the start or end
  itself (RST art. 35 n.º 9, as amended, [DR 6/2019][dr6]; certainty high).
- **Zones** (inside localities only): zona de coexistência H46/H47, zona 30 G4a/G10, and zone of limited speed G4/G8.
  See [Zones](#zones).
- **General-limits panel.** H28 "limites de velocidade" shows the general limits, typically at the border (RST art. 34,
  [RST pre-2019][rst98]).

## How long a speed sign holds

- **Meaning of C13.** C13 is "proibição de exceder a velocidade máxima de ... km/h" (RST art. 24, [RST pre-2019][rst98];
  unchanged in 2019, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Where it starts.** Prohibition signs stand in the immediate vicinity of the point where the prohibition begins
  (RST art. 25 n.º 1, [RST pre-2019][rst98]; art. 25 not amended in 2019). Certainty high.
- **Until April 2020 the sign ended at the next junction.** RST art. 12 n.º 5 read: "Salvo o disposto no n.º 4 do
  artigo 14.º, os sinais de regulamentação são válidos até à intersecção de nível mais próxima" ([RST pre-2019][rst98]).
  - An "intersecção de nível" covers roundabouts, crossroads and T-junctions at the same level (RST art. 4 a), as
    amended, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
  - Grade-separated junctions ("intersecção desnivelada", art. 4 b)) are not level intersections.
- **That rule is now revoked.** DR 6/2019 rewrites art. 12 and marks n.º 5 "(Revogado.)" ([DR 6/2019][dr6];
  [mirror][dr6m]). Its own repeal clause (art. 7) does not list art. 12 n.º 5, but the amended wording of art. 12 is
  explicit. Certainty medium-high that no text now says "valid until the next intersection".
- **Repetition is still required.** RST art. 14 n.º 2 (as amended): "Os sinais de perigo e de regulamentação, bem como
  o sinal H1a, devem ser repetidos depois de cada intersecção de nível, quando as condições se mantenham"
  ([DR 6/2019][dr6]). A limit that continues past a level junction must be signed again after it.
- **Exceptions to repetition,** RST art. 14 n.º 4 ([RST pre-2019][rst98]; a) and b) unchanged in 2019, c) rewritten,
  [DR 6/2019][dr6]):
  - a) signs inside a zone sign apply to all roads of the zone;
  - b) regulatory signs on the same support as the town-name sign (N1) apply to all roads of the locality, unless other
    signs inside it say otherwise;
  - c) B3, E3 and lane-assignment signs, when the road does not allow it.
- **Interpretation for the comma,** certainty medium. After 2020 the law no longer says in words that a C13 ends at
  the junction. Because the sign must be repeated after every level junction when the limit continues, an unrepeated
  limit after a level junction should be taken as no longer signed: fall back to the general limit of the road kind.
  - Exceptions: a limit on the town-name support holds for the whole locality, and a zone sign holds for the whole zone.
  - Motorway and via reservada interchanges are grade-separated and do not end a limit.
- **Old signs.** Signs placed under the old rules stay valid until replaced, at the latest by 1 January 2030. No new
  non-conforming sign may be placed from 1 April 2020 (DR 6/2019 art. 6, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).

## End signs

All in RST art. 24 ([RST pre-2019][rst98]; C13 and C20b unchanged in 2019, [DR 6/2019][dr6]):

- **C20a, end of all prohibitions.** It ends every prohibition previously imposed by signs on moving vehicles. The
  limit returns to the general one. Certainty high.
- **C20b, end of the speed limit.** It marks "o local a partir do qual é permitido circular a velocidade superior à
  imposta pelo sinal C13". The limit returns to the general limit of the road kind. Certainty high for the meaning; that
  "the general limit" is what follows is certainty high (CE art. 27 holds wherever nothing lower is imposed).
- **C20c/d/e** end no-overtaking. Not a speed sign.
- **Road-kind end signs:**
  - N2a/N2b, end of locality: 90 outside localities (RST art. 42);
  - H38, end of autoestrada, and H39, end of via reservada (RST art. 34): the kind of road that follows, usually 90.
- **Zone ends:**
  - G8, end of the zone of limited speed (RST art. 32, [RST pre-2019][rst98]);
  - G9, end of all prohibitions in the zone (same article);
  - G10, end of zona 30 ([DR 6/2019][dr6]);
  - H47, end of coexistence zone ([DR 6/2019][dr6]).
  - Since zones exist only inside localities (RST art. 33 n.º 6, art. 35 n.º 2), the limit after a zone end is 50.
    Certainty high.
- **Other ends:**
  - D14, end of minimum speed (RST art. 27, [RST pre-2019][rst98]). D8 is a minimum, not a maximum.
  - H37, end of recommended speed. H6 is a recommended speed on a blue square, not a limit (RST art. 34,
    [RST pre-2019][rst98]).
- **Road works.** The temporary-signs zone ends with ST14 "fim de obras" and the end-of-prohibition signs (RST art. 88,
  [RST pre-2019][rst98]).

## Zones

- **Where zone signs are used.** Only inside localities, at every access, with an end sign at every exit. The end sign
  may stand on the left (RST art. 33 n.º 6, as amended, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Coverage.** A sign inside a zone sign applies to all roads in the zone (RST art. 14 n.º 4 a), [RST pre-2019][rst98]).
- **G4 / G8, zone of limited speed.** G4 starts it and G8 ends it; the number is on the sign (RST art. 32,
  [RST pre-2019][rst98]).
- **G4a / G10, zona 30 km/h.** G4a marks a zone with traffic-calming devices limiting the speed to 30 km/h; G10 is
  "fim de zona 30 km/h". Added in 2019 ([DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **H46 / H47, zona residencial ou de coexistência.** The 20 km/h limit comes from CE art. 27, not from a number on
  the sign ([CE art. 27][ce27]).
  - H46 at every access and H47 at every exit, inside localities (RST art. 35 n.º 2, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
  - CE art. 78-A sets the behaviour rules in these zones ([CE][ce]).
- **G5c / G11, zona de emissões reduzidas.** Not a speed zone ([DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Zona 30 markings.** Retroreflective markings are not used in zona 30 (RST art. 59 n.º 3, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).

## Variable message signs

- **Status.** Variable message signs are second in the CE hierarchy of signs, after temporary signs and before light
  signals, vertical signs and markings (CE art. 7 n.º 2, [CE][ce]). Agents' orders prevail over everything (n.º 3).
- **Same meaning.** A sign shown inside a variable message sign keeps the meaning it has alone (RST art. 12 n.º 3,
  [RST pre-2019][rst98]).
- **Temporary.** Their messages are temporary and modify the normal use of the road (RST art. 12 n.º 4, as amended,
  [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Two kinds** (RST art. 48 n.º 2, [DR 6/2019][dr6]):
  - a) "painéis de mensagem variável", sign area plus text area;
  - b) "painéis luminosos de mensagem única", single display. These may lower the general limits temporarily by
    showing C13.
- **Placement.** Variable message signs go only over the road. The exception is single-message panels, which stand at
  the side and are repeated on the left as for regulatory signs (RST art. 52 n.º 1, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Look.** A prohibition sign is shown as a red ring on a dark background with white symbols (RST art. 51 n.º 2 a),
  [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Message rules** (RST art. 48 n.º 5, [DR 6/2019][dr6]):
  - a) "Quando não existam mensagens relevantes a mostrar relativas ao trânsito, pode ser exibida uma mensagem
    neutra";
  - c) no flashing or alternating messages;
  - e) national road-safety campaign messages are allowed in passive periods.
- **Pictograms V1–V13** (RST art. 49-A, added 2019, [DR 6/2019][dr6]):
  - V3 "via fechada": no traffic after the next exit, obligation to take it;
  - V4–V6: exits open or closed;
  - V7 congestion; V8 works; V9 snow or ice; V11 slippery road; V13 insufficient visibility.
  - None of them is a speed limit.
- **Blank sign.** No rule was found that says what a dark or blank variable sign means. Art. 48 n.º 5 a) allows a
  "neutral message" when nothing is relevant ([DR 6/2019][dr6]).
  - Interpretation, certainty medium: a blank or neutral panel imposes nothing. The fixed signs and the general limit
    of the road kind apply. A blank panel is not an end sign.
  - Whether a limit shown earlier by a VMS ends when the panel goes blank was not found.

## Supplementary panels

- **Scope.** A panel applies to the sign under which it is placed (RST art. 46 n.º 2, as amended, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Look.** Panels are white with black border and text, with a few blue exceptions (RST art. 47 n.º 4,
  [DR 6/2019][dr6]).
- **Panels relevant to speed** (RST art. 46, [RST pre-2019][rst98] and [DR 6/2019][dr6]):
  - 1a/1b, distance to the regulated point. The sign is then an advance notice; with H24/H25/H38/H39, art. 35 n.º 9.
  - 2, extent: the regulation holds over the stated length (art. 47).
  - 3a–3d, start, repetition or end of the regulated place.
  - 7a–7d, periodicity: days of the month, weekdays, hours.
  - 8a/8b, duration: valid only within, or beyond, the stated time (2019 wording).
  - 10a/10b, does not apply to / applies only to certain vehicles or operations. 10c, not for electric vehicles. 10e,
    not for cycles.
  - 11a–11q, vehicle classes. 11a means light passenger cars, so a C13 with 11a applies to us. 11b is goods vehicles,
    11e heavy vehicles, 11f motorcycles, 11m goods over a weight, 11n caravans and motorhomes. A limit carrying any
    vehicle panel other than 11a (or one that includes cars) does not apply to a passenger car. Certainty high from the
    wording "apenas se aplica aos veículos que figurarem no painel".
  - 15a/15b, weather (rain, snow, ice). In the pre-2019 text they say the danger of signs A5 and A29 comes from that
    weather. No rule for them under a C13 was found.
  - **17, exit-lane panel.** The message applies only on the deceleration, weaving or exit lane the arrow shows. Under
    the lowest sign of a support, it applies to every sign on that support (art. 46 n.º 1 and n.º 2,
    [DR 6/2019][dr6]). Certainty high.
  - 19a/19b, zone information.
- **Lane limits.** A limit for one lane is shown either over that lane, or at the side with markings or F1 signs, or
  with panel 17 (RST art. 12 n.º 2, [RST pre-2019][rst98]). F1a/F1b/F1c lane-assignment signs may give per-lane maximum and
  minimum speeds (RST art. 31 n.º 2, [RST pre-2019][rst98]).

## Special cases for a camera system

- **Temporary signs (works).** They have a yellow background and the same meaning as permanent ones (RST art. 77–78,
  [RST pre-2019][rst98]). They come first in the CE art. 7 hierarchy ([CE][ce]).
  - Speed limits step down 20 km/h at a time; on autoestradas not below 60 km/h, save exceptions (RST art. 86).
  - On autoestradas the first speed sign is at most 400 m before the works, at most 300 m on other roads
    (RST art. 96 c)).
  - The zone ends with ST14 and the end-of-prohibition signs (RST art. 88).
- **Lane closure.** S12 is a red light with two crossed diagonal bars on a black background: no traffic in the lane
  under it. S13 is a yellow 45° arrow: move to the lane shown. S14 is a green down arrow: lane open (RST art. 72 n.º 1,
  [DR 6/2019][dr6]). On reversible lanes S12/S14 assign the direction (art. 72 n.º 2). S12 is not a speed sign.
- **Painted limits.** C13 may be painted on the pavement, elongated, as a complement to the vertical sign at dangerous
  places (RST art. 64 n.º 4, [DR 6/2019][dr6]). It complements and does not replace the sign. Certainty high.
- **Sign height and position.** Signs stand on the right or over the road. Heights are 150 cm outside localities,
  220 cm or more inside, 550 cm or more over the road (RST art. 13, [RST pre-2019][rst98]). On roads with two or more lanes
  in one direction they are repeated on the left (RST art. 14 n.º 1).
- **Limits on the town-name support** hold for the whole locality (RST art. 14 n.º 4 b)). A 40 or 30 under the N1
  sign is the locality's limit, not a sign that ends at the first junction. Certainty high.
- **Recommended speed.** H6 is a blue square with a number: advice, not a limit.
- **Minimum speed.** D8 is a blue circle with a number. Neither should be read as a C13.
- **Average-speed control.** H42 announces average-speed control and H43 instantaneous-speed control (RST art. 34,
  [RST pre-2019][rst98]). Over an average-speed section the offence is located where the controlled section ends (CE
  art. 27 n.º 4, [CE art. 27][ce27]). Not a limit sign.
- **Exit numbers** are yellow on roads with only grade-separated junctions (RST art. 18 n.º 9, [RST pre-2019][rst98]). This
  is a cue that the road is motorway-like.
- **Transition period to 2030.** Pre-2020 sign designs may still be met (DR 6/2019 art. 6, [DR 6/2019][dr6]).

## For the comma

- IMPLICIT_LIMITS row, certainty high (CE art. 27 n.º 1, [CE art. 27][ce27]):

  ```python
  "PT": {"town": 50, "rural": 90, "expressway": 100, "motorway": 120},
  ```

  - `town` is the localidade: N1a/N1b start it (`other_sign:town_entry`) and N2a/N2b end it (`other_sign:town_exit`).
  - `expressway` is the via reservada a automóveis e motociclos: H25 starts it (`other_sign:expressway_start`) and H39
    ends it (`other_sign:expressway_end`, giving rural 90).
  - `motorway` is the autoestrada: H24 starts it (`other_sign:motorway_start`) and H38 ends it
    (`other_sign:motorway_end`, giving rural 90).
  - Extra kinds, all inside localities:
    - zona de coexistência, H46: 20 (implicit, no number on the sign). H47 returns to town 50.
    - zona 30, G4a: 30 (number on the sign). G10 returns to 50.
    - G4 zone of limited speed: the number on the sign. G8 or G9 returns to 50.
    - The code's `zone` kind covers G4/G4a. H46 needs a sign class of its own with an implied 20.
  - Motorway minimum: 50 km/h (CE art. 27 n.º 6). Not a maximum.
  - Novice drivers: no limit found, so nothing to encode (CE art. 122, [CE art. 122][ce122]).
- The intersection rule: **partly**, certainty medium.
  - Until April 2020, RST art. 12 n.º 5 said regulatory signs hold "até à intersecção de nível mais próxima". DR 6/2019
    revoked that number ([DR 6/2019][dr6]).
  - RST art. 14 n.º 2 still requires speed signs to be repeated after each level intersection when the limit continues.
  - So treat a C13 as ending at the next level junction (crossroads, T-junction, roundabout) unless it is repeated, the
    same as CH.
  - Exceptions (art. 14 n.º 4): a limit on the town-name support lasts the whole locality, and a zone limit lasts the
    whole zone.
  - Grade-separated interchanges do not end a limit. Many pre-2020 signs were placed under the explicit old rule and
    stay valid until 2030.
- What a blank variable sign means: not found in the law. RST art. 48 n.º 5 a) allows a "neutral message" when nothing
  is relevant. Recommended: blank or neutral means no VMS limit, so keep the fixed-sign or general limit. Do not treat
  blank as an end sign. Certainty medium.
- Other rules the code should know:
  - Hierarchy (CE art. 7 n.º 2): temporary (yellow) signs, then VMS, then light signals, then fixed signs, then
    markings. A yellow works C13 beats a white one.
  - Panel 17 under a C13 means exit-lane only. Ignore it on the main carriageway; the ramp logic should take it as the
    ramp limit.
  - Any vehicle panel other than 11a, such as heavy vehicles, trailers or caravans, means the limit is not ours.
  - H24/H25/H38/H39 with a distance panel are advance notices: do not switch road kind there.
  - H6 (recommended) and D8 (minimum) carry numbers but are not maximum limits.
  - C20a (end of all prohibitions) and C20b (end of speed limit) both give value 0, restoring the road kind's general
    limit.
  - A C13 painted on the road is only a complement. Reading it as `road_marking` is fine; it should agree with the post
    sign.
  - The H28 border panel lists the general limits. Do not read it as a single limit.

## Open points

- **Intersection rule after 2020.** Art. 12 n.º 5 RST is marked "(Revogado.)" in the amended art. 12, but the repeal
  clause of DR 6/2019 (art. 7) does not list it. Check:
  - the republished RST (Anexo VII of DR 6/2019);
  - Declaração de Retificação 60-A/2019;
  - any ANSR guidance or court ruling (acórdão) on how far a C13 holds. None was found.
- **Novice drivers.** No current limit was found. When the old 90 km/h novice limit ended was not found.
- **Blank or dark VMS.** No explicit rule was found. The same goes for whether a VMS limit ends when the panel goes
  blank.
- **Weather.** No wet-weather or visibility general limit was found. No C13 + rain panel use was found either.
- **Ramp limits.** Whether a limit on an acceleration lane ends at the merge was not found, apart from panel 17
  (exit-lane only).
- **Brisa and Infraestruturas de Portugal** pages on gantry signs and lane crosses were not read. The ANSR page for
  the RST returned 404.
- **Higher limits.** CE art. 28 allows limits above 120 on given sections. Whether any are in force was not found.
