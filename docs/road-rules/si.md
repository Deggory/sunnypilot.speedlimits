# Slovenia (SI): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Zakon o pravilih cestnega prometa (ZPrCP; official consolidated text ZPrCP-UPB7,
Uradni list RS 156/21, corr. 161/21, amended by ZPrCP-G 22/25, Constitutional Court decision 86/25 on art. 107 only);
Pravilnik o prometni signalizaciji in prometni opremi na cestah (Uradni list RS 26/24, corr. 30/24, amended 22/25 and
16/26; unofficial consolidated text NPB2, in use from 2026-02-28); Zakon o cestah (ZCes-2, Uradni list RS 132/22 and
later amendments) for the road definitions.

Every source below was read on 2026-10-29. Certainty marks apply to interpretations only: **high** means the text says
it, **medium** means it follows from the text but is not stated in those words, **low** means a guess or a non-legal
source. "Not found" means it could not be verified.

Note on sources: pisrs.si renders only with JavaScript. The texts were read as the official gazette PDFs on
uradni-list.si ([ZPrCP-UPB7][zprcp], [Pravilnik 26/24][prav24], [ZCes-2][zces]) and as the PisRS file of the
consolidated Pravilnik NPB2 ([NPB2][npb2]), whose wording of art. 17 and art. 52 matches 26/24 word for word. The sign
pictures were read by rendering the pages of [Pravilnik 26/24][prav24] (tables 10, 12 and 13). The 2024 Pravilnik replaced
the 2015 one ([Pravilnik 99/15][prav15]) on its entry into force (art. 77, 78: in force 15 days after 2024-03-26, applied
six months later); signs set up under the 2015 Pravilnik must be brought into line by 2030-01-01 ([22/25 art. 18][a2225]),
so both generations of signs are on the road. The 16/26 amendment changes only signs 3217, 3414 and safety barriers
([16/26][a1626]); the 30/24 correction only art. 76 and 77 ([30/24][corr2430]). ZPrCP-G changes art. 15.a, 30, 101 and
103, not the speed articles ([ZPrCP-G][zprcpg]).

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

Source: ZPrCP art. 46 ("najvišje dovoljene hitrosti"), art. 47 and 48 ([ZPrCP-UPB7, p. 8598-8599][zprcp]).

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Road in a settlement (naselje: from sign 2434 "Rob naselja" to 2435) | 50 km/h | Day and night alike. May be raised to 70 km/h on single roads or parts of them, only by sign (art. 46(2)). | ZPrCP 46(1)1, 46(2) |
| Območje omejene hitrosti (zone 30, sign 2421) | 30 km/h | Only inside a settlement (ZCes-2 art. 2(1)50) | ZPrCP 46(1)2 |
| Območje skupnega prometnega prostora (shared space, sign 2445) | 20 km/h; 30 km/h where signed | | ZPrCP 46(1)3, 46(2) |
| Območje umirjenega prometa (traffic-calmed zone, sign 2427) and območje za pešce (pedestrian zone, sign 2423) | 10 km/h | | ZPrCP 46(1)4 |
| Outside settlements, all other roads (also a "cesta za motorna vozila", sign 2405) | 90 km/h | | ZPrCP 46(3)3 |
| Hitra cesta (expressway, sign 2403) | 110 km/h | Single or dual carriageway alike (no distinction in art. 46(3)) | ZPrCP 46(3)2 |
| Avtocesta (motorway, sign 2401) | 130 km/h | | ZPrCP 46(3)1 |

Conditions:

- **Visibility below 50 m:** 50 km/h for all vehicles ("za vsa vozila, kadar je vidljivost manjša od 50 m", art.
  47(1)4). Certainty high. The camera cannot measure it.
- **Snow chains fitted:** 50 km/h (art. 47(1)4).
- **Wet road, rain, night:** no number in ZPrCP. "Ponoči" (sunset to sunrise) is defined in art. 3(1)28 but no night
  limit exists. Certainty high (art. 45-48 read in full).
- **Novice drivers (voznik začetnik):** no speed limit in ZPrCP. The act mentions novice drivers only for licence loss at
  7 penalty points (art. 26) and a ban on carrying passengers commercially; the EU's Your Europe page lists none
  ([Your Europe][ye]). The Zakon o voznikih (ZVoz-1) was not read. Certainty medium-high that none exists.
- **Signs override the rules:** "Tako morajo ravnati tudi tedaj, ko je to v nasprotju s prometnimi pravili" (art. 98(1)).
- **Other vehicles:** vehicles over 3.5 t 80 km/h everywhere outside settlements (art. 47(1)1), articulated buses and
  over-3.5-t trucks with trailers 70 (47(1)2). On avtocesta and hitra cesta with two carriageways of at least two lanes:
  cars up to 3.5 t towing a trailer 100, certified trucks 90, certified buses 100 (art. 48(1)). A car with a trailer is
  90 on ordinary roads by the general rule.
- **Motorways inside a settlement:** art. 46(3) speaks of "cesta zunaj naselij". The settlement signs 2434/2435 may stand
  on avtocesta and hitra cesta only on the ramp arms of their junctions (Pravilnik table 10, signs 2434 and 2435), so the
  carriageway itself is never signed as a settlement. Certainty medium that 130/110 apply on every motorway section.

All codes are the 2024 Pravilnik's four-digit codes (table 10, "2400 – prometni znaki za urejanje cestnega prometa",
[Pravilnik 26/24 p. 1896-1905][prav24]). The pre-2016 codes (II-30, III-10, III-12, III-14) still appear on Wikipedia and
OSM ([OSM wiki][osm]).

| Kind | Begins | Ends | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avtocesta (motorway) | 2401 "Avtocesta" | 2402 "Konec avtoceste" | **Green** square, white motorway pictogram (two carriageways under a bridge). End: same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Hitra cesta (expressway) | 2403 "Hitra cesta" | 2404 "Konec hitre ceste" | **Blue** square, the same white motorway pictogram. End: same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Cesta za motorna vozila (road for motor vehicles; no own limit) | 2405 | 2406 | Blue square, white car seen from the front. End: red diagonal bar. |
| Settlement (naselje) | 2434 "Rob naselja" (2015 name: "Naselje") | 2435 "Konec roba naselja" | **Yellow** rectangle, black border, black place name (2434-1: two lines). End: same with a red diagonal bar. |

- 2401 and 2403 may carry the supplementary sign 4810 (entry to a toll / vignette road) and a notice of video
  surveillance and tolling (table 10, column C; table 13, 4810).
- Direction signs (art. 20, table 12): avtocesta green with white text, hitra cesta **blue** with white text, other
  roads **yellow** with black text, parts of towns white with black text, tourist brown, cycle routes red. Certainty
  high. A camera can tell motorway from expressway by colour: green 2401 against blue 2403 with the same pictogram.
- The blue car sign 2405 is **not** an expressway in Slovenia: it only bars slow vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists. Its
  road keeps 90 outside and 50 inside settlements. Certainty high (art. 46 has no row for it). This differs from
  Switzerland and Austria, where the car pictogram marks the 100 km/h Autostrasse/Autostraße.
- Definitions (ZCes-2 art. 2(1), [ZCes-2][zces]):
  - 1, avtocesta: separated carriageways, normally a hard shoulder, no level crossing with roads, railways, cycle paths
    or footpaths; "njen sestavni del so tudi servisne prometne površine in cestni priključki nanjo", so the ramps belong
    to the motorway.
  - 22, hitra cesta: "z enim ali z dvema smernima voziščema, dostopna prek cestnih priključkov ali nadzorovanih
    križišč, brez odstavnih pasov". An expressway may have one carriageway and controlled at-grade intersections.
  - 79, rob naselja: the edge of an area with at least ten buildings, marked on the road by the prescribed sign.
- Where 2401/2403 stand: the Pravilnik says only "Mesto, kjer se začenja avtocesta / hitra cesta". The exact position
  (start of the entry ramp or the merge) was **not found** in the Pravilnik. Because the ramps are part of the motorway
  (ZCes-2 2(1)1), the sign at the start of the entry ramp is the natural reading. Certainty low-medium.

## How long a speed sign holds

**Rule: until the end sign, otherwise until the next intersection.** Pravilnik art. 17(4)
([Pravilnik 26/24 p. 1906][prav24]; same text in [NPB2][npb2]):

> "Prepovedi in omejitve, označene s prometnimi znaki za prepovedi in omejitve (2200) ter posameznimi prometnimi znaki
> za urejanje cestnega prometa (2407, 2408, 2409, 2409-1 in 2409-2), veljajo od mesta, kjer so postavljeni, do mesta,
> kjer je postavljen prometni znak o njihovem preklicu, sicer pa do prvega naslednjega križišča javnih oziroma
> nekategoriziranih cest, ki se uporabljajo za javni cestni promet."

The speed sign 2232 "Omejitev hitrosti" is in group 2200, so it ends at the end sign (2233, 2238), at a new speed sign
(art. 17(7): "s prometnim znakom za novo omejitev hkrati prekliče predhodna omejitev"), or at the next intersection of
public roads (or uncategorised roads open to public traffic), whichever comes first. Certainty high.

Details and exceptions:

- **Repeated after junctions.** The 2015 Pravilnik added "Če naj prepoved ali omejitev velja še naprej za križiščem
  teh cest, se znak ponovno postavi za vsakim križiščem s temi cestami" (art. 17(4), [Pravilnik 99/15 p. 12557][prav15]).
  The 2024 text drops that sentence, but the rule it follows from is unchanged, so a limit that continues must be signed
  again after each intersection. Certainty medium-high.
- **Length panel.** A limit may hold only for a stated length, at most 1000 m, with panel 4103, "če znotraj tega dela
  ceste ni nobenega križišča" (art. 17(5)).
- **Settlement-wide limit.** A 2200 sign on the same post as 2434 at the entry of a settlement holds in the whole
  settlement unless other signs say otherwise (art. 17(10)), and "Izrecna odredba, označena s prometnim znakom za omejitev
  hitrosti, s prometnim znakom 2435 preneha veljati" (art. 17(11)). Such a limit does not end at intersections.
  Certainty high.
- **Zones** (2400 group, CONA signs) hold until their end sign (art. 17(3)): art. 17(4) names only group 2200 and signs
  2407-2409. Certainty high.
- **Variable signs** hold until a variable end sign (art. 52(8), see below).
- **After an intersection in a settlement the limit is 50, not "none".** Higher Court of Celje, VSC PRp 49/2022,
  2022-05-17: neither sign 2238 nor an intersection or roundabout cancels the statutory settlement limit; they cancel
  only the limit set by the earlier speed sign ([TFL summary][vsc]). Only the summary was read. Certainty high for the
  holding as summarised.
- **What counts as an intersection.** ZCes-2 art. 2(1)32: "križišče oziroma krožišče je prometna površina, ki nastane s
  križanjem ali združitvijo dveh ali več cest v isti ravnini". Only at-grade junctions count, roundabouts included.
  Grade-separated motorway interchanges (priključek, razcep) are not intersections, so a limit on a motorway carriageway
  goes on through them. Certainty medium-high. Private accesses (individualni cestni priključek, art. 2(1)23) are not
  junctions of public roads and do not end a limit. Certainty medium. The ramp terminal where an exit ramp meets the
  secondary road at grade is an intersection. Certainty medium-high.
- **Ramps and slip roads.** No rule found on whether a limit signed on a ramp ends at the merge. Since the ramp is part
  of the motorway (ZCes-2 2(1)1), the merge is not a junction of two roads, and the literal reading is that a ramp limit
  carries on to the next end sign or new limit. That is impractical, and in practice it is not the case. **Not found**;
  certainty low either way. Whether a limit on the main carriageway binds traffic entering after the sign: not found.
- **End of motorway / expressway.** Whether 2402 or 2404 by themselves end a 2232 limit is not stated. Certainty low.
- **Media check.** Siol.net (2012-01-31) applies the same rule on Zaloška cesta, Ljubljana: a signed limit runs on
  for about 280 m (and 250 m in a second case) to the next junction ([Siol][siol]). Certainty low (press, old Pravilnik).

## Zones

- **Zone 30 (2421 "Območje omejene hitrosti"):** white square panel with "CONA" over a 30 speed sign, 600 x 600 mm. It
  may be fixed to a traffic-light pole. Only in settlements. Ends at 2422. Zone signs stand on every road leading into
  the zone (art. 17(8)). 30 by law (ZPrCP 46(1)2); the sign shows only 30.
- **Shared space (2445 "Območje skupnega prometnega prostora"):** "CONA" panel, blue field with pedestrian, cyclist and
  car, and an inset 20 speed sign (2445-1 shows 30). "Vstavljeni znak za najvišjo dovoljeno hitrost ustreza odrejeni
  prometni ureditvi območja". Ends at 2446. 20 by law, 30 when signed (ZPrCP 46(1)3, 46(2)).
- **Traffic-calmed zone (2427 "Območje umirjenega prometa"):** blue rectangle (900 x 600 or 600 x 400 mm),
  pedestrians have priority and children may play. 10 km/h (ZPrCP 46(1)4). There is **no number on the sign**. Ends
  at 2428.
- **Pedestrian zone (2423 "Območje za pešce"):** "CONA" with the round blue pedestrian sign. Only cyclists and users of
  special means of transport may drive, plus the vehicles listed on a supplementary panel. 10 km/h for vehicles
  allowed in (ZPrCP 46(1)4). Ends at 2424.
- **Automated-vehicle test area (2447/2448):** an end-of-speed-limit sign is always placed there (art. 17(6)).
- **Settlement-wide limit:** a 2232 under 2434 behaves like a zone until 2435 (art. 17(10), (11)).

Source: [Pravilnik 26/24, table 10, p. 1900-1905][prav24]; [ZPrCP art. 46][zprcp]; ZCes-2 art. 2(1)48-50 ([ZCes-2][zces]).

## Variable message signs

Pravilnik section 1.8 "Spremenljiva prometnoinformativna signalizacija" (SPIS), art. 50-53
([Pravilnik 26/24 p. 1957-1958][prav24]; [NPB2][npb2]):

- **Legal status:** "Spremenljivi prometni znaki s kontinuirano in nekontinuirano vsebino prikaza imajo enak pomen kot
  prometni znaki s stalno vsebino prikaza" (art. 52(7)). They are binding like fixed signs. Certainty high.
- **How long a variable speed sign holds:** "Spremenljivi prometni znaki za označevanje omejitve hitrosti (2232)
  veljajo od mesta postavitve znaka do mesta postavitve spremenljivega znaka o njegovem preklicu (2233 ali 2238)" (art.
  52(8)). A variable limit ends at a variable 2233 or 2238, not by default at an intersection. Certainty high for the
  text; the relation to art. 17(4) is medium.
- **Look:** on light-emitting signs the base colour may be black (not retroreflective), black and white symbols may be
  white (or yellow), and black borders white (or yellow) (art. 51(2)). A variable speed sign is a red ring with white or
  yellow digits on black. Certainty medium (the red ring is not mentioned, so it stays). Electromechanical (prism,
  roller) signs look like fixed signs (art. 51(1)).
- **Alternating and flashing:** at most two signs may alternate, at least 1.5 s each (art. 52(3)). A sign that detects
  a violation may flash its content at 1 Hz, with yellow lamps flashing at 2 Hz (art. 52(4), (5)).
- **Blank or dark panel:** "SPIS ob okvari ali prekinitvi izvora svetlobe ne spremeni svojega pomena oziroma takoj
  preide v ničelno stanje signalne vsebine" (art. 52(6)). A failed sign either keeps its meaning or goes blank at once.
  Nothing makes a blank panel an end sign: art. 52(8) ends a variable limit only with a variable 2233/2238. Certainty
  medium. What a driver must do when a whole gantry system is switched off was **not found**.
- **Time-limited fixed signs:** signs built so that their content is visible only while the order applies are allowed
  (art. 17(12)).
- **Gantries over lanes / lane-specific limits:** no article in the Pravilnik assigning a speed limit to one lane was
  found. Lane-use signs 2451, 2451-1 and 2451-2 are used as variable signs (table 10). Hard-shoulder running is allowed
  when "označeno s predpisano spremenljivo prometno signalizacijo" (ZPrCP art. 30(12) in UPB7 numbering).
- **DARS practice:** dynamic speed management on the approaches to the Ljubljana ring and on the ring itself shows 100,
  80 or 60 km/h on 53 overhead displays. It starts about 20 km out (Vrhnika on the A1 Primorska, Grosuplje on the A2
  Dolenjska, Vodice on the A2 Gorenjska, former Kompolje toll station on the A1 Štajerska), was rolled out in July 2025,
  and is set by the traffic data ([N1, 2025-07-01][n1]; [RTV SLO, 2025-03-06][rtv]). Certainty medium (press, not DARS's
  own page).
- **Fog sections:** sign 2443 "Območje s pogostim pojavom megle" ("V MEGLI!" panel) and 2443-1/-2 carrying a 50 or 70
  speed disc require that speed when fog reduces visibility. Used on avtoceste and hitre ceste, with marking 5323. These
  are fixed panels, not SPIS (table 10, 2443).

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **Speed discs on trucks and buses.** Buses, trucks over 3.5 t and trailers must carry, rear left, a disc "po obliki in
   barvi enaka prometnemu znaku za omejitev hitrosti", at least 20 cm across, showing their own limit. Vehicles cleared
   for 80/90/100 on motorways carry it too (ZPrCP 47(2)). Expect many 80, 90 and 100 discs on vehicles ahead.
   Certainty high.
2. **Town signs are yellow** with black names, and a speed sign on the same post sets a settlement-wide limit that
   survives intersections until 2435 (art. 17(10), (11)).
3. **Motorway vs expressway** by colour: green 2401 = 130, blue 2403 = 110, same pictogram. The **blue car** 2405 is
   not an expressway: it keeps 90/50.
4. **End signs show numbers** (2233 with a grey crossed 20-120) and may stand on the left only. They must not be read
   as limits.
5. **2232 with "Konec"** (4401-1) is an end, not a new limit.
6. **Conditional numbers:** 2232 with a weather panel (4403), a time panel (4402) or a truck/bus panel (4501); the 50/70
   discs on the fog panel 2443. None of these applies to a car in normal conditions.
7. **Intersections end limits, except** settlement-wide limits, zones and variable limits. Inside a settlement the
   limit after an intersection is 50, not "no limit" ([vsc]).
8. **Roadworks:** white-based order signs used for closures have a **yellow** base (art. 16(2) and 20(4)); a yellow
   speed disc is an ordinary limit.
9. **Two generations of signs:** signs under the 2015 Pravilnik (town board titled "Naselje", older pictograms) may stay
   until 2030-01-01 ([22/25 art. 18][a2225]).
10. **No settlement boards on motorway carriageways:** a yellow name board seen while on avtocesta/hitra cesta is on a
    ramp arm (table 10, 2434/2435) and does not apply to the main carriageway.

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:**
  ```python
  "SI": {"town": 50, "rural": 90, "expressway": 110, "motorway": 130},
  ```
  `expressway` is the hitra cesta (blue 2403/2404). The "cesta za motorna vozila" (blue car 2405) maps to `rural`, not
  `expressway`: if the classifier's `other_sign:expressway_start` was learned from the Swiss/Austrian car pictogram,
  a Slovenian 2405 must not set 110. Zone 30 (2421) and shared space (2445) carry their number, so `zone:30` and
  `zone:20` cover them. The traffic-calmed and pedestrian zones (2427, 2423) are 10 km/h with **no number on the sign**:
  supporting them would need a new key (e.g. `"calmed_zone": 10`) and a sign class. Their end signs and 2422 restore
  `town` (50).
- **The intersection rule: yes** (Pravilnik art. 17(4)): a 2232 sign holds until 2233/2238, a new limit or the next
  at-grade intersection of public roads, whichever comes first. Exceptions: a limit posted with the town sign holds to
  2435 (art. 17(10), (11)); zone signs hold to their end sign (art. 17(3)); variable limits hold to a variable 2233/2238
  (art. 52(8)); grade-separated interchanges are not intersections (ZCes-2 art. 2(1)32). After an intersection the
  general limit of the road comes back, 50 inside a settlement (VSC PRp 49/2022). The code's map-change fallback
  (`MAP_SECONDS`) fits this. On avtocesta/hitra cesta the map's change at an interchange should not drop the limit read
  on the carriageway. Certainty medium.
- **Blank variable sign:** not an end sign. The law ends a variable limit only with a variable 2233 or 2238 (art. 52(8)).
  A failed panel goes to "ničelno stanje" (art. 52(6)). Keep the last limit; the end of a dynamic-management section
  is not regulated. Certainty medium.
- **Other rules the code should know:**
  - 2435 (end of settlement) cancels any speed limit set by sign (art. 17(11)) and restores 90.
  - 2238 restores the road's general limit; inside a settlement that is 50, never "no limit" (VSC PRp 49/2022).
  - A new speed sign replaces the previous one outright (art. 17(7)), higher or lower.
  - A speed sign on the town-sign post covers the whole settlement (art. 17(10)): do not let the map-change rule end it
    inside the settlement.
  - 47(1)4: 50 km/h for everyone when visibility is under 50 m. Not observable by the camera, but a reason not to trust a
    high limit in dense fog.
  - Ignore rear-of-vehicle speed discs (ZPrCP 47(2)).

## Open points

1. **Ramps:** no rule found on whether a limit on an entry or exit ramp ends at the merge, or whether a main-carriageway
   limit binds traffic that joins after the sign. The ramp is legally part of the motorway (ZCes-2 2(1)1), so the merge
   is not an intersection. A DARS signing guideline (technical specification) might settle it; not read.
2. **Where 2401/2403 stand** (start of the entry ramp or at the merge): not found in the Pravilnik.
3. **2402/2404 and a running 2232:** whether the end of a motorway/expressway by itself ends a signed limit was not
   found.
4. **2233 placement wording** in 26/24 ("samo na levi strani", except multi-lane) differs from all other end signs
   ("lahko ... tudi samo na levi"). The consolidated NPB2 tables are images and were not compared. Possibly a drafting
   slip.
5. **Blank gantries / system off:** what ends a dynamic limit when DARS switches the system off (all panels blank) is
   not regulated in the Pravilnik; DARS's own operating rules were not found.
6. **Lane-specific limits** on gantries: no provision found.
7. **Novice drivers:** ZVoz-1 (Zakon o voznikih) not read; ZPrCP has no novice speed limit.
8. **Case law:** only the TFL summary of VSC PRp 49/2022 was read; the full text on sodnapraksa.si was not opened (the
   session's web search budget ran out). No ruling on ramps, interchanges or variable signs was found.
9. **ZCes-2 amendments after 78/23** were not checked; the definitions quoted are from the 2022 original.
