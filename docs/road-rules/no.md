# Norway (NO): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Vegtrafikkloven (LOV-1965-06-18-4) § 5 and § 6; Trafikkreglene (FOR-1986-03-21-747) § 4, § 11, § 13; Skiltforskriften (FOR-2005-10-07-1219) § 1, § 2, § 7, § 8, § 11, § 12, § 17, § 18, § 26, § 27, § 47; Vegdirektoratet's sign standard N300:2026 Trafikkskilt (in force 13 March 2026). Under Skiltforskriften § 35, N300 binds the road authorities that set up signs; it is not itself a regulation that binds drivers. So N300 tells the camera where signs will stand and what they will mean.

Certainty tags: **high** = the text of the law or N300 says it; **medium** = a direct reading of those texts, or an official secondary source; **low** = an inference with no text that says it.

Limits are in km/h only. No mph signs exist in Norway (high: Skiltforskriften § 8, sign 362 "angitt antall km/t").

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Outside a built-up area | 80 | It is not signed as a rule. A 364 sign marks the return to it, and so does a 362 "80" sign after a stretch above 80 (high). | Vegtrafikkloven § 6 second paragraph; N300 Krav 4.5.30-1, 4.5.31-1 |

Other conditions:
- **Wet road, night, visibility:** there is no numeric rule. Vegtrafikkloven § 6 first paragraph and Trafikkreglene § 13 no. 1 give only the duty to adapt speed (high).
- **Season:** there is no national winter limit (medium: nothing in Vegtrafikkloven § 6, Trafikkreglene § 13 or N300 4.5.29–4.5.30). Local seasonal limits exist. Oslo's *miljøfartsgrense* is 60 km/h from 1 November until the start of May, on Rv 4 Sinsen–Grorud, Ring 3 Ryen–Granfosstunnelen, E18 Hjortnes–Lysaker (weekdays 06:00–22:00 only) and Rv 163 Østre Aker vei Økern–Stovner (high, Statens vegvesen's miljøfartsgrense page). Under N300 Krav 4.5.30-7, a limit restricted in time shall be shown with variable signs (high).
- **Novice drivers:** no lower speed limit found in Vegtrafikkloven § 6 or Trafikkreglene § 13 (medium). Not found elsewhere.
- **Heavy vehicles and trailers:** on a road with a special limit above 80, a motor vehicle over 3,500 kg or a motor vehicle with a trailer may not exceed 80 (Trafikkreglene § 13 no. 4, high). There are exceptions:
  - an M1 car up to 7,500 kg without a trailer;
  - certain buses, up to 100;
  - a car and trailer combination meeting Forskrift om bruk av kjøretøy § 4-4 and § 4-5, up to 100 ("Tempo 100").

**Telling a motorway from a motor-traffic road with the camera:**
- Each has its own pictogram: 502 versus 503.
- A two-lane motor-traffic road without a median carries warning sign 148 "Møtende trafikk" (oncoming traffic) above the 503 sign (N300 Krav 6.4.2-6, high).
- A motorway shall have grade-separated junctions and be at least 5 km long (N300 Krav 6.4.1-2 and 6.4.1-3).
- A motor-traffic road shall have no private accesses and grade-separated junctions. It may have an at-grade junction only by exception, and then it is signed 70 (N300 Krav 6.4.2-2).

**Where the signs stand:**
- 502 and 503 stand where the road begins and at the start of every road or ramp that leads to it (N300 Krav 6.4.1-7 and 6.4.2-11, high):
  - on both sides at the start of the road and on two-lane ramps;
  - on the right only on one-lane ramps.
  - They may share a post with a 362 sign, which is then on top (Krav 6.4.1-9, 6.4.2 placement).
- 504 is pre-warned 400–500 m ahead with the distance plate 802 and stands on both sides (Krav 6.4.3-4).
- 505 is pre-warned 250–350 m ahead (Krav 6.4.4-4).
- 504 is **not** put on exit ramps, unless it is unclear where the motorway ends (Krav 6.4.3-3). 505 is likewise not put on exit ramps (Krav 6.4.4-3).
- Where a motorway runs straight into a motor-traffic road, only a 503 is put up, never a 504 (Krav 6.4.3-2). The reverse transition shows a 502 and no 505 (Krav 6.4.4-2).
- **Neither 502–505 nor any road sign changes the speed limit.** Sign 362 is ended only by another 362, or by 366, 364, 540 or 548 (Skiltforskriften § 8, sign 362; high).

## How long a speed sign holds

- **Sign 362 "Fartsgrense" holds "on the stretch concerned until another limit is given"** by a new 362 or a 366, or until a 364, 540 or 548 stands (Skiltforskriften § 8, sign 362; high). This overrides the general rule for prohibition signs, which hold only up to the next junction (§ 7 no. 2, "dersom ikke annet er bestemt i § 8"; high).
- **Junctions:** "Særskilte fartsgrenser oppheves ikke av kryss" — special limits are not ended by junctions (N300 4.5.30, Anvendelse; high).
- **Repeaters:**
  - Special limits shall be repeated after junctions (N300 Krav 4.5.30-12, high).
  - Repeaters stand at most 5 km apart for 90, 100 and 110, and at most 500 m apart for 70 and below (N300 Krav 4.5.30-13, high). N300 sets no distance for 60 or 80.
  - Repeaters stand on the right, and also on the left on roads with three or more lanes in one direction (Krav 4.5.30-14).
  - The general 50 may be repeated after junctions where speeds are too high (Krav 4.5.30-1).
- **Turning onto another road:** the limit belongs to the stretch of road, not to the driver. A 364 shall not be put on a side road for traffic leaving the limited road (Krav 4.5.31-3). A new limit on a side road is signed there, just after the junction (Krav 4.5.30-9 and 4.5.30-10). So on another road, its own signs or its general limit apply (medium).
- **Pre-warning:** a 362 with distance plate 802 "Avstand" warns of a limit ahead. The limit starts at the full sign put up at that distance (Skiltforskriften § 18, sign 802; N300 Krav 4.4-1_1; high). It is required when the limit drops by more than 30 km/h (Krav 4.5.30-5).
- **Zones:** a 366 holds for the whole area until 368, 540 or 548 (Skiltforskriften § 8, sign 366). It is not repeated inside the zone (N300 Krav 4.5.32-3), and no other limit may be signed inside it (Krav 4.5.32-2) (high).
- **Ramps and slip roads:**
  - Traffic rules for motorways and motor-traffic roads also apply on their entry and exit roads. These are the 40 km/h minimum design speed, the ban on reversing and turning, and the ban on stopping (Trafikkreglene § 4 no. 2, § 11 no. 2 and § 17 no. 1 e; high).
  - No rule was found that gives a ramp its own speed limit or says what holds on an unsigned ramp: **not found**.
  - Interpretation (low): Trafikkreglene names entry and exit roads apart from the carriageway, and a limit belongs to its stretch of road. So a 362 put on a ramp is that ramp's limit and ends at the merge. On the carriageway, the carriageway's own 362 holds, and it is repeated after each junction.
  - Traffic entering from a ramp is bound by the carriageway's limit, because the limit holds for the whole stretch (Skiltforskriften § 8) (medium).
- **Interchanges:** motorway rules end where the exit road meets a road that is not a motorway, with no 504 sign (Skiltforskriften § 12; N300 Krav 6.4.3-3; high).
- **Case law:** no Norwegian decision was found on how far a 362 limit extends, or on junctions: **not found**. The Supreme Court decisions found (HR-2022-2301-U, HR-2023-2233-U) are about sentencing and leave to appeal, not about where a sign applies.

## Zones

- **Speed-limit zone:** 366 "Fartsgrensesone", ended by 368 (Skiltforskriften § 8). It is used **only for 30 km/h**, in residential areas, town and village centres and similar areas (N300 Krav 4.5.32-1). The 366 also warns that physical speed bumps may be present (§ 8). It is mandatory, not advisory (high).
  - It is not repeated inside the zone (Krav 4.5.32-3), and no other limit is signed inside it (Krav 4.5.32-2).
  - The 368 is on the back of the 366 (Krav 4.5.34-2).
  - Sign 367 "Fartsgrensesone for liten elektrisk motorvogn" sets 6 km/h for e-scooters only, ended by 369. **The car must ignore it** (high).
- **Living street and pedestrian street** (540/542, 548/550): areas where walking pace applies (Trafikkreglene § 13 no. 3). They end 362 and 366 limits (Skiltforskriften § 8).
- There is no "recommended" area limit like Sweden's (not found).

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status:** a sign may be built to switch between symbols or texts, and its design and colour may then differ from the regulation (Skiltforskriften § 2 no. 3). A variable sign must meet the rules for the sign it shows (N300 Krav 1.2.2-1). A variable 362 is therefore a mandatory speed limit (high).
- **Not allowed as variable signs:** 366–368 zone signs (N300 Krav 1.2.2-2, high).
- **Inverted colours:** LED variable prohibition signs may swap white and black (N300 Krav 1.5.4-1, high). A camera can therefore see a red ring with a **white number on black**.
- **Lanes:**
  - With two or more lanes in one direction, a sign placed over the middle of a lane applies only to that lane (Skiltforskriften § 2 no. 2, second paragraph; N300 Krav 1.3.3-7; high).
  - A sign that applies to all lanes shall not be placed over one lane only (N300 1.3.3).
  - An overhead 362 that introduces or changes a limit shall be an LED variable or lit sign (N300 Krav 4.5.30-9).
- **Time-limited special limits:** these shall be shown with variable signs, not with a time plate (N300 Krav 4.5.30-7, high).
- **Where in use:** variable limits run on E18 Asker–Holmen (nine new gantries, both directions, in service from May 2026: Statens vegvesen news, 13 April 2026) and on E6 in Trondheim (miljøfartsgrense page) (medium).
- **Blank or dark panels:** no rule found on what a dark speed VMS means: **not found**. Interpretation (medium):
  - A sign applies only once set up and uncovered (Skiltforskriften § 2 no. 1).
  - Signs not in force shall be covered or removed (N300 Krav 1.2.4-1).
  - Variable information boards shall be dark when there is no message (N300 Krav 6.4.39-6).
  - So a dark panel shows no regulation. The 362 limit in force before it **keeps holding**, because 362 ends only by the signs listed in § 8. A dark panel is not an end sign.

## Supplementary panels

- **A permanent 362 may carry only plate 802 "Avstand"** (distance) (N300 Krav 4.5.30-8, high). There are no wet, time, season or vehicle-class plates under a permanent Norwegian speed limit. A number with 802 under it is a pre-warning (see above).
- **Plates that exist for other signs** (Skiltforskriften § 18):
  - 804 "Utstrekning" (length of stretch);
  - 806 "Tid" (hours: black for weekdays, black in brackets for Saturdays, red for Sundays and holidays);
  - 807.x vehicle symbols ("Gjelder ikke"/"Unntatt" for exceptions);
  - 808 "Tekst";
  - 810 "Svingpil" (applies after a turn at the junction).
  - These are not to be used under 362 (Krav 4.5.30-8), but older signs may still carry them (low).
- **812 "Anbefalt fart"** (recommended speed) under a warning triangle is **advisory only**. It is the highest speed the hazard allows in normal conditions (Skiltforskriften § 18). It is not a limit (high).
- **856** is a miniature of the general limit, placed under 556.2 "Automatisk trafikkontroll – strekningsmåling" (average-speed camera) to show the limit on that stretch (Skiltforskriften § 18). In practice it shows 80 (2022 consultation note). Reading it as the limit is correct (high).
- **Temporary 362 at roadworks** may have a **yellow background** (Skiltforskriften § 8, sign 362). It is as binding as a white one (high). Roadworks signing is in N301 (not read). The 2022 consultation on N301 proposed repeating temporary motorway limits at most every 500 m (medium, draft).

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **There is no town-entry sign.** A 727.1 place-name sign means nothing for speed. The built-up 50 is always a 362 "50" (or lower) sign (N300 Krav 4.5.30-1, 8.5.3). A detector class "town_entry" must not set 50 in Norway.
2. **Road signs do not reset the limit.** 502, 503, 504 and 505 leave the 362 in force unchanged (Skiltforskriften § 8). A 362 often stands above the road sign on the same post.
3. **364 means 80** (outside built-up areas only, N300 Krav 4.5.31-1). Inside built-up areas the end of a special limit is a 362 "50". After 90, 100 or 110, the return to 80 is a 362 "80", not a 364.
4. **Allowed values:** 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100 and 110 only (N300 Krav 4.5.30-2, Figure 4.5.30-1, high). A reading of 20, 120 or 130 in Norway is a misread or an unofficial sign (medium: private roads and roadworks were not checked).
5. **Inverted LED signs** (white number on black) and **yellow temporary signs**.
6. **Gantries:** take the sign over the ego lane (§ 2 no. 2).
7. **Pre-warnings** (362 with 802) and **advisory 812 plates** must not become the limit.
8. **Ignore 367/369**, the 6 km/h zone for e-scooters.
9. **Seasonal limits** exist only locally (Oslo, 1 November to early May, 60 km/h). They are posted as signs, so the camera just reads them. How they are physically signed (VMS or swapped plates) was not found.

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:** `"NO": {"town": 50, "rural": 80, "expressway": 80, "motorway": 80}`. Not None: Norway's general limits apply on every road, motorways included. The motorway and motor-traffic road have no limit of their own and are always signed above 80 (or 80, 70).
  - **Extra condition needed:** in NO, ROAD_SIGNS (`motorway_start`/`_end`, `expressway_start`/`_end`) must only set `self.road` and **never replace the held limit**. Otherwise a 502 seen after a 362 "110" would drop the limit to 80, against Skiltforskriften § 8.
  - `town_entry`/`town_exit` have no Norwegian counterpart. A place-name sign must not set 50 (N300 8.5.3).
  - Map 364 → 80. Map 368, 542 and 550 alone → 50 (medium). Map 366 → 30. Map 540/548 → walking pace: no number, so treat as "no new limit" or a separate key.
- **Intersection rule: partly.** A 362 does **not** end at junctions on the same road: Skiltforskriften § 8 (sign 362) overrides § 7 no. 2, and N300 4.5.30 says "særskilte fartsgrenser oppheves ikke av kryss" (high). It does end in effect when the car turns onto another road: the limit belongs to the stretch, and N300 Krav 4.5.31-3 puts no end sign on side roads (medium). The code's rule "the map's new limit wins when the car changes road" fits Norway. A timeout on a held limit should be long, because repeaters are up to 5 km apart (N300 Krav 4.5.30-13).
- **Blank variable sign:** no regulation is shown. Keep the held limit (medium; no explicit text found).
- **Other rules:**
  - Ignore numbers with plate 802 (pre-warning), 812 (advisory) and 367 (e-scooter).
  - Accept yellow and inverted signs.
  - Lane-specific gantries apply to their lane.
  - When towing a trailer, cap the limit at 80 where the sign shows more than 80 (Trafikkreglene § 13 no. 4), unless the driver says the combination is "Tempo 100" (up to 100).

## Open points

1. **Speed on an unsigned ramp,** and whether a ramp's 362 ends at the merge: no text found. The interpretation above is low certainty.
2. **Dark or blank speed VMS:** no explicit rule. "Keep the held limit" is an inference (medium).
3. **Oslo miljøfartsgrense and other local winter limits:** how they are physically signed (VMS or seasonally swapped plates) was not found. No list of other seasonal stretches was found.
4. **Look of 502–505 and 366/368:** the exact pictograms and end-sign marks were not verified in Norwegian text, because the regulation gives only images. The blue colour and the sizes are verified.
5. **What 368, 542 and 550 restore:** the law says the general limit applies. Taking it as 50 (zones lie in built-up areas) is medium certainty.
6. **Roadworks:** current N301 rules (temporary values, repetition, end of roadworks limits) were not read. Only the 2022 draft was seen.
7. **Older signs:** fixed 362 signs with time or vehicle plates put up before N300's "802 only" rule may still stand. How common they are was not found.
8. **Case law:** no Norwegian court ruling was found on 362 and junctions, or on drivers entering a road with a special limit.
