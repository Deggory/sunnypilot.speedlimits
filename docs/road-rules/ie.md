# Ireland (IE): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Road Traffic Act 2004 (No. 44 of 2004), Part 2, ss. 2, 4–12, as revised by the Law Reform Commission (stated "up to date with all changes known to be in force" as of 13 October 2026), including the sections inserted by the Road Traffic and Roads Act 2023 (ss. 9A, 10A, 10B–10E) and the Road Traffic Act 2024 (s. 6 of the 2004 Act, in force 7 February 2025); Road Traffic (Traffic Signs) Regulations 2025 (S.I. No. 433 of 2025), regs 13, 33–37 and Schedule 3, as amended by S.I. No. 205 of 2026; Department of Transport Traffic Signs Manual (TSM) Chapters 3, 4, 5 and 6; Guidelines for Setting and Managing Speed Limits in Ireland, Chapter 7 (September 2025).

Certainty tags: **high** = the text of the Act or the regulations says it; **medium** = a direct reading of those texts, or the TSM, the guidelines or an official secondary source (Citizens Information, TII, RSA); **low** = an inference with no text that says it.

Limits are in km/h only. Every Irish fixed speed sign carries "km/h" beneath the figures (S.I. 433/2025 reg 33(1)), with three exceptions where it is optional: the periodic sign RUS 045 (reg 34(2)), the electronic sign RVMS 102 (reg 36(2)) and the 20 inside the shared-zone sign RUS 070/070A (Schedule 3 Part 1 note 5) (high). Ireland went metric on 20 January 2005 (Parts 1 and 2 of the 2004 Act, S.I. No. 8 of 2005, medium). No mph sign in the Republic was found. Northern Ireland, across the land border, signs in mph.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

Limits follow the legal class of the road (motorway, national, regional, local) and the legal "built-up area". They do not follow what the road looks like, so a camera cannot infer them from the scene alone.

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Built-up area, any public road except a motorway | 50 | "Built up area" means "the area of a city, a borough or a town within the meaning of the Local Government Act 2001" (s. 2). It is a legal boundary, not lights, houses or a place-name sign (high). The limit gives way to a special or road-works limit (s. 5(2), high). A change to **30** is enacted (Road Traffic Act 2024 s. 6) but **not commenced** (high). Meanwhile councils are bringing in 30 by bye-law, with an aim of 31 March 2027 (medium). | RTA 2004 ss. 2, 5; Citizens Information |
| Local road outside a built-up area | 60 | 80 until 6 February 2025, 60 from 7 February 2025 (RTA 2024 s. 7, S.I. 616/2024) (high). | RTA 2004 s. 6(2), (4) |
| Regional road outside a built-up area | 80 | | RTA 2004 s. 6(1), (3) |
| National road outside a built-up area (single or dual carriageway) | 100 | A change to **80** for national roads other than national primary roads, and a new s. 7A keeping national primary roads at 100, are enacted (RTA 2024 ss. 8, 9) but **not commenced** (high). | RTA 2004 s. 7 |
| Motorway | 120 | Applies to "all motorways", including those inside a built-up area (high). | RTA 2004 s. 8 |
| "Expressway" | no such legal class | Ireland has no expressway or motor-road class and no sign for one. A grade-separated national dual carriageway is a national road (100 by default). A bye-law may raise a national dual carriageway to 120 (s. 9(2)) (high). | RTA 2004 ss. 7, 9(2) |

Other conditions:
- **Wet road, night, visibility, season:** no numeric rule in Part 2 of the 2004 Act or in S.I. 433/2025 (medium: not found).
- **Novice drivers:** no lower limit found. Learner drivers may not use motorways (medium, Citizens Information).
- **Vehicle classes:** ordinary speed limits are set by regulations under s. 4 (S.I. 546/2008, as amended by S.I. 255/2022; not read directly). Per Citizens Information (medium):
  - a vehicle towing a trailer or caravan: 80 on every road, motorways included;
  - coaches: 100 on motorways and dual carriageways, 80 elsewhere;
  - buses with standing passengers: 65;
  - trucks over 3,500 kg drawing another vehicle: 90 on motorways only, 80 elsewhere.
- **Values a special limit may take** (s. 9(2), high): 20, 30 and 40 per Ministerial guidelines (s. 10D); 50 except in a built-up area; 60 anywhere; 80 on a motorway, national road, local road or built-up road; 100 on a motorway, non-urban regional or local road, or built-up road; 120 on a non-motorway national dual carriageway. Fixed signs exist for 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100 and 120 only (Schedule 3 Part 1, high).

## Road kinds and their signs

Sign numbers are from S.I. 433/2025. Placement is from the TSM.

- **Speed limit signs** (reg 33, Schedule 3 Part 1, high): a white disc, red border, black figures, "km/h" in black beneath.
  - RUS 039 = 120, RUS 040 = 100, RUS 041 = 80, RUS 042 = 60, RUS 043 = 50, RUS 064 = 40, RUS 044 = 30, RUS 065 = 20.
  - The same signs mark default, special and road-works limits (reg 33(3); TSM 5.16.10).
  - A sign may stand alone, on a backing board, or as part of a larger sign (Schedule 3 Part 1 note 3).
  - Repeaters may be smaller (900 down to 300 mm, note 2).
- **Rural speed limit sign, RUS 042 alternative (TSM "RUS 042A")** (reg 33(4)–(5), high): a white disc with a black border and black diagonal parallel lines, **no number**. It means the local roads limit, **60**, or a 60 special limit on a local road in a built-up area. TSM 5.16.1a: used on Local Tertiary and minor Local Secondary roads where they meet a road with a higher limit or an urban road, and only with plate P 080 "SLOW / GO MALL" (medium). Not permitted at road works (TSM Table 5.9, medium).
  - **History that matters for labels** (high): under S.I. 200/2024 reg 29(4)–(5) the same diagonal disc was the alternative to RUS 041 and meant **80**. S.I. 618/2024 moved it to RUS 042 (60) from 7 February 2025. Footage before that date shows the same disc meaning 80.
- **Motorway** (TSM 4.4.23–4.4.29, medium; these are information signs, the limit comes from s. 8):
  - F 330 Start of Motorway (blue, white motorway symbol), on the mainline and on both sides of merge slip roads at the start of the slip, "even if the motorway order does not start precisely at that point";
  - F 331 Motorway Prohibitions; F 332 Motorway Ahead;
  - F 333 End of Motorway, on the mainline and on diverge slip roads;
  - F 334 End of Motorway Ahead, at 1 km and 500 m.
- **National, regional, local roads:** no class sign. The class shows only in the route number on direction signs and markers (N, R, L) (medium). Green direction signs mean a national road; white ones a regional or local road (TSM 4.2.2–4.2.3, medium).
- **Built-up area:** no legal entry sign. Town and village name signs are information signs (green on national roads, white on regional and local roads) with no regulatory meaning (TSM 4.2.2–4.2.3, medium). The 50 or lower limit is marked by an RUS 043/044 terminal sign where the bye-law or boundary puts it (medium).
- **Light rail:** RUS 033, a white diamond with a black border and black figures, applies to trams only (reg 87, high).
- **Border and gateways:** F 400 "Speed Limits in km/h" faces traffic entering the State from Northern Ireland, and at ports and airports (TSM 4.5.3, medium).

## How long a speed sign holds

- **The limit belongs to a stretch of road, not to a sign** (high). A default applies to a whole road class (ss. 5–8). A special limit applies to "any specified public road or specified part … or specified carriageway or lane" described in the bye-law (s. 9(1); s. 9A for national managed roads). A road-works limit applies to the road or part in the order (s. 10(1), s. 10A(1)).
- **Signs mark the ends of that stretch.** Terminal signs go on both sides "at the location described in the relevant bye-law or Road Works Speed Limit Order". The bye-law describes "a complete road or … a point a specified distance from some feature" (TSM 5.16.13, medium). So a sign holds until the next terminal sign, the end of the described stretch, or a change of road.
- **No junction rule in the Act** (high: none in ss. 5–12). Junctions do not end a limit on the same road. TSM 5.16.14–5.16.15 and 5.16.17 say limits "often change at road junctions" and recommend a repeater soon after the start, and after an intersection with a road with a lower limit (medium).
- **Repeaters** (TSM Table 5.9 and 5.16.16–5.16.24, medium):
  - 30 and 40: about every 500 m;
  - 50: about 500 m, special limits only; not normally used where 50 is the built-up default (5.16.24);
  - 60: 1 km; 80: 2 km; 100: 3 km;
  - 120: 5 km; not normally needed on motorways and 120 dual carriageways (5.16.18).
- **Periodic limits** hold only while the RUS 045 sign is lit (Schedule 3 Part 2 note 2, high).
- **Variable limits** hold "for the period during which it is displayed" (s. 10B(2), high).
- **Road-works limits** last for the works, at most 12 months per order (s. 10(2), s. 10A(2), high).
- **Precedence** (high): a special limit displaces the default (ss. 5(2), 6(3)–(4), 7(2), 8(2)). A road-works order displaces a special limit (s. 9(11)). A displayed variable limit displaces a bye-law limit on a national managed road (s. 9A(7)).

## End signs

- **No "end of speed limit" or derestriction sign** exists in Schedule 3 (medium: none found in S.I. 433/2025). A limit ends at the terminal sign that shows the next limit. At the end of a periodic section, permanent signs show the limit beyond, often on the back of the RUS 045 (TSM 5.16.26–5.16.32, medium).
- **F 333 End of Motorway** (TSM 4.4.27–4.4.29, medium). After it the road is usually a national road (default 100), or a special limit is signed. Not "rural 80" as in the code's default mapping (low: no text says what follows; it depends on the road class).
- **RUS 070A End of Shared Zone** (reg 13(2), high).
- **RUS 068A End of Cycle Street** (reg 7(3), high). A speed limit for a cycle street was not found.
- **Slow Zone exit:** speed limit signs on the reverse of the entry signs (Guidelines Chapter 7, 7.3, medium).
- **Plate P 010 "End"** exists, but its use with speed signs was not found (it is used with cycle-track signs, TSM 5.18.8).

## Zones

- **Shared zone, RUS 070** (reg 13, high): a blue rectangle with pedestrian, pedal cycle and vehicle symbols, containing an RUS 065 (20) roundel, which may omit "km/h". Ends with RUS 070A.
- **Slow Zone, F 403** (TSM 4.5.7a, medium): an information sign with a 30 roundel, for housing-estate roads. The legal limit is a 30 special limit by bye-law. Guidelines 7.3: entry and exit are gateways; leaving, speed signs stand on the reverse of the entry signs (medium).
- **Urban 30** (Guidelines Chapter 7, 7.3, medium): 30 in the urban core, housing estates and residential streets; 20 in pedestrian and shared zones; 40 or 50 on arterial and link roads; 60 in transition zones. Applied by s. 9 bye-laws being adopted county by county in 2026 (e.g. South Dublin consultation; Waterford adoption by 31 October 2026) (medium).
- **School zones:** periodic special limits under s. 9(5) ("for a specified period or periods during any day or during specified days") with the lit sign RUS 045 (reg 34, high). Typical use is at schools. Values 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80 or 100. "At periods when the speed limit is not in operation, the sign shall remain unilluminated" (TSM 5.16.26–5.16.32, medium). School times are in Guidelines Table 7.8 (medium).
- **School Street, RUS 032A** (reg 31, high): an access restriction (no entry with a time plate), not a speed limit.
- **Cycle Street, RUS 068** (reg 7, high): no speed value found.

## Variable message signs

- **Legal basis** (high): s. 10B (inserted 31 July 2023 by RTRA 2023 s. 56).
  - TII may apply a variable limit by automated means on any national managed road, part, carriageway or lane, "by displaying the speed limit … electronically on a traffic sign above or to the side" of it (s. 10B(1)).
  - The limit must be at least 20 and no more than the limit that otherwise applies (s. 10B(1)(a)–(b)).
  - Values: 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100, 110, 120; different lanes and carriageways may have different limits (s. 10B(3)).
  - It "is in force for the period during which it is displayed" (s. 10B(2)).
  - Any other displayed value "shall have no effect and the speed limit provided or having effect under this Act shall apply" (s. 10B(7)).
  - Schemes: s. 10C (whether a scheme for the M50 has been made: not found).
- **National managed roads** (high): defined in RTRA 2023 s. 48. Designated so far: the N50 (M50) and parts of the N1/M1 and N11/M11 (S.I. 688/2024), and part of the N4 (S.I. 207/2026).
- **M50** (medium): variable limits since October 2021 under TII's eMOS programme, extended to J4–J9 on 12 May 2022. "When the digital signs are switched off, the normal limit applies" (AA Ireland, secondary). Normal limit on the M50: 100, and 120 south of J14 (AA Ireland, medium).
- **Sign types** (S.I. 433/2025, high):
  - **RVMS 102** electronic speed limit sign (reg 36): red border, black disc, white figures, "km/h" optional. Applies to the road, part, carriageway or lane it is provided for. May show only the limits in Part 2 of the Act. Not limited to national managed roads. TSM 5.16.3–5.16.6: may carry plate P 054 "km/h"; 70, 90 and 110 appear only in VSL zones (medium).
  - **RVMS 100** tunnel speed limit sign (reg 35): a black square that, when lit, shows a red ring and white or yellow figures, with a fixed "km/h" plate. Where it is used, fixed speed signs are not required in the tunnel (reg 35(4)).
  - **RVMS 101** lane control signals (reg 37): green down arrow, red X, diagonal arrows.
  - **RVMS 104** (added by S.I. 205/2026): bus priority on the hard shoulder of a national managed road, with a speed limit roundel (e.g. 60). For buses in that lane only (medium).
- **Gantries** (TSM 5.16.33–5.16.41, medium): lane limits are centred over each lane on backing boards that are blue on motorways, green on national roads and white elsewhere; roadside lane signs are an alternative. In a VSL zone, "fixed plate speed limit signs should not be provided" where the electronic signs are displayed (5.16.40).
- **Vehicle-activated signs** (TSM 3.8, medium): blank when not triggered, may show the vehicle's speed, "shall not depict Regulatory Signs" (except RUS 011). A fixed roundel beside a VAS may show the posted limit. A VAS is not a speed-limit repeater.

## Supplementary panels

- **P 080 "SLOW / GO MALL"**: under the RUS 042 alternative (diagonal disc) only (TSM 5.16.1a, medium).
- **P 054 "km/h"**: under RVMS 102 (TSM 5.16.3–5.16.6, medium).
- **P 051 Periods of Operation**: days and times, used with bus lanes (TSM 5.19.11). Its use under a fixed speed sign was not found; periodic limits use the lit RUS 045 instead (medium).
- **P 011 Cautionary Speed**: an **advisory** speed, only under W 033 (loop) or W 134 (slippery road), and at road works (TSM 6.3.7, 6.24.14–6.24.15, medium). Not a limit.
- **F 401 Speed Limit Ahead**: a rectangular information sign with a roundel (30, 40, 50, 60, 80 or 100) and a distance (TSM 4.5.4–4.5.6, medium). Not a limit until the terminal sign.
- **Wet, season, night, vehicle-class or "trailer" plates under a speed sign:** not found in S.I. 433/2025 or TSM Chapter 5 (medium).

## Special cases for a camera system

- **"km/h" under the figures** is the Irish norm and a cue for the country. Northern Ireland signs show mph with no unit; near the border the country must come from GPS, not the sign (a NI "60" is 60 mph ≈ 97 km/h).
- **The diagonal disc has no number.** It means 60 since 7 February 2025 and meant 80 before (see above). Train it as its own class and map it by country and date.
- **Ignore for the limit:**
  - F 401 Speed Limit Ahead (has a distance);
  - P 011 advisory plates;
  - VAS "your speed" displays and "slow down" messages;
  - Luas tram diamonds RUS 033;
  - RVMS 104 bus-lane roundels, unless the car is a bus in that lane;
  - unlit RUS 045 (a black disc): no periodic limit in force.
- **Lane-specific limits:** gantry signs apply to the lane below; roadside lane signs and service roads may differ from the main carriageway (TSM 5.16.33–5.16.37, medium).
- **Slip roads:** merges, diverges, slip roads and loops on motorways may have their own special limit of 20–100 (Guidelines 7.1, medium). That limit applies only to the slip-road section in the bye-law; after the merge the main carriageway's limit applies (medium, from s. 9(1) "specified part … or specified carriageway"). Ramps have 750 (900) mm signs (TSM Table 5.9).
- **No fixed signs in VSL zones:** when the gantries are blank there may be nothing to read; keep the default for the class (or the map).
- **Older mph signs:** after metrication an old mph sign was "regarded as indicating the new speed limit" (s. 12(3): 30 mph → 50, 40 mph → 60, 50 mph → 80) until replaced. Historical only; none known to stand today (low).
- **Town name signs** carry no limit; do not use them as `town_entry`/`town_exit`.

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:** `"IE": {"town": 50, "rural": 80, "expressway": 100, "motorway": 120}`.
  - `rural` 80 is the regional-road default only. Local roads are 60 and national roads 100 (ss. 6, 7). The code needs the road class from the map: OSM `ref` starting with L/R/N, or `highway` = `unclassified`/`residential` (local), `secondary` (regional), `primary`/`trunk` (national). Suggested extra keys: `"rural_local": 60, "rural_regional": 80, "rural_national": 100`.
  - `expressway` 100: no legal class; use it for a grade-separated national dual carriageway the road-type network calls "expressway".
  - Map the RUS 042 alternative (diagonal disc) → 60 (80 before 2025-02-07).
  - `town_entry`/`town_exit`: no Irish counterpart with legal effect. Town name signs must not set 50 or end it.
  - `motorway_end` (F 333) → in IE the next road is usually national (100), not `rural` 80. Needs a per-country override, or "hold the next sign / map".
  - `motorway_start` (F 330) → 120 (s. 8), unless a sign shows otherwise.
  - Valid fixed values: {20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100, 120}. Electronic signs in VSL zones add {70, 90, 110}.
  - When RTA 2024 ss. 6, 8, 9 commence: `town` → 30, `rural_national` → 80 except national primary roads (100). Not in force on 2026-10-29.
- **Intersection rule: partly** (no junction rule in RTA 2004 Part 2). A limit belongs to the road, part, carriageway or lane named by the class (ss. 5–8) or the bye-law (s. 9(1), s. 9A(1)). Going straight through a junction the held sign stays until the next terminal sign (TSM 5.16.13–5.16.17). Turning onto another road, that road's own class or bye-law limit applies; the map's limit should win. Certainty: medium. The code's `MAP_SECONDS` junction hand-over fits a turn, but not a straight crossing.
- **Blank variable sign:** a variable limit is in force only "for the period during which it is displayed" (s. 10B(2), high). Blank = the default or special limit beneath applies. In a VSL zone there may be no fixed sign (TSM 5.16.40), so fall back to the map or class default. An unlit RUS 045 = no periodic limit in force. A displayed value outside s. 10B(3) has no effect (s. 10B(7)).
- **Other rules:**
  - Repeaters can be up to 5 km apart (3 km at 100), so a held-limit timeout must be long.
  - Towing a trailer: cap at 80 on every road, motorways included (medium).
  - No wet, night or seasonal limits to model.
  - Learner drivers are banned from motorways; no novice limit.
