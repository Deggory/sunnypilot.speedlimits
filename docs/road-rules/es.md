# Spain (ES): speed rules for the comma

Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Reglamento General de Circulación (RGC, Real Decreto 1428/2003; consolidated text,
last update published 26/06/2026), arts. 45-52 (speed) and Title IV with Anexo I (signs), as rewritten by
Real Decreto 465/2025 (new Catálogo oficial de señales, in force 01/07/2025) ([RGC][rgc]; [RD 465/2025][rd465]).
Ley sobre Tráfico, Circulación de Vehículos a Motor y Seguridad Vial (texto refundido, Real Decreto Legislativo
6/2015), arts. 21 and 54 and Annex I (definitions) ([LSV][lsv]). The urban 20/30/50 limits come from Real Decreto
970/2020, in force 11/05/2021 ([RGC art. 50 note][rgc50]).

Every link below was read on 2026-10-29. Certainty marks apply to interpretations only: **high** means the text says
it, **medium** means it follows from the text but is not stated in those words, **low** means it is an educated guess.
"Not found" means it could not be verified.

Sign codes are those of the new catalogue (RGC Anexo I as rewritten by RD 465/2025) unless "old" is stated. The old
catalogue (RGC arts. 149-173 before 01/07/2025) was read in the BOE version in force from 21/03/2022
([RGC 2021 version][rgc21]). The new catalogue is published only as drawings inside the BOE PDF
([RD 465/2025 PDF][rd465pdf]). Its sign definitions were read by extracting the text from that PDF; the colours of the
drawings were **not** read, so colour statements below are marked as general knowledge.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

Car = "turismo" (and motorcycle, camper ≤ 3.5 t) in RGC art. 48.1.a. "Poblado" (built-up area) is the space between
the S-500 entry and the S-510 exit signs ([LSV Annex I, 70][lsvan]).

Other conditions:

- Wet road, night, fog, snow: no numeric general limit. The driver must moderate speed (art. 46.1.g and 46.1.k);
  numbers come only from signs, for example with weather panels S-890a/b/c (see below) ([RGC art. 46][rgc46]).
  A numeric wet or night limit: not found.
- Novice drivers: art. 52.1.c allows prevailing limits "a los conductores noveles", but no current text sets a number
  ([RGC art. 52][rgc52]). The old 80 km/h novice limit was removed around 2011, according to a web search summary of
  secondary sources (certainty medium; primary text of the removal not found).
- Overtaking: the old +20 km/h margin for overtaking on conventional roads (art. 51) was repealed by Ley 18/2021 from
  21/03/2022 ([RGC art. 51][rgc51]).
- Vehicle-specific reductions that do not concern a normal car: −10 km/h for school transport and dangerous goods
  (art. 48.1.b, amended by RD 518/2026 from 01/10/2026), 70 km/h for three-wheelers and quadricycles (art. 48.1.h)
  ([RGC art. 48][rgc48]).
- Signed limits prevail over the generic ones (art. 52.1.a); in the absence of a specific sign the generic limit of
  the road applies (art. 47; LSV art. 21) ([RGC art. 47][rgc47]; [RGC art. 52][rgc52]; [LSV art. 21][lsv21]).

Look of the signs (general knowledge, **not** read from the catalogue drawings; certainty low to medium):

- S-1 and S-1a are blue square signs with a white pictogram (two carriageways under a bridge for the autopista; a
  similar pictogram for the autovía). S-2/S-2a and S-4 repeat the pictogram with a red diagonal bar. S-3 shows a white
  car on blue.
- S-500 is a white rectangular panel with the town name in dark letters; S-510 repeats it with a red diagonal bar.
- Direction signs on autopistas and autovías have a blue background; on conventional roads a white one.

How a camera can tell motorway from expressway:

- Spain has no legally separate "expressway" with its own generic speed. Autopista and autovía share the 120 limit
  (art. 48.1.a), so S-1 and S-1a both mean "motorway" for the comma (high).
- S-3 (vía para automóviles), S-1b (multicarril) and S-1c (2+1) do not raise the limit above 90 (medium, see the table).
- Where the sign stands: at the start of the carriageway, and possibly on the entry ramp of an interchange (high, sign
  definition above). So an S-1/S-1a seen on a ramp already means motorway rules for that ramp (medium).
- Road markings M-8.1 (dientes de dragón, "dragon's teeth" at the lane edges) warn of an approaching travesía or urban
  road where speed must drop markedly ([new catalogue, PDF][rd465pdf]; high). They are a cue that a town or a lower
  limit is near, not a limit.

## How long a speed sign holds

The definition of R-301 Velocidad máxima in the new catalogue (same wording as old RGC art. 154,
[RGC 2021 version, art. 154][rgc21_154]) ([new catalogue, PDF][rd465pdf]):

> "Prohibición de circular a velocidad superior, expresada en kilómetros por hora, a la indicada en la señal. Obliga
> desde el lugar en que esté situada hasta la próxima señal «Fin de limitación de velocidad», de «Fin de prohibiciones»
> u otra de «Velocidad máxima», salvo que esté colocada en el mismo poste que una señal de advertencia de peligro o en el
> mismo panel que esta, en cuyo caso la prohibición finaliza cuando termine el peligro señalado. Situada en una vía sin
> prioridad, deja de tener vigencia al salir de una intersección con una vía con prioridad. Si el límite indicado por la
> señal coincide con la velocidad máxima permitida para el tipo de vía, recuerda de forma genérica la prohibición de
> superarla."

So (high unless stated):

- A speed sign holds until R-501 (end of speed limit), R-500 (end of all prohibitions) or another R-301.
- It does **not** end at every intersection. It ends only when it stands on a road **without priority** and the
  driver leaves an intersection with a road **with priority**.
- On the same post or panel as a danger sign it ends where the danger ends; a length panel S-810 can give that length
  (medium for the S-810 link).
- An R-301 showing the road's own generic limit (for example 120 on an autopista) is only a reminder.
- Start: from the cross-section where the sign stands, unless a distance panel below says it starts further on
  ([RGC art. 145.4][rgc145]).
- Town-entry case: regulatory signs placed beside or above the town-name sign apply to the whole poblado, except where
  other signs regulate certain stretches ([RGC art. 145.3][rgc145]). This is wider than the intersection clause above
  (medium: the text does not say which wins; art. 145.3 is the more specific rule).
- A speed number painted on the road (M-6.6 outside towns, M-6.7 in travesías and urban roads) binds only its lane when
  that lane is bounded by longitudinal lines, and holds "hasta la siguiente señal de «Fin de limitación», «Fin de
  limitación de velocidad» u otra señal de velocidad máxima de valor diferente" ([new catalogue, PDF][rd465pdf]). The
  marking text has no intersection clause.
- Repeated after junctions: no rule requiring repetition was found (**not found**). Whether a sign seen on one road
  binds after turning into another road: the text speaks of the road where the sign stands; no explicit rule found
  (interpretation: it does not carry over, medium).

Ramps and slip roads:

- A specific rule for ramps: **not found**.
- Entry ramp: the ramp joining a motorway is the road without priority: a driver joining from an access road must give
  way to the traffic on it (art. 72.1) ([RGC art. 72][rgc72]). Read with the R-301 intersection clause, an R-301 on an entry ramp would end once the car has
  merged (medium-low: a merge at an interchange is not clearly an "intersección"). An S-1/S-1a on the ramp makes
  motorway rules apply from the ramp (medium).
- Exit ramp: panel S-870a/S-870b under a sign says it applies **only** to the exit branch in the direction of the
  arrow ([new catalogue, PDF][rd465pdf]; high). Without that panel, a sign beside the main carriageway applies to the
  main carriageway (medium).
- A limit on the main carriageway and traffic entering from a ramp after the sign: by the R-301 text the sign binds
  from where it stands, so a car joining after it was never passed by it. No text found on this case (**not found**;
  interpretation: the joining car is bound from the next sign it passes, low).
- Motorway interchanges (nudos): no specific speed rule found (**not found**).

Case law: no Tribunal Supremo or Tribunal Constitucional ruling on the extent of a speed sign was found (**not found**).
A motoring article summarises the same rules (until R-501/R-500/R-301; the intersection clause; the danger-sign case;
painted limits bind their lane) ([Diariomotor, 30 Aug 2022][dm]).

## Variable message signs

- Legal status: binding. Temporary speed limits can be set by the traffic authority (Jefatura Central de Tráfico, or
  the regional or local authority) "mediante la correspondiente señalización circunstancial o variable" (art. 47).
  The traffic authority runs circumstantial and variable signalling and decides the messages of variable message
  panels (art. 139.2). Circumstantial signalling (with balizamiento) ranks above traffic lights, fixed signs and road
  markings; between signs of the same type the more restrictive wins (art. 133; LSV art. 54) ([RGC art. 47][rgc47];
  [RGC art. 139][rgc139]; [RGC art. 133][rgc133]; [LSV art. 54][lsv54]). High.
- How long it holds: "Las modificaciones que la señalización circunstancial introduce respecto de la habitual
  señalización vertical y horizontal terminan cuando lo establezca la propia señalización, o las causas que motivaron
  su imposición, momento a partir del cual aquellas vuelven a regir" (art. 142.1; same rule for variable message panels
  in old art. 144.1) ([RGC art. 142][rgc142]; [RGC 2021 version, art. 144][rgc21_144]). High.
- Catalogue messages for VMS include "Restricción temporal de velocidad", "Velocidad recomendada" and "Limitación de
  velocidad máxima por episodio de contaminación"; a panel never shows more than two alternating messages; on a fixed
  or variable panel an inscription may stand beside the sign instead of on a panel below (art. 137)
  ([RGC Anexo I][rgcani]; [RGC art. 137][rgc137]). High.
- Luminous signs may show white symbols on a dark background (art. 145.5) ([RGC art. 145][rgc145]). High.
- Gantries over lanes and lane-specific limits: a sign applies to the whole carriageway, but markings can limit it to
  some lanes (art. 135) ([RGC art. 135][rgc135]). Lane signs S-50 to S-53 carry an R-301 (and R-411 minimum) per lane
  and may omit them when no lane-specific speed is set ([new catalogue, section 2.7][rd465pdf]). For a speed sign over
  one lane on a gantry no explicit text was found; interpretation: it binds that lane (medium).
- Blank or dark panel: **not an end sign** in the law. A circumstantial limit ends when the signalling says so or when
  its cause ends (art. 142.1). The end can therefore come without any visible end sign, which a camera cannot see.
  A motoring article: "cuando el siguiente panel está apagado no quiere decir que elimina el límite anterior"; only
  cancellation signs (on the screen or beside the road) or a new limit cancel it ([Diariomotor, 30 Aug 2022][dm]).
  Certainty medium.
- Red-cross lanes: a red X-shaped light forbids occupying that lane; drivers in it must leave it as soon as possible.
  A green arrow pointing down allows the lane. A white or yellow oblique arrow pointing down (steady or flashing)
  tells users of that lane to merge safely into the lane it points to. These lane signals affect only the lane they stand over, or the lane the panel names
  ([RGC Anexo I, lane-occupation signals][rgcani]; [new catalogue][rd465pdf]). High.
- In practice:
  - Catalonia's traffic authority (Servei Català de Trànsit) has run variable limits on the C-31 and C-32 near
    Barcelona since January 2009. Limits change in steps of 10 km/h, and the overhead panels prevail over the fixed
    signs ([Generalitat 2011 document][gencat]; read only through a web-search summary, medium).
  - A DGT pilot on the AP-7 in Catalonia and on the AP-2 started on 1 October 2026: 120 can drop to 100, 80 or 60, and
    to 40 in fog on the AP-2. The panel limit prevails over the fixed 120 sign, which stays in place
    ([Motor16, 24 Sep 2026][m16]; medium).

## Special cases for a camera system

- R-411 Velocidad mínima is a **blue** round sign with a white number; R-301 is a red ring with a black number. A
  blue round number must never be read as a maximum ([new catalogue][rd465pdf]; colour from general knowledge, medium).
- S-7 Velocidad máxima aconsejada (recommended speed) is a square sign with a number: advisory, not a limit. Its end
  S-8 is not an end of a legal limit. S-9/S-10 are recommended intervals ([new catalogue][rd465pdf]).
- S-940 "Limitaciones de velocidad en España" at border crossings lists the generic limits for each road kind. It is not
  a limit for the current road and must be ignored ([new catalogue][rd465pdf]).
- S-500 can now carry a speed number for that road. Read it as an R-301 for that road (medium).
- Roadworks: R-300 to R-306 (R-301 included) and the end signs R-500 to R-504 have a **yellow** background in
  roadworks; other circumstantial signalling uses **orange** instead of yellow. Obsolete signs are covered with black
  adhesive. A yellow or orange R-301 is circumstantial and ranks above fixed signs (art. 133)
  ([new catalogue, sections 2.5 and 2.6][rd465pdf]; [RGC art. 133][rgc133]).
- Speed numbers painted on the road (M-6.6, M-6.7) bind only their lane when that lane is bounded by lines.
- An R-301 on a danger-sign post ends with the hazard, not at the next R-501.
- Two catalogues coexist until signs are renewed. The old S-28 (20, calle residencial) and old S-30 (Zona a 30) may
  still stand. New S-28 means 10, S-47 means 20.
- Variable limits for pollution episodes exist as a VMS message ([RGC Anexo I][rgcani]); they can appear without
  weather or traffic cues.
- Catalonia and the Basque Country have their own traffic authorities. Variable limits are common on the main roads
  around Barcelona (see Variable message signs).

## For the comma

- IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:

  ```python
  "ES": {"town": 50, "rural": 90, "expressway": 90, "motorway": 120},
  ```

  - `town` 50 is the limit of a travesía (the same road continuing past the S-500) and of urban roads with two or more
    lanes per direction (high). It is wrong for most town streets.
  - Spain needs a lane-count condition in town (art. 50.1): 30 with one lane per direction, 20 on a plataforma única.
    Suggested extra keys: `"town_one_lane": 30, "town_shared": 20`. Use them after the first road change (junction)
    inside the poblado, choosing by the number of lanes per direction from the map (OSM `lanes`, `oneway`). Without
    lane data use 30 after a junction in town: it is the commonest case and the safe side (medium).
  - Autopista or autovía inside a poblado: 80 (art. 50.6). It needs a `motorway` + `town` case: `"motorway_town": 80`
    (high for the number).
  - `motorway` 120 covers both autopista (S-1) and autovía (S-1a) (high).
  - `expressway` 90 is for the vía para automóviles (S-3), which has no row in art. 48 (low). Multicarril (S-1b) and
    2+1 (S-1c) map to `rural` 90 (medium).
  - 100 on conventional roads with physical separation is always set by the road owner, so it comes from a sign, not
    from the implicit table (medium).
  - Car with trailer: 90 motorway, 80 rural. Only if the code knows about a trailer.
  - ROAD_SIGNS additions:

    | Sign | Effect |
    |---|---|
    | S-1, S-1a | Start of motorway |
    | S-2, S-2a | End of motorway: the following road becomes `rural`, or `town` inside a poblado |
    | S-3 | Start of expressway |
    | S-4 | End of expressway: `rural` |
    | S-1b, S-1c, S-2b, S-2c | `rural`, no change of limit |
    | S-500 | Town entry. A number on it is the limit for that road |
    | S-510 | Town exit |
- The intersection rule: **partly**. An R-301 does not end at every intersection. It ends on leaving an intersection
  with a priority road when it stands on a road without priority (R-301 definition in RGC Anexo I, new catalogue by RD
  465/2025; same text as old RGC art. 154). Otherwise it holds until R-501, R-500 or the next R-301, or until the hazard
  ends when it shares a post or panel with a danger sign.

  The Swiss-style reset at every map change (`MAP_SECONDS`) is right for:
  - a car leaving a minor road onto a priority road;
  - turning into another road (medium).

  It is too eager for a car going straight on along the priority road. There the limit should survive the junction
  (high).

  An R-301 beside or above the S-500 applies to the whole poblado (art. 145.3).
- A blank variable sign: not an end sign. Keep the last limit until an end sign, a new limit, or the code's existing
  timeout. The law also lets a circumstantial limit end when its cause ends (art. 142.1), which a camera cannot see, so
  a timeout or the map is the only practical end (medium).
- Other rules the code should know:
  1. Ignore R-411 (blue round minimum), R-506, S-7/S-8/S-9/S-10 (recommended speeds) and S-940 (border summary).
  2. Panels S-870 (exit only), S-880 (vehicle classes) and S-890 (snow/rain/fog) restrict the sign. A plain car in
     normal weather on the main carriageway ignores it.
  3. S-800 (distance) delays the start; S-810 (length) gives the end.
  4. A yellow or orange R-301 (works or circumstantial) beats a white one; among signs of the same type the more
     restrictive wins (art. 133).
  5. Zones: S-28 → 10 (old S-28 → 20), S-47 → 20, old S-30 → 30. S-29/S-48/old S-31 restore the general rules.
  6. A painted M-6.6/M-6.7 binds only its marked lane and has no intersection clause.
  7. On an exit or entry ramp, a limit on the ramp should end once the car is on the main carriageway. This is not
     explicit in the law (medium-low). The code's `RAMP_SECONDS`/`MERGE_SECONDS` logic fits it.

## Open points

- Ramps: no rule says whether an R-301 on an entry ramp ends at the merge, or whether a limit on the main carriageway
  binds a car that joined after the sign. The interpretation above (ends at the merge; joining cars bound from the next
  sign) is medium-low.
- Vía para automóviles (S-3): no generic limit row in art. 48 was found; 90 is an interpretation (low).
- Town default: Spain's urban limit depends on lanes per direction (20/30/50). One number cannot be right; the code
  needs map lane data or a travesía/street distinction.
- Zone 30 after 2025: the new catalogue has no "Zona a 30" sign. Whether the old S-30 is a suppressed sign (to be
  removed by 01/07/2026) or may stay until renewal: not found.
- Blank variable panels: the law ends a circumstantial limit when its cause ends, without requiring a visible end sign.
  The safe reading (keep the limit) rests on a secondary source.
- Lane-specific limits on gantries: no explicit text for a speed sign over a single lane (medium that it binds that
  lane only).
- Case law on the extent of speed signs: not found.
- Colours and look of S-1, S-1a, S-500, S-510 and direction signs: from general knowledge; the catalogue drawings were
  not read as images.
- Novice drivers: no current numeric limit found; the date and act of the removal of the old 80 km/h limit were not
  verified in primary law.
- Regional traffic authorities (Catalonia, Basque Country) run their own variable limits; a list of the roads with
  variable limits was not found.
