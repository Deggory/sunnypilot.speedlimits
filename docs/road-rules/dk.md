# Denmark (DK): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Færdselsloven (Road Traffic Act), consolidated as LBK nr 118 af 12/01/2026 [FL]; Bekendtgørelse om vejafmærkning, BEK nr 425 af 13/04/2023 (design and meaning of signs) [VA]; Bekendtgørelse om anvendelse af vejafmærkning, BEK nr 426 af 13/04/2023 (use and placement of signs) [AVA]; Bekendtgørelse om lokale hastighedsgrænser, BEK nr 3 af 03/01/2022 as amended by BEK nr 1356 af 27/09/2022 [LH]; Bekendtgørelse om afmærkning af vejarbejder, BEK nr 393 af 14/04/2025 [VAR]; Højesteret, judgment of 17 January 2025, case 56/2024 [HR].

All statutory texts were read in full from the official XML on retsinformation.dk; every one was marked "Valid" (in force) on 2026-10-29. Quotes are in Danish, followed by an English gloss. Certainty: **high** = literal text of the law; **medium** = clear reading of the law or an official secondary source; **low** = interpretation or a non-official source.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| In a built-up area ("tættere bebygget område") | 50 km/h | Applies only between the E 55 and E 56 signs. The law defines the area by its signs: "område, hvis grænser tilkendegives ved særlig afmærkning" (an area whose limits are shown by special signs), § 2 nr. 27. It does **not** apply on a motorvej or motortrafikvej, even inside an E 55 area (§ 42 stk. 1 begins "På andre veje end motorveje og motortrafikveje"). | [FL] § 42 stk. 1 nr. 1, § 2 nr. 27 (high) |
| Outside built-up areas | 80 km/h | Every road that is not a motorway or motortrafikvej and is outside an E 55 area. Signs may raise it to 90 ([LH] § 3) or lower it to 40, or to 30 near beaches and forests, possibly only in certain months ([LH] § 8). | [FL] § 42 stk. 1 nr. 2 (high) |
| Motortrafikvej (expressway) | 80 km/h | Signs may raise it to 90 or 100. 100 is allowed only with a central barrier and no level crossings. The legal maximum is 100. | [FL] § 42 stk. 3, stk. 4; [LH] § 4 (high) |
| Motorvej (motorway) | 130 km/h | No sign may raise it above 130 ([LH] § 5). Many stretches are signed 110 with a C 55 sign: the 2004 change to 130 covered only about 55 % of the network ([TRM130], medium). Down to 30 at junctions with the ordinary network, or 60 for noise ([LH] § 9). | [FL] § 42 stk. 2, stk. 4 (high) |
| Higher limits in towns | 60 or 70 by sign | Only by C 55 sign. | [LH] § 2 (high) |
| Lower limits in towns | 40, or 30 and below | By C 55 sign or zone sign (residential areas, play streets, schools). | [LH] § 6 (high) |

Conditions:
- **Wet road, night, visibility:** no numeric limit. The general duty to adapt speed applies, including "vådt, glat eller fedtet føre" (wet, slippery or greasy surface): [FL] § 41 stk. 1–2 (high).
- **Season:** no national seasonal limit. Local seasonal limits are possible on rural roads near beaches, forests and recreational areas, down to 30, "begrænses til de måneder af året, hvor der er behov herfor" (restricted to the months of the year when needed): [LH] § 8 stk. 2 as amended by BEK 1356/2022 (high).
- **Novice drivers:** no separate speed limit. Neither Chapter 5 of [FL] nor borger.dk mentions one [BORGER] (medium: absence of a rule).
- **Trailers and heavy vehicles** (the comma cannot know this; for information only):
  - car up to 3,500 kg with a trailer or caravan: 80 everywhere, 100 on a motorway if the vehicle is approved for it ([FL] § 43 stk. 3–4);
  - buses over 3,500 kg: 80, 100 on a motorway if approved (§ 43 stk. 1);
  - lorries over 3,500 kg: 80 (§ 43 stk. 2) (high).
- **Border sign:** E 80 "Generelle hastighedsbegrænsninger" shows the national limits at borders, ferry ports and international airports ([VA] § 27; [AVA] § 157) (high).

## Road kinds and their signs

| Kind | Begins | Ends | Look (see the colour note below) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Built-up area | **E 55** "Tættere bebygget område" | **E 56** "Ophør af tættere bebygget område" | E 55: white rectangle with a black town-silhouette symbol, optionally with the town name. E 56: identical, with a red diagonal bar ([AVA] § 139 stk. 1: "identiske bortset fra den røde streg om ophør", identical except for the red end bar). Not combined with other signs (§ 139 stk. 4). |
| Motortrafikvej | **E 43** "Motortrafikvej" | **E 45** "Motortrafikvej ophører" | Blue rectangle with a white car symbol. E 45 adds a red bar. |
| Motorvej | **E 42** "Motorvej" | **E 44** "Motorvej ophører" | Green rectangle with the white motorway symbol. E 44 adds a red bar. The supplementary panels of E 42 and E 44 have a green background ([VA] § 9 stk. 2, high). |

Meaning:
- E 42 marks the stretch where the motorway rules apply, together with its on- and off-ramps ("til- og frakørselsveje"), under [FL] §§ 44–47. E 43 does the same for motortrafikvej under [FL] § 48: [VA] § 27 (high). The law defines both road kinds by their signs: "vej ... som med færdselstavler er afmærket som henholdsvis motorvej og motortrafikvej" (a road signed as motorway or motortrafikvej), [FL] § 2 nr. 17 (high).

Where the signs stand ([AVA] §§ 127–130, high):
- **E 42** stands where the motorway continues the ordinary road, and **at the start of each on-ramp**. If there is no reasonable alternative route there, it is announced with a distance panel at the junction before (§ 127). For the camera, an E 42 with a distance panel is advance notice, not the start of the motorway.
- **E 43** stands where the motortrafikvej begins **and again after every junction** with a road or path open to traffic that may not use it (§ 128). A Danish motortrafikvej can therefore have level junctions, and the E 43 sign is repeated after each one.
- **E 44** stands where the motorway ends and is announced **400 m before**. At exits it is used "kun ... hvor det ikke tydeligt fremgår, at motorvejen forlades" (only where it is not obvious that the motorway is being left). It stands on both sides (§ 129).
- **E 45** is announced **200 m before**. On side roads and exits it is used only where leaving is not obvious (§ 130).

**Telling motorway from motortrafikvej by camera:**
- Motorway signs are **green** (E 42/E 44); motortrafikvej signs are **blue** with a car symbol (E 43/E 45).
- A motortrafikvej may have level crossings, with E 43 repeated after each one.

Sources for the look: the colours and symbols are not in the regulation text, which shows them only as images. They were taken from the Wikimedia Commons SVGs of the Danish signs [COMMONS]:
- E42 and E44: fill `#00ab67`, green;
- E43 and E45: fill `#007ac2`, blue;
- E44 and E45: red bar `#ed171f`.

Certainty: medium. Check against Vejdirektoratet's sign catalogue, which [VA] § 1 stk. 2 refers to.

**Exits without an end sign:** leaving a motorway or motortrafikvej by an ordinary exit usually shows **no** E 44 or E 45 ([AVA] §§ 129–130). The code must switch the road kind by itself when the road-type network sees the exit ramp and then a non-motorway road (high, from the placement rule).

## How long a speed sign holds

**C 55 "Lokal hastighedsbegrænsning" (local speed limit).** Red ring, white disc, black number. The meaning in [VA] § 19, quoted literally (high):
> "Tavlen fastsætter en lokal hastighedsbegrænsning for en strækning på den aktuelle vej, jf. færdselslovens § 42. Hastighedsbegrænsningen gælder uanset bestemmelsen i § 18, stk. 2, indtil den ophæves ved ophørstavle, ved afstandsangivelse på undertavle eller ved angivelse af anden hastighedsbegrænsning."

In English: the sign sets a local limit for a stretch of the current road. Notwithstanding § 18 stk. 2, the limit holds until it is cancelled by an end sign, by a distance on a supplementary panel, or by another speed limit.

- **§ 18 stk. 2** is the general rule for prohibition signs: they hold "frem til næste vejkryds" (until the next junction) unless ended earlier or a panel says otherwise. **C 55 is expressly excluded**, so a speed limit does **not** end at the next junction (high).
- **Supreme Court, 17 January 2025, case 56/2024** [HR]. A car was measured at 101 km/h after a signal-controlled junction on Ishøj Stationsvej, inside a 70 km/h C 55 stretch. The prosecution argued that the 70 limit had ended at the junction. Højesteret held that "en lokal hastighedsbegrænsning ikke ophæves, blot fordi der er et vejkryds på vejstrækningen" (a local speed limit is not cancelled just because there is a junction on the stretch). It ends only by an end sign, a distance panel or another limit. Certainty: high for the holding. It was read from the court's summary page and search extracts, because domstol.dk returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches.
- **No automatic expiry by distance.** No legal rule makes a sign lapse after N metres. The repetition intervals in the non-binding Vejregler (road design guidelines) are guidance only: about every 500 m in towns, 800 m outside towns and 1,500 m on motorways [ING2016] (medium; secondary source quoting the Vejregler).
- **Mandatory repeats** (rules for the road authority, not a legal end of the limit; [AVA] § 97, high):
  - after every significant side road, at most 50 m after the junction, or up to 150 m if the side road has a lower limit (stk. 1);
  - after acceleration lanes, at most 100 m after their end (stk. 1);
  - on roads with more than one lane in the direction of travel, on both sides (stk. 3);
  - on motorways and motortrafikveje, at most 50 m after each on-ramp's merge point ("tilkørselsrampens kilespids"), and at least every 4,000 m where it is not obvious that the limit still applies (stk. 4 nr. 1–2).
- **Road-kind signs end a local limit in practice.** "Såfremt en lokal hastighedsbegrænsning skal gælde forbi tavlerne E 42, E 44, E 55 og E 56, skal C 55 gentages ved eller højst 60 m efter tavlen" (if a local limit is to apply past E 42, E 44, E 55 or E 56, C 55 must be repeated at or within 60 m after that sign), [AVA] § 97 stk. 2. Reading: these four signs bring in the general limit of the new road kind, which counts as "another speed limit" in [VA] § 19 (medium). **E 43 and E 45 are not in that list**, so whether a motortrafikvej sign ends a C 55 is not stated (open point; treating it like E 42 is the reasonable choice, low).
- **Ramps and slip roads:**
  - A C 55 on the main carriageway does not cover ramps: "C 55 og C 56 opsættes kun på den aktuelle vejstrækning, som ikke omfatter til- og frakørselsrampe" (C 55 and C 56 are placed only on the current road stretch, which does not include on- and off-ramps), [AVA] § 97 stk. 4 nr. 4 (high).
  - Traffic entering from a ramp sees the main carriageway's C 55 within 50 m after the merge point (stk. 4 nr. 1). A camera therefore normally gets the main-road limit shortly after merging (high, placement rule).
  - A C 55 standing on a ramp applies "for en strækning på den aktuelle vej", and the ramp is a different stretch from the main carriageway. Reading: a ramp limit ends where the ramp joins the main carriageway (medium; no literal text or ruling found).
  - Exit-ramp curves often carry **E 41** "Hastighedsangivelse for frakørsel", which is **advisory** (see Special cases) ([VA] § 27; [AVA] § 126, high).
  - No legal text says which general limit applies on an unsigned motorway ramp. E 42 stands at the start of the on-ramp and the motorway rules cover the ramps (§§ 44–48), but § 42 stk. 2 says only "på motorveje" (not found; open point).
- **Turning onto another road.** The C 55 limit is for "en strækning på den aktuelle vej" (a stretch of the current road). After turning onto another road it no longer applies, and that road's own signs or general limit apply (medium, reading of [VA] § 19).

## Zones

- **E 68,4 "Zonetavle" with local speed limit** (a zone sign showing the red C 55 roundel; Lyngby-Taarbæk Kommune calls it "den røde zoneforbudstavle (E 68,4)" [LTK]). It is **mandatory**. The zone rules hold "indtil de ophæves ved tavle E 69, eller indtil de erstattes af angivelse vedrørende samme forhold på anden zonetavle" (until cancelled by E 69 or replaced by another zone sign about the same matter). "Inden for området kan zonetavlens bestemmelser fraviges ved lokal afmærkning" (inside the zone, local signs may deviate): [VA] § 27 (high).
  - Signed at every road entering the zone ([AVA] § 140).
  - Allowed values: in built-up areas **30 or 40 only**; outside built-up areas **30, 40 or 50**, for a delimited area of side roads; a main road cannot be part of it ([AVA] § 145; [LH] § 8) (high).
  - A zone covers at least two road stretches ([AVA] § 146), and a direct crossing of the zone may not exceed 1,800 m ([AVA] § 148) (high).
  - No repeater signs inside the zone: zone signs stand only at the entrances [LTK] (medium).
- **E 53 "Område med fartdæmpning" (area with speed calming).** Blue square with a number between 20 and 45 ([AVA] § 137 stk. 3). It is **advisory**: "Tavlen angiver et område, hvor kørebanen er indrettet således, at den ikke er egnet til kørsel med højere hastighed end den angivne" (the sign marks an area where the roadway is not suited to a higher speed than the one shown), [VA] § 27. Lyngby-Taarbæk: "Tavlerne viser anbefalet hastighed" (the signs show a recommended speed) [LTK]. Ended by E 54 (high for the text; medium that it is not enforceable, because no fine rule was found).
- **E 49 Gågade** (pedestrian street, "normalt under 15 km/h"), **E 51 Opholds- og legeområde** (play street, "normalt under 15 km/h") and **E 47 Cykelgade** (cycle street, "normalt under 30 km/h"). These set no numeric limit: the driver must go at very low speed, or at cycling speed. They are ended by E 50, E 52 and E 48: [VA] § 27 (high).
- **Municipal 40 km/h zones without police approval** (pilot scheme, [LH] § 7). Under [LH] § 12 stk. 2 the scheme expired on 30 June 2025. A bill to widen municipal powers was withdrawn in April 2025 [FDM2025] (medium). Under the general rules, 40 km/h zones and stretches remain possible by police decision ([LH] § 6).

## Variable message signs

- **Legal status:** "Variable færdselstavler har samme gyldighed som faste færdselstavler" (variable traffic signs have the same validity as fixed ones), [VA] § 5 stk. 1 (high). C 55 and C 56 may be variable (§ 5 stk. 2).
  - The red ring stays red; the background may be **white or black**. A lit C 55 can therefore be a red ring with white digits on black (§ 5 stk. 1, high).
  - Variable signs may be prism signs or made of light sources (§ 5 stk. 1).
- **Gantries and lanes:** signs may be restricted to individual lanes; on overhead signs this is shown "med nedadrettede pile" (with downward arrows): [VA] § 8 (high). Lane signals Y 17 (red X, yellow diagonal arrow, green arrow) can be combined with variable C 55 per lane ([AVA] § 252 stk. 2; [VA] Y 17) (high). Lane-specific limits therefore exist.
- **Blank or dark panel:** "Hvis der ikke er aktuel information at vise på en variabel tavle, skal tavlens visning være sort eller grå" (with no current information to show, a variable sign shows black or grey), [AVA] § 9 stk. 3 (high).
  - A dark panel is **not** among the ways a C 55 ends ([VA] § 19: end sign, distance panel, another limit). Blank is not an end sign (medium).
  - A variable C 56 must be controlled together with its variable C 55 ([AVA] § 100), so a system that lifts a variable limit can show C 56.
  - What applies after a gantry that goes dark (the earlier limit, or the fixed or general limit) is **not stated** (open point).
- **Rules for road authorities:**
  - variable signs are used only for incidents or conditions that need changed regulation (§ 9 stk. 1);
  - they must not contradict fixed signs (§ 9 stk. 2);
  - they are monitored around the clock, with a 24-month log (§ 11);
  - variable A 20 "Kø" (queue) warnings may be used only together with variable C 55 (§ 37) ([AVA], high).
- **Road works:**
  - on motorways and motortrafikveje, a roadworks C 55 is repeated at most every 2,000 m ([VAR] § 25);
  - a variable C 55 switches to 50 km/h for at most five minutes when a works vehicle presses the trigger button (§ 31);
  - at acute incidents the C 55 normally shows 80 on motorways and 70 on motortrafikveje (§ 79);
  - a C 56 carried on a works vehicle is replaced by C 55 on stretches with a permanent local limit (§ 71 stk. 3) (high).

## Supplementary panels

General rule: "En færdselstavles betydning kan præciseres, indskrænkes eller udvides ved tekst, tal eller symbol på en rektangulær undertavle under hovedtavlen" (a sign's meaning may be clarified, restricted or extended by text, numbers or a symbol on a rectangular panel below it), [VA] § 9 (high). Panels under prohibition signs have a white background (§ 9 stk. 2).

| Panel | Meaning | Effect for a car |
|---|---|---|
| **U 1** | Distance to the point where the sign applies | The limit starts later: advance notice. |
| **U 2** | The sign applies to a stretch, "x–y" from the sign | The limit ends after y without an end sign. |
| **U 3** | Hours or minutes, weekdays, times. Black or white times = weekdays except Saturday; times in brackets = Saturday; red times = Sundays and holidays. | Time-dependent limit. |
| **U 4** | Only the vehicle types shown | Applies only to those vehicles. |
| **U 5** | Not for the vehicle type shown | Excluded vehicles. |
| **U 6** | The sign applies to an **adjoining road** | Not for the current road: the camera must ignore it. |
| **Weight panel under C 55** | Applies only to vehicles or combinations whose permitted total weight exceeds the value | Ignore for a car of 3,500 kg or less. |
| **Vehicle-type panel under C 55** | Valid only when it shows, on one panel: lorry with trailer, bus with trailer, car up to 3,500 kg with trailer, and motorcycle with trailer | Applies only to vehicles with trailers. |
| **"Støj"** (noise) | Reason for the limit | Applies to everyone. |
| **E 55 symbol / UC 57 under C 56** | See End signs | → 50 / → 80. |
| Season in months | [LH] § 8 stk. 2 allows a seasonal rural limit | The panel format was not found (open point). |

Panel sources: [VA] §§ 9 and 19 (high). Wet-road panels: none found for speed limits.

## Special cases for a camera system

- **Units:** km/h only. No mph signs were found (high). Signs show only the number.
- **Advisory signs that look like limits.** Never take these as a limit:
  - **E 39** "Anbefalet hastighed" (recommended speed): blue square, white number, used outside built-up areas, ended by E 40 or a distance panel ([VA] § 27; [AVA] § 125).
  - **E 41** exit-curve speed.
  - **UA 41** curve speed panel under curve warnings A 41/A 42: "angiver den hastighed, hvormed svinget ... under normale forhold kan gennemkøres" (the speed at which the curve can be driven in normal conditions).
  - **E 53** speed-calmed area (20–45).
  - **E 91 Fartviser**, a speed display that shows the driver's own speed in lit digits, used on roads up to 80 km/h ([VA] § 27; [AVA] § 159).

  All high from the text. Telling them apart: a **red ring** means a mandatory limit (C 55, also inside the E 68,4 zone sign). A **blue square** means advisory.
- **Temporary and road-works signs:** a C 55 on a **yellow backing plate with a black border** is a temporary sign with full legal force ([VA] § 6) (high).
- **Variable C 55** may be white digits on a black background inside a red ring ([VA] § 5).
- **The town limit does not apply on motorvej or motortrafikvej** inside an E 55 area ([FL] § 42 stk. 1). The road-kind signs take precedence over town status (high).
- **Advance notices:** E 42, E 44 and E 45, and C 55 with U 1, can stand 200–400 m before the point where they apply ([AVA] §§ 127–130). A distance panel means "not yet".
- **U 6 panels** (adjoining road) and signs on side roads at junctions must not be applied to the main road.
- **Motortrafikvej with level junctions:** E 43 is repeated after each junction ([AVA] § 128), which is a useful confirmation signal.

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:** `"DK": {"town": 50, "rural": 80, "expressway": 80, "motorway": 130}` (km/h).
  - `expressway` = motortrafikvej (E 43, blue sign). `motorway` = motorvej (E 42, green sign).
  - No extra key is needed: there are no seasonal national limits and no wet or night limits.
  - Signed local values to expect: 30/40/60/70 in town, 90 rural, 90/100 on a motortrafikvej, 110 often on motorways. Seasonal local limits come only from signs.
  - The road-kind signs should map as in ROAD_SIGNS: E 42 → motorway, E 44 → rural (or town if E 55 follows), E 43 → expressway, E 45 → rural, E 55 → town, E 56 → rural.
  - When the road kind is motorway or expressway, the town key must not apply, even inside an E 55 area ([FL] § 42 stk. 1).
- **Intersection rule: No.** A C 55 limit does **not** end at the next junction. [VA] § 19 says the C 55 limit applies "uanset bestemmelsen i § 18, stk. 2" (the next-junction rule for other prohibitions), and the Supreme Court confirmed this on 17 January 2025 (case 56/2024). A limit applies only to "the current road", so after **turning onto another road** it lapses (medium).
  - For the code: DK must not use the Swiss "holds to the next junction" fallback.
  - Follow a map change only when the car is on a different road (another way, name or ref), not when the map's limit changes along the same road.
- **Blank variable sign:** "no information" ([AVA] § 9 stk. 3). It is not an end sign. Keep the limit held until a C 56, another C 55, a road-kind sign or a road change (medium; open point).
- **Other rules:**
  - Limits end with C 56 or C 59 (→ the general limit of the road kind), a U 2 stretch end, another C 55, or the E 42/E 44/E 55/E 56 signs, unless a C 55 is repeated within 60 m.
  - Zones (E 68,4): the limit holds until E 69 or another zone sign. Local signs inside the zone override it.
  - A ramp C 55 lapses at the merge. The main road's C 55 must appear within 50 m after the merge point, so the existing RAMP/MERGE logic fits DK.
  - Leaving a motorway at an exit usually shows no E 44: switch the road kind from the road-type network.
  - Ignore blue advisory squares (E 39, E 41, E 53), UA 41 panels, E 91 speed displays, weight-panel C 55 (> 3,500 kg) and trailer-only C 55.
  - Respect U 3 time panels when possible, or treat time-panel limits as uncertain.

## Open points
1. **Unsigned motorway ramps:** which general limit applies, 130 by § 42 stk. 2 or none, is not stated in law: not found. In practice ramps are signed (C 55, E 41).
2. **E 43/E 45 and a running C 55:** [AVA] § 97 stk. 2 lists only E 42, E 44, E 55 and E 56 as signs past which a C 55 must be repeated. Whether a motortrafikvej sign also ends a local limit is not stated (low).
3. **Dark variable gantry after a variable limit:** the law says only that a dark panel means "no current information". What limit then applies is not found. Vejdirektoratet's operating manuals (approved by the police, [AVA] § 3) may say; they were not found online.
4. **Seasonal (month-limited) rural limits ([LH] § 8 stk. 2):** the panel format is not found.
5. **Exact look of E 68,4/E 69,4, C 59, E 41, E 53 and UC 57:** not verified. Commons rate-limited the downloads; the regulations show them only as images. Vejdirektoratet's sign catalogue should be checked.
6. **The municipal 40 km/h scheme ([LH] § 7)** expired on 30 June 2025. No replacement regulation was found on retsinformation (BEK 3/2022 shows only the 2022 amendment); check for a 2026 amendment.
7. **The "turning onto another road ends a C 55" reading** rests on the words "på den aktuelle vej". No ruling on it was found (medium).
