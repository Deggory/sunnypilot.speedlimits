# Croatia (HR): speed rules for the comma
Checked: 2026-10-29. Legal basis: Zakon o sigurnosti prometa na cestama (ZSPC, Road Traffic Safety Act), NN 67/08, 48/10, 74/11, 80/13, 158/13, 92/14, 64/15, 108/17, 70/19, 42/20, 85/22, 114/22, 133/23, 145/24 (consolidated text in force since 21 Dec 2024); Pravilnik o prometnim znakovima, signalizaciji i opremi na cestama (signs regulation), NN 92/2019.

Notes on the sources:
- Consolidated ZSPC: zakon.hr, which is not the official gazette (it says so itself). Original act and amending acts: the official Narodne novine (NN) site. All article numbers below come from the consolidated text unless marked "2008 text".
- Signs regulation: the official NN text of NN 92/2019. No later amendment was found (search on 2026-10-29; certainty medium that none exists).
- The web-search budget ran out before every point could be checked twice. Points that rest on a single source are marked.

## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| In a built-up area (*naselje*, between C76 and C77) | 50 km/h | A sign may set a lower limit "for the whole settlement or part of it". A sign may also allow more than 50, but **at most 80 km/h**, where the road allows it (art. 53(2)). No night, wet or visibility variant. | ZSPC art. 53(1)-(2) [1] |
| Outside built-up areas, ordinary roads (*ostale ceste*) | 90 km/h | Art. 54(1) reads "brzinom većom od brzine dopuštene postavljenim prometnim znakom, **a najviše**": a sign may lower the limit but never raise it above 90. | ZSPC art. 54(1) t. 3 [1] |
| Expressway (*brza cesta*) and road reserved for motor vehicles (*cesta namijenjena isključivo za promet motornih vozila*) | 110 km/h | Both kinds share sign C66 and the same limit. 110 is also the ceiling for any sign. | ZSPC art. 54(1) t. 2 [1] |
| Motorway (*autocesta*) | 130 km/h | 130 is also the ceiling for any sign. | ZSPC art. 54(1) t. 1 [1] |
| Traffic-calmed zone (*zona smirenog prometa*, C28 to C29) | Walking pace | No km/h figure in the law. | ZSPC art. 2(1) t. 25 [1]; Pravilnik, C28 [5] |
| Pedestrian zone (*pješačka zona*) | No motor vehicles | Only vehicles with a special permit may enter. | ZSPC art. 2(1) t. 23 [1] |

Other conditions:
- **Wet road, night, fog or visibility:** no numeric rule was found in ZSPC arts. 51-55. Art. 51(1) only requires speed to be adapted to visibility, weather, road condition and traffic [1]. The signs regulation defines B30 as the limit "u idealnim uvjetima" (in ideal conditions) [5]. Certainty high that no fixed weather or night limit exists in these articles.
- **Minimum for a sign:** art. 51(4) says that under normal traffic conditions speed may not be limited below 30 km/h [1]. English Wikipedia gives 40, probably the older text [8]. Certainty medium: one consolidated source.
- **Young drivers (*mladi vozač*):**
  - Definition: a driver under 24 with a Croatian licence (art. 2(1) t. 69) [1].
  - The 2008 text (art. 221(1)) limited young drivers to 80 km/h on roads, 100 km/h on expressways and motor-vehicle roads, and 120 km/h on motorways [2].
  - The consolidated text shows art. 221 as **"Brisan" (repealed)** [1], and EU "Your Europe" lists no separate speed limit for novice drivers [6].
  - The amending act that repealed it was not identified. NN 64/2015 removed only paragraphs 3-4 [3]. NN 92/2014 [4], NN 70/2019 [7] and NN 85/2022 [9] do not touch the speed paragraph.
  - Certainty medium that no young-driver speed limit applies today.
- **Vehicle classes (art. 55):**
  - Off motorways (expressways and other roads): 80 for buses, vehicles over 3.5 t and any motor vehicle towing a trailer without brakes; 80 for buses carrying organised groups of children; 70 for articulated buses without standing places; 50 when people ride in the cargo area and for buses with standing places; 40 for vehicles towing a broken-down vehicle and for tractors; 20 for tourist trains.
  - On motorways: 100 for buses (not those carrying children), 90 for vehicles over 3.5 t and vehicles towing a trailer without brakes.
  - A passenger car with a **braked** trailer has no special limit in art. 55 [1]. Certainty high on the text.

Art. 29(1) t. 1 makes green the base colour of motorway guidance signs (certainty medium: the C64 row itself gives no colour). |

Art. 29(1) t. 2: blue is the base colour of expressway guidance signs. C95 on an expressway "sadrži znak ... (C66) i plave je boje". Certainty medium on the colour of C66 itself. |

Definitions (ZSPC art. 2(1)) [1]:
- **Motorway, t. 3:** a public road built for and reserved to motor vehicles, with physically separated carriageways and no level crossings, marked by the prescribed sign.
- **Expressway, t. 4,** as amended by NN 70/19 art. 2 [7]:
  - a public road for motor vehicles only, "s jednom ili dvije razdvojene kolničke trake" (**one or two carriageways**);
  - every junction on two or more levels, as a rule with no emergency lane;
  - marked by the prescribed sign;
  - by exception, during phased construction, it may have at-grade junctions.
- **Road reserved for motor vehicles, t. 5:** a public road with at least two lanes, open only to motor vehicles that can exceed 60 km/h, marked by the prescribed sign.
- **Settlement, t. 24:** its boundaries are marked by traffic signs. So the 50 km/h of art. 53 starts at C76 (certainty high).

How a camera can tell motorway from expressway:
- **C64** (green, motorway symbol) against **C66** (blue, car symbol).
- Guidance signs: green on motorways, blue on expressways, yellow on other roads, white for parts of towns (art. 29(1)) [5]. The same colour code is repeated on C71 (mountain pass) and C73 (tunnel): "na autocesti zelene je boje, na brzim cestama je plave, a na svim ostalim cestama žute je boje" [5].
- **C81** is placed at the junction where a road enters a motorway or expressway: green for a motorway, blue for an expressway [5].
- C66 covers both the expressway and the motor-vehicle road, and both have 110. The camera does not need to tell those two apart.

Where the signs stand:
- No placement rule for C64 or C66 was found in the regulation text (not found). Whether they stand at the start of the entry ramp, after the toll plaza or on the carriageway is not stated in the text read.
- C81 stands at the junction, before the ramp [5].

## How long a speed sign holds

- **Start (art. 20(1)-(3)):** a prohibition or restriction sign (*znak izričitih naredbi*, which includes B30) stands where the obligation begins. If it stands earlier, for safety, it must carry a distance panel (E01) [5].
- **Junctions (art. 21(1)):** "Znakovi izričitih naredbi moraju se ponovno postaviti nakon svakoga raskrižja ako izričita naredba vrijedi i poslije takvog raskrižja." (The signs must be placed again after every junction if the order also holds after it.) [5]
  - Interpretation: a B30 that is not repeated after a junction no longer holds beyond it. The rule ends at the end sign (C11/C14) or at the next junction, whichever comes first.
  - **Certainty medium.** The text puts the duty to repeat the sign on the road authority and does not say in so many words "the limit ends at the junction". No B30 entry and no court ruling saying so was found.
- **Exception, signs at the town entry (art. 21(2)):** a prohibition, restriction or obligation sign "postavljenim na ulazu u naselje na istom stupu na kojem je postavljen i znak za obilježavanje naseljenog mjesta (C76) vrijedi na području cijelog naselja" (on the same post as C76, it holds over the whole settlement). It holds "ako na pojedinim cestama ili dijelovima ceste u naselju nije drugim prometnim znakom izražena druga naredba" (unless another sign gives another order on a given road), and it also covers tunnels and bridges [5].
  - So a **B30 under or on the C76 post holds through every junction until C77**.
  - Matches ZSPC art. 53(1): a limit "za cijelo naselje ili njegov dio" [1]. Certainty high.
- **What counts as a junction (*raskrižje*, art. 2(1) t. 26):** "površina na kojoj se križaju ili spajaju dvije ceste ili više cesta", squares included [1].
  - "Cesta" (t. 1) includes unclassified roads (*nerazvrstane ceste*). T. 9 lists access roads, car parks and petrol-station areas among them.
  - Joining traffic from a garage, yard or car park is governed by art. 45(1) (yield to all), not by junction rules.
  - No provision was found that excludes driveways or field roads from "raskrižje". Whether a driveway ends a speed sign is **not settled** (certainty low).
- **Ramps and interchanges:**
  - The law itself calls interchanges junctions "u dvije ili više razina" (expressway definition, t. 4) [7]. A ramp joins ("spajaju") the main carriageway, so a merge is a junction under t. 26.
  - Interpretation: a limit on a ramp ends at the merge, and a limit on the main carriageway must be repeated after each interchange to bind traffic entering from the ramp (art. 21(1)).
  - Certainty medium. No text or ruling deals with ramps specifically (not found).
- **Case law:** not found. A search of zakon.hr's court-practice index found speeding cases under arts. 53-54, but none on how long a sign holds.

## End signs

| Sign | Meaning (Pravilnik NN 92/2019 [5]) | What it restores |
|---|---|---|
| **C11** "prestanak ograničenja brzine" | "Označava mjesto od kojeg prestaje ograničenje brzine." | The general limit of the road kind: 50 in a settlement, 90 outside, 110 on an expressway, 130 on a motorway (ZSPC arts. 53-54). |
| **C14** "prestanak svih zabrana" | "Označava mjesto na cesti od kojeg prestaju vrijediti sve zabrane na toj cesti." | The same: the general limit. |
| **C23** zone end | "...prestaje ograničenje brzine u određenom području, odnosno zoni." | The general limit, normally 50 (zones are in settlements). |
| **C29** calmed-zone end | "...izlazi iz područja smirenog prometa" | 50 (still in the settlement). |
| **C77** end of settlement | Settlement ends. | 90, or the limit of the road kind. It also ends a town-wide B30 placed at C76 (art. 21(2)). |
| **C65** end of motorway | Motorway ends. | 90 outside a settlement, unless a sign says otherwise. |
| **C67** end of expressway / motor-vehicle road | That road ends. | 90 outside a settlement. |

Note: C13 ends the ban on sound signals and C21 ends the obligation to use dipped headlights. Neither is a speed end sign [5].

## Zones

- **Speed-limit zone:**
  - Starts at **C22**: "Označava područje, odnosno zonu kojom se vozila ne smiju kretati brzinom (u km/h) većom od one što je označena na znaku" [5].
  - Ends at **C23**.
  - The zone limit is not ended by junctions inside the zone: the sign marks an area (certainty high).
  - Typical value 30, but the sign carries any number.
- **Traffic-calmed zone (*zona smirenog prometa*):**
  - Starts at **C28**: "...ne smije voziti brže od brzine hoda pješaka jer je dječja igra svugdje dopuštena." Ends at **C29** [5].
  - The law gives no km/h value, only walking pace (ZSPC art. 2(1) t. 25) [1].
- **Town-wide limit:** a B30 on the C76 post holds for the whole settlement (art. 21(2)) [5]. It works like a zone that ends at C77.
- **Pedestrian zone:** no motor vehicles (ZSPC art. 2(1) t. 23) [1].

## Variable message signs

ZSPC [1]:
- Art. 12(3) lists "promjenjivi prometni znakovi" (variable signs) among the traffic signs, and art. 12(4) obliges road users to obey the restrictions they show.
- Art. 17(2): signs on motorways and in tunnels longer than 500 m must be able to change meaning with traffic conditions.

Signs regulation NN 92/2019 [5]:
- **Types (art. 39):** any sign may be built fully or partly as a variable sign, either continuous or non-continuous (art. 39(1)-(2)). Lit variable signs combined with fixed ones always stand **after** the fixed sign in the direction of travel (art. 39(3)). Variable prohibition signs likewise stand after the fixed ones (art. 22(2)).
- **Legal status (art. 40(5)):** "Svjetlosno promjenjivi prometni znakovi s kontinuiranim prikazom sadržaja imaju isto značenje kao prometni znakovi sa stalnim sadržajem." A lit sign that shows its content continuously is a full traffic sign.
- **Colours (art. 40(2)):** where the technology cannot show the normal colours, the background may be non-reflective **black** and the symbols white or yellow instead of black and white.
- **Dark or faulty panels (art. 41):**
  - Art. 41(1): if part of a non-continuous variable sign fails or a light burns out, the sign must not change its meaning: it "mora biti bez signalnog pojma (nulto stanje)" (no signal meaning, null state).
  - Art. 41(2): in normal driving conditions the signal terms of non-continuous variable signs "moraju biti ugašeni" (must be off). The signal is switched on when conditions change, and stays on until normal conditions return.
  - **A dark panel therefore has no meaning. It is not an end sign:** the limit of the fixed sign before it, or the general limit, still holds. Certainty high on the text, medium on applying it to every gantry type.
- **Variable speed limit (art. 41(3)):** a variable speed-limit sign stands "u pravilu" (as a rule) after the fixed speed-limit sign in the direction of travel.
- **Over-lane panels:** D07 places each panel over its own lane as a separate sign, and art. 47(2) does the same for lane signals. A rule specific to **lane-specific variable speed limits** was not found.
- **Wind (bura) and works in practice:** HAK reports show temporary limits shown by signs, for example 40 km/h on an A1 section in a storm, and full closures of A1 Sveti Rok-Posedarje [10][11]. These are news reports (certainty low on the exact values and the signs used).

## Supplementary panels

Signs regulation NN 92/2019, art. 35 and art. 38 / Table 7 [5]. Panels "pobliže određuju značenje prometnog znaka" (they make the meaning of the sign more precise), and a panel is part of the sign it stands under (art. 11(3)).

| Panel | Meaning | Effect on a B30 |
|---|---|---|
| **E01** | Distance from the sign to the start of the section. Required when a sign stands before the place where the order holds (art. 20(3)). | The limit starts that far ahead. |
| **E02** | Length of the section where the danger or order applies. | The limit ends after that length. |
| **E03** | Distance and direction to an object. | Usually not for speed. |
| **E04** | Time period when the order applies, when it does not apply all the time. | The limit holds only at those times. Art. 22(1) also allows signs whose symbol is visible only during that time. |
| **E05** | Explanatory text or symbol. | Read the text. |
| **E06 to E06-16, E07** | Vehicle type, or maximum permitted mass (e.g. 5 t). | B30 with them "odnosi se samo na tu vrstu vozila ili na vozila čija najveća dopuštena masa prelazi označenu": **ignore it for a passenger car** unless the panel shows a car. |
| **E13** | Start of a section with possible unexpected weather (rain, snow). | A warning, not a wet-road condition on the limit. |
| **E18** | Sign placed because of ruts (*kolotrag*). Used with B30. | The limit holds normally; the panel gives the reason. |
| **E19** | Speed humps. Used with B30. | The limit holds normally. |

- **No "when wet" panel** was found in Table 7 (certainty medium: the summary of the table read showed none).
- **No "exit only" panel** was found (not found). Lane assignment is shown by lane arrows on direction signs (D07/D08) and by road markings H22-H43.

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **Town-entry limit:** a number on or under the C76 town-name post holds for the whole town until C77, through every junction (art. 21(2)). It must not be dropped at the next junction.
2. **Signs cannot raise the cap:** at most 80 in a settlement (art. 53(2)), 90 on ordinary rural roads, 110 on an expressway or motor-vehicle road, 130 on a motorway (art. 54(1) "a najviše"). A higher reading for the known road kind is either a misreading or a sign for another road (for example a slip road or a parallel road). It can be used as a plausibility check.
3. **Vehicle panels (E06/E07):** B30 limits for buses, trucks or mass (e.g. 80 with a truck pictogram, or 3.5 t / 5 t) are common. They must be ignored for a passenger car.
4. **Distance panel (E01):** the limit starts later, not at the sign.
5. **Dark VMS:** a dark panel has no meaning. Keep the last fixed limit (art. 41).
6. **One sign, two road kinds:** C66 covers both the expressway and the motor-vehicle road, and both have 110. Expressways may also be single-carriageway (NN 70/19), so a single-carriageway road under C66 still has 110.
7. **End signs:** C11, C14, C23, C29, C65, C67 and C77 each bring back a general limit, never "no limit".
8. **Repetition after junctions:** on motorways the limit should be repeated after each interchange (art. 21(1)). A missing repeat means the general 130 or 110 applies again (certainty medium).

## For the comma

- **IMPLICIT_LIMITS row:** `"HR": {"town": 50, "rural": 90, "expressway": 110, "motorway": 130}`.
  - `expressway` = C66 (*brza cesta* and *cesta namijenjena isključivo za promet motornih vozila*), started by C66 and ended by C67.
  - `motorway` = C64, ended by C65.
  - `town` = C76, ended by C77.
  - No new key is needed for the road kinds.
  - Optional: a young-driver key (`novice`) is **not** needed today, because the art. 221 limits appear repealed (certainty medium).
- **The intersection rule:** **Yes, partly** (certainty medium).
  - Art. 21(1) of the signs regulation NN 92/2019 requires a prohibition or restriction sign to be repeated after every junction if it still applies. A B30 that is not repeated is taken to end at the next junction (*raskrižje*, ZSPC art. 2(1) t. 26). Interchange merges count too.
  - Exceptions:
    - a B30 on the C76 town-entry post holds for the whole settlement until C77 (art. 21(2));
    - zone limits (C22) hold until C23.
  - The code's current rule (the map's limit change at a junction takes over, MAP_SECONDS) fits Croatia. Town-wide limits must be exempted.
- **Blank VMS:** no meaning ("nulto stanje", art. 41(1)-(2)). It is not an end sign: keep the limit held.
- **Other rules the code should know:**
  1. Clamp or flag readings above the cap of the known road kind: town 80, rural 90, expressway 110, motorway 130.
  2. A number confirmed together with the town-entry sign (C76) should be held as a town-wide limit until town_exit (C77), not dropped at the next junction.
  3. C14 (end of all prohibitions) ends a speed limit like C11.
  4. C28 (calmed zone) has no number. If the code needs one, walking pace (around 5-10 km/h) is an interpretation, not law.
  5. B30 with a vehicle panel (E06/E07) applies only to that vehicle class.
  6. No weather or night limits exist in law, so none need to be modelled.

## Open points

1. **Intersection rule, wording:** art. 21(1) is a duty to repeat the sign, not an explicit "ends at the junction" clause. No court ruling or official commentary confirming the reading was found. Certainty medium.
2. **Driveways and field roads:** whether they count as a *raskrižje* that ends a B30 is not settled. Unclassified roads are "ceste", so their junctions probably count; property exits fall under art. 45.
3. **Young drivers:** art. 221 is shown as repealed in the consolidated text. The repealing act (probably NN 108/17, 133/23 or 145/24) was not identified.
4. **Where C64 and C66 stand** (ramp start, after the toll plaza, on the carriageway): not found in the regulation.
5. **Exact colours of C64, C66 and C76:** not stated in the regulation text read. Green and blue for C64 and C66 are inferred from art. 29(1) and the C71/C73/C81 rows; C76's colour was not found.
6. **Amendments to the signs regulation after NN 92/2019:** none found (certainty medium).
7. **Lane-specific variable limits** on gantries, and the operators' wind (bura) and tunnel limit schemes (HAC, ARZ, Bina-Istra): no legal text or official operator document found; only news reports.
8. **Art. 51(4) minimum (30 or 40 km/h):** the consolidated text read gives 30, while secondary sources give 40. One more read of the official consolidated text would settle it.
