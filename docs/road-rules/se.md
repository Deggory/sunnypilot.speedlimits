## General limits (passenger cars, no sign)

| Road kind | Limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inside a built-up area (*tättbebyggt område*) | **50 km/h** | The municipality may set 30 or 40 for the whole area or part of it (TrF 3 kap. 17 § 2nd para), and deviate further by local traffic regulation (TrF 10 kap. 1 § 2nd para items 14–15). No wet, night or season rule. **high** | [TrF] 3 kap. 17 § 1st para |
| Outside built-up areas | **70 km/h** (*bashastighet*, base speed) | Trafikverket may set 80, 90, 100 or 110 on a road (TrF 3 kap. 17 § 4th para); lower limits by local traffic regulation (länsstyrelsen or the municipality, TrF 10 kap. 1 and 3 §§). **high** | [TrF] 3 kap. 17 § 3rd–4th para |
| *Motortrafikled* (expressway, road for motor vehicles) | **No limit of its own**: 70 outside built-up areas, 50 inside, unless signed. Signed values range from 80 to **110 max**: 120 is allowed only on a motorway | TrF 9 kap. 2 § extends to the motortrafikled only items 1–5 of 9 kap. 1 § (access, no turning or reversing, no stopping), **not** item 6 (110 km/h). So the 3 kap. 17 § limits apply. **high** for the text, **medium** that an unsigned motortrafikled is 70 (in practice it is always signed) | [TrF] 9 kap. 1–2 §§, 3 kap. 17 § |
| Motorway (*motorväg*) | **110 km/h** | Trafikverket may set **120** (TrF 9 kap. 1 § 3rd para); lower limits by local traffic regulation (10 kap. 1 § item 15). **high** | [TrF] 9 kap. 1 § 1st para item 6 and 3rd para |
| Pedestrian street (*gågata*, E7) and walking-speed area (*gångfartsområde*, E9) | **walking pace** (*gångfart*), no number | "Fordon får inte föras med högre hastighet än gångfart." **high** (no km/h value is defined: **not found**) | [TrF] 8 kap. 1 § |
| Bicycle street (*cykelgata*, E33) | **30 km/h** | "Fordon får inte föras med högre hastighet än 30 kilometer i timmen", and drivers must adapt to cyclists. **high** | [TrF] 8 kap. 1 a §; sign: [TS-E] `cykelgata/` |
| Every road | adapt speed | "Ett fordons hastighet skall anpassas till vad trafiksäkerheten kräver" (3 kap. 14 §); 3 kap. 15 § lists where to keep a low speed. These are not number limits. **high** | [TrF] 3 kap. 14–15 §§ |

Other conditions:

- **Season/winter:** TrF has **no** seasonal limit. Its 10 kap. 14 § allows temporary lower limits for short periods, and the police or road authority may lower the limit temporarily to prevent wildlife collisions. **high** that the law has no seasonal rule, [TrF] 10 kap. 14 §. No Swedish national winter-limit scheme (like Finland's) was found. Trafikverket has made temporary cuts "till vintern", such as E14 to 80 km/h from December 2021 until median barriers are built. Those cuts are ordinary signed C31 limits, not a seasonal rule ([Vi Bilägare, 2021-05-18](https://www.vibilagare.se/nyheter/trafikverket-vill-sanka-hastigheten-pa-e14-att-hoja-sakerheten)). **medium**.
- **Wet road, night, visibility:** no number limit in TrF, only the general duty of 3 kap. 14–15 §§. **high**.
- **Novice drivers:** no speed rule found in TrF 3 kap. 17 § or 4 kap. 20 §. **medium**: absence not confirmed by an agency text.
- **Vehicle classes** (not passenger cars, for context): a car with a trailer is limited to 80. A heavy truck is limited to 80, or 90 on a motorway or motortrafikled. A heavy bus is limited to 90, or 100 if every seat has a belt. TrF 4 kap. 20 §, as amended by SFS 2023:378. A new version (SFS 2026:1052) takes effect **2026-12-01**. It keeps these values, adds 40 km/h for a car towing a vehicle on a lifting device, and says a lower posted limit applies. **high**, [TrF] 4 kap. 20 §.
- Since 2008, limits can be set in all ten 10 km/h steps from 30 to 120 ("Sedan 2008 är det möjligt att införa hastighetsgränser i alla tio steg mellan 30 och 120 km/tim"). **high**, [TRV-HG]. Trafikverket sets 80–120 on state roads. The länsstyrelse sets local regulations outside built-up areas, and the municipality inside them. [TRV-HG], [TrF] 10 kap. 3 §.
- **Signing duty:** speed limits under 3 kap. 17 §, 8 kap. 1 a § and 9 kap. 1 § "ska utmärkas" with road signs (TrF 10 kap. 13 § item 1). **high**. In practice every limit change has a C31. Sweden has **no general "end of speed limit" sign** (C32 ends only a temporary limit). **high**, [VMF] 2 kap. 8 §.

Definitions ([DEF] 2 §, **high**):

- *Motorväg*: "En väg eller en vägsträcka som enligt en lokal trafikföreskrift ska vara motorväg och som är utmärkt med vägmärke för motorväg". *Motortrafikled* is defined the same way.
- *Tättbebyggt område*: "Ett visst område som enligt en lokal trafikföreskrift ska vara tättbebyggt område". Unlike motorväg, the definition does **not** require the sign.
- Transportstyrelsen has ruled that only an area can be a tättbebyggt område, never a single road or road section: the Brålanda decision of 2012-02-21, https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/trafikregler-och-vagmarken/trafikregler/beslut-i-overklagningsarenden/tattbebyggtomrade/bralanda/. **high**.

## Road kinds and their signs

Codes and names are from [VMF] 2 kap. 8 § (C signs) and 2 kap. 12 § (E signs, *anvisningsmärken*). VMF 2 kap. 2 § says a sign "har den betydelse som framgår av deras namn". The look described below is from the official PNG files on [TS-C31], [TS-C32] and [TS-E], viewed on 2026-10-29. **high** for codes; **high** for look (official images).

| Kind | Begins | Ends | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motorway | **E1 Motorväg** | **E2 Motorväg upphör** | Green upright rectangle with the white motorway symbol (road and bridge). E2 is the same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Expressway (*motortrafikled*) | **E3 Motortrafikled** | **E4 Motortrafikled upphör** | Green upright rectangle with a white car seen from the front. E4 is the same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Built-up area | **E5 Tättbebyggt område** | **E6 Tättbebyggt område upphör** | White horizontal rectangle with a black town skyline (houses, church spire). The place name may be inserted. E6 is the same with a red diagonal bar. |
| Pedestrian street / walking-speed area | E7 Gågata / E9 Gångfartsområde | E8 / E10 (may be omitted when the end is obvious) | Blue square, white pictograms |
| Bicycle street | E33 Cykelgata | E34 Cykelgata upphör | Blue square, white car above a bicycle |
| Speed limit | **C31 Hastighetsbegränsning** | another C31; **C32** only for a temporary limit | C31: **yellow** disc, **red** ring, black digits, values 30–120 (variants C31-3 … C31-12). C32: yellow disc, **grey** number, four black diagonal stripes (variants C32-2 … C32-11, i.e. 20–110). |

Telling motorway from motortrafikled with a camera:

- **E1 and E3 are both green rectangles.** E1 shows the motorway symbol (two carriageways under a bridge); E3 shows a car. The end signs have a red diagonal bar. **high**.
- **E2 is not used where a motorway turns into a motortrafikled, and E4 is not used where a motortrafikled turns into a motorway.** Only the new road's start sign stands there ([VMF] 2 kap. 12 §). So E3 can directly follow a motorway, and vice versa. **high**.
- Advance notice: E1 to E4 used with a T2 distance plate are an advance notice, not the start ([VMF] 2 kap. 12 §). TSFS 6 kap. 4 § and 6 § require an E2 + T2 advance notice where a motorway turns into a motortrafikled, and an E4 + T2 notice for the reverse. **high**.

Placement ([TSFS] 6 kap. 3–6 §§, **high**):

- E1 stands on both sides of the right carriageway where the motorway begins. Where the motorway begins on a one-lane on-ramp, the left sign may be left out.
- "Märket ska upprepas efter varje påfartsväg som inte är motorväg": **E1 is repeated after every on-ramp**. A camera on the main carriageway therefore sees E1 at interchanges, and E1 there does not mean a new motorway starts.
- E2 and E4 stand on both sides where the road ends, and on the right only when the road ends on an off-ramp.
- E3 and E4 follow the same pattern as E1 and E2.

Ramps belong to the road: TSFS size tables say "Avser påfarts- eller avfartsväg som ingår i motorväg eller motortrafikled". So on- and off-ramps can be part of the motorway or motortrafikled, and the motorway may begin on the on-ramp itself (TSFS 6 kap. 3 §). **high**.

**Important:** none of E1–E6 is listed among the signs that end a C31 (see next section). They change the general limit of the road, but they do not cancel a speed sign already in force. **high** for the text ([VMF] 2 kap. 8 § C31 entry); **medium** for what that means when a road sign and a held C31 disagree (see "For the comma").

## How long a speed sign holds

**C31 holds until the next C31, a C32, an E7 or an E9: it does not end at intersections.**

- [VMF] 2 kap. 8 §, C31: "Angivelsen gäller till den plats där en annan hastighetsbegränsning anges med märket, eller där märke C32, tillfällig hastighetsbegränsning upphör, E7, gågata, eller E9, gångfartsområde, satts upp." **high**. Same text on [TS-C31].
- This overrides the general rule for prohibition signs. Under [VMF] 2 kap. 7 § 1st para, a prohibition sign holds "från den plats där märket satts upp antingen till nästa korsning med en annan väg än sådan som avses i 3 kap. 21 § andra stycket trafikförordningen ... eller till den plats dessförinnan där ett motsvarande märke med tilläggstavla T11 ... eller ett slutmärke har satts upp", but only "om inte annat anges nedan eller i 8 §". The C31 entry in 8 § is such an exception. **high**.
- The roads listed in TrF 3 kap. 21 § 2nd para do not count as intersections, even for other prohibition signs. They are exits from parking areas, properties and petrol stations, private access roads and tracks, cycle paths, pedestrian streets, walking-speed areas, cycle streets and terrain ([TrF] 3 kap. 21 §). **high**.

Repetition after junctions:

- VMF 2 kap. 7 § 2nd para: if a prohibition continues past a junction, "är märket upprepat efter korsningen om inte annat sägs i 8 §". The C31 entry in 8 § then relaxes this: "Märket behöver inte vara upprepat efter en vägkorsning om samma högsta tillåtna hastighet gäller på den korsande vägen." **high**.
- [TSFS] 4 kap. 27 §: C31 need not be repeated after a junction if the next sign for a different limit stands less than 200 m after the junction. **high**.
- [TSFS] 4 kap. 29 §: no repetition is needed after a junction with a road of TrF 3 kap. 21 § 2nd para, or with a private road little used by the public. **high**.
- [TSFS] 1 kap. 18 §: a sign repeated after a junction stands at most **40 m** after the junction inside built-up areas, and at most **100 m** after it outside. **high**.
- Turning onto another road: the law has no rule that the limit ends when you turn. The limit you carry holds until a new C31. The repetition rules above make sure a C31 stands after the junction when the crossing road's limit differs. **medium** (interpretation of VMF 2 kap. 7–8 §§).

Repeaters between junctions ([TSFS] 4 kap. 28 §, **high**):

- At most every **10 km** on public roads where the limit is 110 or lower.
- At most every **30 km** on motorways and motortrafikleder.
- These are maximum gaps, not the typical spacing.

Placement of C31 ([TSFS] 4 kap. 25–26 §§ and 4 kap. 2 §, **high**):

- C31 stands on **both sides** where a limit begins. The left sign may be left out only for special reasons, and only where it does not matter for understanding.
- On one-lane on- and off-ramps of motorways, motortrafikleder and similar roads, the left sign is not needed.
- A repeated C31 more than 100 m before a junction may stand on the left only.
- Where the limit drops by more than 20 km/h at once, C31 must be given one of three ways:
  - as an **advance notice with a T2 distance plate**,
  - in size large, or
  - in size very large.

  On multi-lane roads the advance notice stands on both sides.
- C31 may hang **over the carriageway** (gantry). It then need not also stand at the roadside.

Combined with a built-up area sign:

- [VMF] 2 kap. 7 § 4th para: "Om ett förbudsmärke är uppsatt tillsammans med märke E5, tättbebyggt område, gäller anvisningen hela det tättbebyggda området, om inte annat anges för en viss väg eller vägsträcka. I sådana fall behöver förbudsmärket inte vara upprepat inom området." **high**.
- So **E5 + C31 (e.g. 40) is an area-wide limit** for the whole built-up area, on every street, without repeaters. It holds until another C31 on a given road, and ends where the area ends. That last point is **medium**: the text says "hela det tättbebyggda området".

Ramps and slip roads:

- A C31 on a ramp is an ordinary C31. By the letter of VMF 2 kap. 8 §, it holds until the next C31, C32, E7 or E9. **high** for the text.
- No provision was found that ends a ramp limit at the merge, or that makes the main carriageway's limit apply to traffic entering from a ramp. **Not found**.
- The only explicit interchange rule is that E1 is repeated after every on-ramp ([TSFS] 6 kap. 3 §). Whether C31 is in practice repeated after each on-ramp could not be verified (**low**).
- Länsstyrelser do issue separate local traffic regulations for ramp limits. Example: 01FS 2004:187 for on- and off-ramps at E4 trafikplats Hallunda, https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/download/18.76f16c3d1665eba4c3efb83/1540365731017/01FS%202004%20187%20L%C3%A4nsstyrelsens%20i%20Stockholms%20l%C3%A4n%20lokala%20trafikf%C3%B6reskrifter%20om%20hastighetsbegr%C3%A4nsning%20p%C3%A5%20avfartsv%C3%A4gar%20fr%C3%A5n,%20p%C3%A5fartsv%C3%A4gar%20till%20respektive%20parallellv%C3%A4g%20till%20E4%20i%20trafikplats%20Hallunda,%20Botkyrka%20kommun.pdf. Seen only as a search-result title, not read: **medium** that ramps get their own regulated limits.

Case law on how long a speed sign holds: **not found**. The web-search budget ran out before a targeted search of HD/hovrätt rulings could be made.

## Zones

- **No mandatory speed zone sign exists.** The area sign **E20 Områdesmärke** (yellow square, red border, an inserted C sign) and **E21 Slut på område** exist. But [TSFS] 6 kap. 26 § lists exhaustively the signs that may be inserted in E20 (C2, C3, C4, C6, C7, C8, C17, C18, C20, C21, C23, C24, C36–C39, C44, E19), and **C31 is not among them**. The official variants E20-1 … E20-16 on [TS-E] (`omradesmarke/`) contain no speed. **high** (as consolidated through TSFS 2024:4).
- **Area-wide mandatory limit = E5 + C31.** A C31 posted together with E5 holds for the whole built-up area, without repeaters, unless another limit is signed on a road ([VMF] 2 kap. 7 § 4th para). This is how "30-/40-zones" are signed in Sweden. **high** for the text, **medium** that this is the usual practice.
- **Recommended (not mandatory) area: E11 Rekommenderad lägre hastighet.**
  - Look: blue square in a white frame, white text "max NN km/tim".
  - Meaning: "Märket anger en vägsträcka eller ett område där särskilda åtgärder vidtagits ... Den rekommenderade lägre hastigheten anges på märket" ([VMF] 2 kap. 12 §).
  - It ends at E12, or where the end is obvious.
  - The value is in whole tens ([TSFS] 6 kap. 8 §). Without traffic-calming works it may only be posted where a warning sign has flagged a danger ([TSFS] 6 kap. 7 §).
  - **It is not a legal limit.** **high**.
- **Walking-speed areas (E9/E10), pedestrian streets (E7/E8), bicycle streets (E33/E34):** see the first section.
- *Miljözon* (E31/E32) is an environmental zone with no speed meaning ([TS-E]). **high**.

## Variable message signs

- **Legal basis for lit signs:** "Vägmärken och tilläggstavlor kan vara lysande. Text och symbol kan då vara lysande vit på mörk botten. Bård, snedstreck och annan röd del av vägmärket eller tilläggstavlan är dock alltid lysande röd." ([VMF] 1 kap. 4 §). A lit C31 is therefore **white digits on black with a red ring**. **high**.
- **Mandatory vs recommended (Trafikverket, [TRV-VAR], medium–high):**
  - "Vägmärke med hastighetsangivelse och en röd ring visar den föreskrivna högsta hastigheten. Det är ett förbudsmärke och har samma innebörd som ett traditionellt förbudsmärke i plåt."
  - "Vägmärke med hastighetsangivelse utan röd ring visar den rekommenderade högsta hastigheten." This is **E13 Rekommenderad högsta hastighet**: white number on a black square with a white frame. E13 is "endast ... omställbart vägmärke" and "Den angivna hastigheten är alltid lägre än den högsta tillåtna" ([VMF] 2 kap. 12 §). The value is in whole tens ([TSFS] 6 kap. 12 §). It may hang over the carriageway ([TSFS] 6 kap. 2 §), and it may be mounted on moving vehicles at roadworks ([TSFS] 1 kap. 16 §).
  - "För korsande trafik får endast rekommenderad hastighet användas": intersection warning systems show **E13, never a mandatory C31**.
- **Use:** risk of ice, queues, crossing traffic ([TRV-VAR]). Example: E4/E20 Botkyrka–Södertälje has had variable limits since 2021-03-08. Sensors lower the limit automatically in dense traffic. https://www.trafikverket.se/vara-projekt/projekt-i-stockholms-lan/e4e20-variabla-hastighetsgranser/ (seen in search results only: **medium**).
- **Gantries / per lane:** "Förbudsmärken och påbudsmärken som är uppsatta över körbanan gäller endast det körfält som de har placerats över." "Märke C31 ... gäller dock i förekommande fall även vägrenen intill det körfält märket placerats över." ([VMF] 2 kap. 3 §). So a C31 over a lane applies **to that lane only** (plus its shoulder): **lane-specific limits are legal**. **high**.
- **Blank or dark panel: not found.** Neither VMF, TSFS 2019:74 nor [TRV-VAR] says what a dark variable sign means. No provision makes a dark sign an end sign.
  - Interpretation: a dark panel has no legal content, and the last valid sign keeps holding. Under VMF 2 kap. 8 §, only a C31, C32, E7 or E9 ends a C31. **low**.

## Supplementary panels

Plates (*tilläggstavlor*) T1–T25 are in [VMF] 2 kap. (tilläggstavlor). Each plate adds to the sign above it, and a plate under stacked signs applies to all of them ([VMF] 2 kap. 2 §). **high**.

| **T1 Vägsträckas längd** | The sign applies over the length shown | high |
| **T6 Tidsangivelse** (time) | The sign applies only at the times shown. Black or white figures without brackets mean weekdays except the day before a Sunday or holiday. Figures **in brackets** mean the day before a Sunday or holiday. **Red** figures mean Sundays and holidays. A period over midnight runs into the next day. | high for the plate; a time-limited C31 in use was **not verified** |
| **T8 Symboltavla** | The sign applies only to the vehicle type or road user shown | high |
| **T5 Totalvikt** | Applies only to vehicles above the weight shown | high |
| **T11 Utsträckning** | Extent of the sign: arrows; a downward arrow means "angivelsen slutar" | high |
| **T12 Riktning** | Direction to what the sign refers to | high |
| **T22 Text** | Free text, e.g. "Gäller även lätt lastbil" | high |
| Wet road / rain plate | **Not found**: no such plate exists in the T1–T25 list | high (absence in the list) |

Other things a camera may see:

- **M29** is a painted speed number on the road surface: "Markeringen upplyser om högsta tillåtna hastighet" ([VMF] 4 kap. 9 §). It only informs; the sign is what counts. **high**.
- **E24 Automatisk trafikövervakning** is the speed-camera sign. It may be combined only with C31 ([TSFS] 6 kap. 33 §), so a C31 is often mounted on the same post. **high**.

## Special cases for a camera system

1. **Yellow C31.** The disc is **yellow** with a red ring and black digits, not white ([TS-C31] image, Wikipedia "Road signs in Sweden": "circular in shape with yellow backgrounds and red borders"). A detector trained on white-disc signs must accept yellow. **high**.
2. **No "end of limit" for permanent limits.** Only C32 (temporary) exists. C32 is a **yellow disc with a grey number and black stripes**; do not read its number as a new limit. **high**.
3. **Recommended signs are blue or black squares, not discs.**
   - E11 is a blue square reading "max NN km/tim"; E13 is a black square with a white number and no red ring.
   - Both are recommendations: they must **not** set the legal limit. They can at most be shown as advisory.
   - Their end signs: E12 is E11 with a red bar; E14 is a black square with white stripes. **high**.
4. **Lit C31 vs lit E13.** On gantries, the mandatory sign is white on black **with a red ring**; the recommended sign is white on black **without** a ring. The ring is the only difference. **high**.
5. **C31 + T2 plate = advance notice** (common before drops of more than 20 km/h). Apply it only after the distance. **high**.
6. **E5 is a white plate with a black skyline** (not the town-name sign of other countries). E5 + C31 means a whole-area limit. E5 alone means 50 by law, but in practice a C31 is almost always on the same post. **medium**.
7. **E1 repeated after every on-ramp**: it is not a new motorway start. **high**.
8. **Lane-specific gantry limits** are legal (VMF 2 kap. 3 §). A camera reading a gantry should take the sign over the ego lane. **high**.
9. **Values:**
   - C31 variants on [TS-C31] are 30–120.
   - C32 variants go down to 20 (C32-2), which suggests temporary 20 limits exist.
   - Wikipedia says private sites can sign below 30. **medium**.
10. **Walking pace (E7/E9)** has no km/h value in law. A value for the code has to be chosen: **not found**, see Open points.
11. **No mph, no seasonal sign swap, no wet-road plate.** **high** for mph (all limits are km/h, [TrF] 3 kap. 17 §); the other two are as above.

## For the comma

**IMPLICIT_LIMITS row**

```python
"SE": {"town": 50, "rural": 70, "expressway": 70, "motorway": 110},
```

- `town` 50: TrF 3 kap. 17 § 1st para. **high**.
- `rural` 70: the base speed, TrF 3 kap. 17 § 3rd para. **high**. Most rural roads are signed 80–100, but 70 is what applies with no sign.
- `expressway` 70: a motortrafikled has no limit of its own (TrF 9 kap. 2 § does not take over item 6), so the base speed applies. **high** for the law, **medium** as a value to use, because a motortrafikled is in practice always signed 80–110. A defensible alternative is `None`, so that E3 never invents a limit. Do **not** use 110: that is only the legal cap.
- `motorway` 110: TrF 9 kap. 1 § item 6. **high**. 120 needs a sign.

**Behaviour the code needs for SE (differs from the CH logic in vision.py):**

- **Road signs must not override a held C31.**
  - In SE a C31 holds until the next C31, C32, E7 or E9, and E1–E6 are not in that list ([VMF] 2 kap. 8 §).
  - Suggested rule: E1/E3/E5 set the general limit only when no C31 has been read since the last change of road kind, or when the C31 was read on the same post (same frame or within a few metres). In Sweden C31 and E1/E5 usually share the post. **medium**.
  - E2 and E6 restore 70 only if the limit held came from the road kind itself, or (for E6) from a C31 posted with E5. Otherwise keep the held C31 until the next C31. **medium**.
- **C32** (temporary end): restore the limit held before the temporary C31; else the map's limit; else the general limit of the road kind. Needs a new sign class, and must not be read as a number. **medium**.
- **E11/E13 recommended:** separate classes, never written to `speed_limit`. E12/E14 end only the recommendation. **high**.
- **E7/E9** (walking pace) end the held C31. **E33** sets 30 by law. **high**.
- **T2 plate under C31:** delay or ignore. **high**.

**The intersection rule: NO.** A C31 does **not** end at the next intersection ([VMF] 2 kap. 8 §, C31 entry, an explicit exception to the general next-junction rule of 2 kap. 7 § for prohibition signs). **high**.

- Signs are repeated after junctions only when needed. They need not be repeated if the crossing road has the same limit (VMF 2 kap. 8 §), or if the next different-limit sign is within 200 m (TSFS 4 kap. 27 §), or after exits and access roads (TSFS 4 kap. 29 §).
- vision.py's `MAP_SECONDS` map fallback (written for CH OSStr art. 16) is **not** the Swedish legal rule. As a heuristic it is still reasonable after a turn: Swedish practice requires a new C31 after the junction when the crossing road's limit differs (VMF 2 kap. 7 § 2nd para). **medium**.

**A blank variable sign:** no legal meaning found. Treat it as **no sign**: keep the held limit. It is not an end sign. **low** (not found in law).

**Other rules the code should know:**

- **Repeater gaps.** A missing repeater does not end a limit, so the gaps cannot be used as an expiry time. TSFS 4 kap. 28 § allows repeaters up to 10 km apart (roads ≤ 110) and 30 km apart (motorways and motortrafikleder). **high**.
- **Ramps.** The VMF text does not end a ramp C31 at the merge. Ramps can be part of the motorway or motortrafikled (TSFS size tables), and E1 is repeated after on-ramps (TSFS 6 kap. 3 §).
  - vision.py's ramp logic (the ramp limit ends at the merge, then the general limit comes back) gives 110 on a motorway or 70 on a motortrafikled. It is a heuristic with no Swedish text behind it. **low**.
  - Prefer the map's limit at the merge over the SE general limits.
- **Built-up area areas.** E5 + C31 applies to every street of the area, without repeaters (VMF 2 kap. 7 § 4th para). Inside such an area, after a turn, keep the area value, not the map's 50 default. **medium**.
- **Vehicle limits.** A car with a trailer is limited to 80 regardless of signs (TrF 4 kap. 20 §). **high**.

2. **Legal construction of mandatory variable limits:** the TRVFS föreskrift wording ("hastighet som anges med omställbara vägmärken"?) was **not found**.
3. **Ramps:** no text on whether a ramp C31 ends at the merge, or whether a C31 is repeated after each on-ramp. Check a Trafikverket guideline: VGU, or "Vägmärken – handbok"/"Utmärkning av hastighetsbegränsning", not read.
4. **E1/E5 without a C31 on the same post while a different C31 is held:** the text conflict between VMF 2 kap. 8 § (C31 holds) and TrF 9 kap. 1 § item 6 / 3 kap. 17 § (110 / 50 by road kind) is unresolved. No court ruling found.
5. **E6 alone after a C31 not posted with E5:** does the C31 continue outside the area? The letter of VMF 2 kap. 8 § says yes; practice says there is always a C31. **Not found**.
6. **Case law** (HD/hovrätt) on sign validity and duration: **not found**. The search budget ran out.
7. **Seasonal limits:** none in law. Whether any road uses T6/T22 plates or swapped signs for a winter period was **not found** (only temporary all-year cuts such as E14 2021).
8. **Walking pace (gångfart)** has no km/h value: the code needs a choice (e.g. 10 km/h as a display value, or treat as "no number").
9. **TSFS amendments after 2024:4** (2025–2026) were not checked. The consolidated PDF is "t.o.m. TSFS 2024:4", with one 2022:81 note.
10. **Time-limited C31 (T6 plate, e.g. school zones)**: the plate is legal, but whether it is used with C31 on public roads was not verified.
